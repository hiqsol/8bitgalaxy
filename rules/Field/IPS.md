1. **IPS**, Inter Planetary [[Space]], is the inner part of a [[Star]]
   system
2. **IPS** has two vertical rows of [[Slot]]s:
	1. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Unit]]s
	2. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Structure]]s
3. Each [[Star]] has its own split between the rows, e.g. a [[Star]]
   of 8 **Slots** can be 3+5, 4+4 or 5+3 [[Unit]]+[[Structure]]
   [[Slot]]s
4. The [[World slot]] of the [[Star]] is in its **IPS** too: its
   [[Card]] is a [[Direct participant]] like the [[Card]]s in the
   rows, see [[PerformAction]]

#### Notes

1. [[Slot]]s are [[Pocket]]s of the [[Star]] [[Tile]], in the physical
   and the virtual game alike

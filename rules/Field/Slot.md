1. **Slot** aka **pocket** is a **Warp orbit**, a place for one
   [[Card]] in a [[Star]]
2. [[Card]] in a **slot** shows only the [[End]] that is up, the other
   [[End]] is inside the **Pocket**
3. **Slots** are shared by all players
4. [[Unit]] and [[Structure]] **Slots** are separate rows, [[Unit]]
   **Slots** are thinner - for vertically oriented [[Card]]s
5. **Slots** in a row are numbered 1-5 by height, so [[Card]]s
   overlap:
	1. A [[Card]] is **overlapped** when the next lower **slot** of
	   its row holds a [[Card]]
	2. Overlapped [[Card]]'s [[SecondaryAbility]] is not available
6. A [[Card]] that comes into a [[Star]] takes a free **slot** of its
   row, its player picks which: without one it can't come

#### Notes

1. A **Warp orbit** is not a gravitational orbit, a [[Star]] has
   countless of those. Warp tears space-time, so a [[Star]] holds only
   a few **Warp orbits**: that's why the number of **Slots** is
   limited
2. Overlap is a new mechanic, not seen in other board games: [[Card]]s
   naturally get one under another

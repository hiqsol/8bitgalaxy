1. **Star** is a [[Field]] [[Tile]], see [[Component]]
2. **Star** represents star system with planets
3. **Star** has 7 to 10 [[Slot]]s, the main property, in two
   vertical rows: up to 5 for [[Unit]]s and up to 5 for
   [[Structure]]s; each **Star** has its own split, e.g. 8 [[Slot]]s
   as 3+5, 4+4 or 5+3 [[Unit]]+[[Structure]] [[Slot]]s
4. Below its rows a **Star** has the **World slot**, shaped as a
   [[Structure]] [[Slot]], for its [[World]]: its [[Card]] is a
   [[Direct participant]] like the [[Card]]s in the rows, see
   [[PerformAction]]
5. The [[Card]] in the **World slot** gives the **Star** its [[Class]]
   and [[Ability|Abilities]], a **Star** with an empty one is [[Wild]]
6. A **Star** has [[Level]] 1, a [[Scenario]] may set another: a
   route passing the **Star** counts it, see [[Distance]]

#### Notes

1. [[Slot]]s are [[Pocket]]s of the **Star** [[Tile]], in the
   physical and the virtual game alike
2. **Star** [[Tile]]s come in 4 sizes only, a **Star**'s variety comes
   from its [[World]]

1. **Star** is a [[Field]] [[Tile]], see [[Component]]
2. **Star** represents star system with planets
3. **Star** contains its [[IPS]]: 7 to 10 [[Slot]]s, the main
   property, split between the [[Unit]] and [[Structure]] rows
4. Below its rows a **Star** has the **World slot**, shaped as a
   [[Structure]] [[Slot]], for its [[World]]
5. The [[Card]] in the **World slot** gives the **Star** its [[Class]]
   and [[Ability|Abilities]], a **Star** with an empty one is [[Wild]]
6. A **Star** has [[Level]] 1, a [[Scenario]] may set another: a
   route passing the **Star** counts it, see [[Distance]]

#### Notes

1. **Star** [[Tile]]s come in 4 sizes only, a **Star**'s variety comes
   from its [[World]]

1. **Supernova** is a [[Space]] type
2. A route can't pass **Supernova**, see [[Distance]]
3. **Supernova** can not be explored. It can only appear as result of
   **Supernova event**
4. An [[Action]] started in a [[Star]] bordering a **Supernova** gets
   +1 [[Power]] of any [[Class]]

#### Notes

1. A dying star: nothing crosses it, and those working beside it draw
   on its light

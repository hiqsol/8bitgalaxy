1. **Singularity** is the [[Science]] path of [[Goal]]
2. A [[Faction]] has **Singularity** when for every [[Class]] it has
   an [[ActiveFor|Active]] [[Card]] that provides that [[Class]] at
   the highest [[Level]] of that [[Class]] in its [[Faction]] deck

#### Notes

1. The highest [[Level]] is taken from the [[Faction]] deck, so the
   path fits any deck
2. [[Research]] leads to **Singularity**
3. Unused [[Card]]s in [[Hand]] count, they are active: the check
   comes before they go to [[Discard]], see [[Turn]]
4. A [[Card]] that [[Deplete]]s in the [[Turn]] doesn't count at
   **Check**: a [[Structure]] shows its [[Weak]] [[End]], a lower
   [[Level]], an [[Actor]] is in [[Discard]]. The highest [[Card]]s
   are held, not used

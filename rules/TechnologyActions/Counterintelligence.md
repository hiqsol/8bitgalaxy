1. **Counterintelligence** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Counterintelligence** is an [[ImmediateAction]]
3. It is used when a rival shows a [[Card]] for an
   [[ImmediateAction]], also at the rival's [[Turn]]
4. **Outcome**: the rival's [[ImmediateAction|Immediate action]] has
   no effect, the rival's [[Card]] [[Deplete]]s anyway

#### Notes

1. Like Sabotage of Twilight Imperium: an answer to
   [[ForceDiscard]], [[Retreat]], [[Stealth]] and [[Forecast]]
2. A **Counterintelligence** can be cancelled by another one

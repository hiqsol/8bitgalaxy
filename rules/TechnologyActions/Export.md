1. **Export** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Export** [[Action]]:
	1. Converts [[Goods]] to [[Energy]]
	2. Performed as a [[Move]] of [[Goods]] between two [[Faction]]s
3. **Direction**:
	1. From [[Star]] having [[Colony]] of one [[Faction]]
	2. To [[Star]] having [[Colony]] of other [[Faction]]
4. **Requirements**: consent of the party [[Faction]]s' players
5. **Outcome**:
	1. Party [[Faction]] gets 1 item of [[Goods]]
	2. Other items of carried [[Goods]] convert to [[Energy]]

#### Notes

1. Performer can be from different [[Faction]] than the
   [[Colony|Colonies]]
2. In a duel the other party is the rival: **Export** needs its
   consent, a [[Smuggle]] doesn't

1. **Stealth** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Stealth** is an [[ImmediateAction]]
3. **Outcome**: the shown card makes another own card on the
   [[Field]] [[Hidden]]
4. At [[Takeoff]] a card can be placed with its [[Hidden]] [[End]] up
   when **Stealth** is provided by the card itself or by an own
   [[Enabled]] card in that [[Star]]
5. A card providing **Stealth** at Takeoff [[Deplete]]s, as for any
   [[ImmediateAction]]

#### Notes

1. **Stealth** is the only way to hide a card on the [[Field]], at
   [[Takeoff]] or later: it makes hidden moves possible, [[Explore]]
   counters it
2. A card from [[Hand]] joins a [[Combat]] face up: a bluff in a
   Combat needs Hidden cards on the Field, so it needs **Stealth**

1. **Stealth** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Stealth** is an [[ImmediateAction]]
3. **Outcome**: the shown [[Card]] makes another own [[Card]] on the
   [[Field]] [[Hidden]]
4. At [[Takeoff]] a [[Card]] can be placed with its [[Hidden]] [[End]]
   up when **Stealth** is provided by the [[Card]] itself or by an own
   face-up [[Card]] in that [[Star]]
5. A [[Card]] providing **Stealth** at [[Takeoff]] [[Deplete]]s, as
   for any [[ImmediateAction]]

#### Notes

1. **Stealth** is the only way to hide a [[Card]] on the [[Field]], at
   [[Takeoff]] or later: it makes [[Hidden]] moves possible,
   [[Explore]] counters it
2. A [[Card]] from [[Hand]] joins a [[Combat]] face up: a bluff in a
   [[Combat]] needs [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s on the [[Field]], so it needs
   **Stealth**

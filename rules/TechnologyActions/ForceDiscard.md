1. **Force Discard** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Force Discard** is an [[ImmediateAction]]
3. **Outcome**: a rival moves one [[Card]] of the rival's choice from
   [[Hand]] to [[Discard]]

#### Notes

1. [[Hand]] is used for defense at rivals' [[Turn]]s, see [[Combat]]:
   discarding weakens both the rival's defense and the rival's next
   [[Turn]]
2. Like Star Realms: the rival's [[Hand]] is drawn at the end of
   its [[Turn]], so it waits to be hit
3. An empty [[Hand]] at the start of own [[Turn]] leaves only a
   [[Field]] [[Starter]] to make a move, see [[Goal]]: **Force
   Discard** makes it a real risk

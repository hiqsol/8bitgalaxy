1. **Defection** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Defection** replaces a rival [[Damaged]] [[Hero]] with the
   player's own copy of it
3. **Direction**: [[Source]] [[Star]] to the [[Star]] of the rival
   [[Hero]]
4. **Requirements**:
	1. The target [[Star]] is [[ControlledBy]] the player
	2. The rival [[Hero]] is [[Damaged]], it is the target, not a
	   participant
	3. The player's own copy of that [[Hero]] is anywhere in the
	   player's [[Factory]] or [[Laboratory]] piles, unlike the top
	   [[Card]] rule of the piles
	4. The player is below its [[Hero limit]], see [[Hero#5]]
5. **Cost**: the rival [[Hero]]'s current [[Level]]
6. **Outcome**:
	1. The rival [[Hero]] is [[Destroy]]ed: it goes to its
	   [[Faction]]'s [[Factory]]
	2. The own copy goes to the player's [[Discard]], the other
	   [[Card]]s of its pile keep their order

#### Notes

1. A beaten leader changes sides, like a [[Colony]] taken by
   [[Assimilate]]: the rival's [[Card]] goes home, the player's own
   copy joins, no [[Card]] of another color enters a deck
2. The rival must build the [[Hero]] again: in its [[Factory]] it
   counts as no leader for [[PaxGalactica|Pax Galactica]]

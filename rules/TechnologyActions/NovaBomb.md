1. **Nova Bomb** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Nova Bomb** [[Destroy]]s every [[Card]] in the target [[Star]]:
   own, rival and [[Galaxy]] [[Card]]s, the [[World]] included
3. **Direction**: [[Source]] [[Star]] to another [[Star]]
4. **Cost**: the total current [[Level]] of the [[Card]]s in the
   target [[Star]], [[Hidden]] ones included: the player learns it
   after the start, as for [[Explore]]
5. **Outcome**: the [[Card]]s are Destroyed, the [[Card]] that
   provides **Nova Bomb** leaves the game

#### Notes

1. A doomsday weapon of the [[Ancient]]s, recruited from a
   [[Derelict]]: like the [[Supernova]] [[Event]], a [[Star]] burns
   for one shot
2. Not a [[Combat]]: [[Card]]s from [[Hand]] can't defend, only the
   [[Cost]] stops it

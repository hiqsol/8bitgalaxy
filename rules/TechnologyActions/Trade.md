1. **Trade** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Trade** is a joint action of two [[Faction]]s: both players add
   participants to acquire [[Goods]] together
3. **Direction**: from a [[Star]] with a [[Colony]] of one party to a
   Star with a Colony of the other
4. **Requirements**: consent of the other party's player
5. The other party's participants join at the starter's [[Turn]],
   cards from its [[Hand]] included
6. **Outcome**:
	1. Power of both parties converts to [[Goods]] one to one
	2. The players split the [[Goods]] between their Stars of the
	   Direction as they agree

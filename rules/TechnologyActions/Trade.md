1. **Trade** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Trade** is a joint [[Action]] of two [[Faction]]s: both players
   add [[Participants]] to acquire [[Goods]] together
3. **Direction**: from a [[Star]] with a [[Colony]] of one party to a
   [[Star]] with a [[Colony]] of the other
4. **Requirements**: consent of the other party's player
5. The other party's [[Participants]] join at the [[Starter]]'s
   [[Turn]], [[Card]]s from its [[Hand]] included
6. **Outcome**:
	1. [[Power]] of both parties converts to [[Goods]] one to one
	2. The players split the [[Goods]] between their [[Star]]s of the
	   [[Direction]] as they agree

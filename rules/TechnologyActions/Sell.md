1. **Sell** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Sell** is a [[Trade]] over a [[Card]]: it converts a selected
   [[Card]] [[ControlledBy]] the player in the [[Source]] [[Star]] to
   [[Goods]], the [[Card]] is a target, not a participant
3. **Direction**: from a [[Star]] with a [[Colony]] of one party to a
   [[Star]] with a [[Colony]] of the other
4. **Requirements**: consent of the other party's player
5. **Outcome**:
	1. The [[Price]] of the [[Card]] converts to [[Goods]] one to one
	2. The players split the [[Goods]] between their [[Star]]s of the
	   [[Direction]] as they agree
	3. The [[Card]] is [[Destroy]]ed

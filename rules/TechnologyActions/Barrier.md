1. **Barrier** is [[Science]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Barrier** turns a revealed [[Space]] face down: it is [[Hidden]]
   again, [[Card]]s can't pass it until it is revealed
3. **Direction**: [[Source]] [[Star]] to the [[Space]]
4. **Requirements**: the [[Space]] borders the [[Source]] [[Star]] and
   is not a [[Supernova]]
5. **Cost**: the [[Price]] of the [[Space]]

#### Notes

1. A wall of warp, a [[WarpStorm|Warp storm]] raised by the player
2. [[Explore]] opens it again for the same [[Price]]
3. A [[Supernova]] can't be explored, so it stays revealed

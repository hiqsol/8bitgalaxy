1. **Explore** is [[Military]] and [[Science]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. Flavour names:
	1. **Recon** for [[Military]]
	2. **Survey** for [[Science]]
3. **Explore** reveals one target:
	1. [[Hidden]] [[Space]]
	2. [[Hidden]] [[Card]]
4. **Direction**: [[Star]] to the target
5. **Requirements**:
	1. [[Hidden]] [[Space]] borders the [[Source]] [[Star]] or a
	   revealed [[Tile]] reachable within [[Range]]
	2. [[Hidden]] [[Card]] is in the [[Source]] [[Star]] or in a
	   [[Star]] reachable within [[Range]]
6. **Cost**: the target's [[Price]]
7. The [[Cost]] is not shown: the player [[Declare]]s the [[Action]],
   then learns the [[Cost]], and [[Commit]]s more [[Card]]s or stops,
   see [[PerformAction]]
8. For a [[Hidden]] [[Card]] the acting player chooses to reveal it to
   all or keep it [[Hidden]]
9. **Recon** directed to an [[Academy]] [[ControlledBy]] a rival
   looks at that rival's [[Hand]]:
	1. The [[Academy]] [[Card]] is the target: in reach as a
	   [[Hidden]] [[Card]], the [[Cost]] is its [[Price]]
10. In a [[Combat]] a player can reveal a rival's [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s
    with **Explore**

#### Notes

1. Both military and scientific [[Faction]]s can reveal, like
   [[Create]] serves both [[Culture]] and [[Production]]
2. Better [[Tile]]s cost more, so running out of [[Power]] hints at a
   good one
3. Learning the [[Cost]] and stopping at once is a cheap look, the
   [[Action]] fails and costs only its [[Starter]], that's fine
4. The [[Academy]] is where a [[Faction]]'s [[Leader]]s come from, so
   **Recon** of it opens the way to the [[Faction]]'s deck, see also
   [[Academy]]

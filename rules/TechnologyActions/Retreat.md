1. **Retreat** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Retreat** is an [[ImmediateAction]] in a [[Combat]] the player
   takes part in
3. **Outcome**: escape from the [[Combat]]:
	1. The [[Combat]] finishes, the retreating side loses it as by a
	   **Rout**, see [[Combat#10]]: the winner Damages none of own
	   [[Participants]]
	2. Own [[Participants]] are neither [[Damaged]] nor Destroyed,
	   they only [[Deplete]]
	3. A retreating [[Defender]]'s face-up [[Unit]]s in the
	   target [[Star]] go to its [[Discard]]

#### Notes

1. Useful to save your forces when you are losing the [[Combat]]
2. A retreat costs the [[Combat]] but saves the [[Card]]s: a side
   that leads gains nothing by it, a beaten one escapes before the
   reveal, its [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s stay [[Hidden]]
3. A retreating [[Defender]] gives up the [[Star]]: the [[Attacker]]
   moves in, see [[Combat#9.3]], and captures the [[Structure]]s left
   there, see [[ControlledBy]]

1. **Recover** is [[Culture]] and [[Production]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. Flavour names:
	1. **Heal** for [[Social]]
	2. **Fix** for [[Industrial]]
3. **Recover** [[Flip]]s a [[Damaged]] [[Card]] to [[Enabled]], its
   [[Weak]] [[End]] up, or its [[Strong]] [[End]] up for 1 [[Power]]
   more
4. **Direction**: from the [[Card]] that provides **Recover** to the
   target [[Card]]
5. **Cost**: half the [[Price]] of the target [[Card]], rounded up,
   +1 for the [[Strong]] [[End]]

#### Notes

1. **Recover** answers the check of [[PaxGalactica|Pax Galactica]]: a
   player whose last [[Hero]] got [[Damaged]] has one [[Turn]] to
   **Recover** it
2. **Recover** takes any [[Damaged]] [[Card]], a rival's too: a
   **Heal** of a captured [[Hero]] of a [[Fallen]] [[Faction]] brings
   that [[Faction]] back
3. Half the [[Price]]: a repair costs about half of a new [[Card]],
   a [[Damaged]] [[Card]] is worth fighting over

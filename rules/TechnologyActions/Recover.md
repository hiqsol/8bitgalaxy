1. **Recover** is [[Culture]] and [[Production]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Recover** [[Flip]]s a [[Damaged]] [[Card]] face up, its
   [[Weak]] [[End]] up, or its [[Strong]] [[End]] up for 1 [[Power]]
   more
3. **Direction**: from the [[Card]] that provides **Recover** to the
   target [[Card]]
4. **Cost**: half the [[Price]] of the target [[Card]], rounded up,
   +1 for the [[Strong]] [[End]]

#### Notes

1. **Recover** answers the check of [[PaxGalactica|Pax Galactica]],
   see [[PaxGalactica#Notes#2]]
2. **Recover** takes any [[Damaged]] [[Card]], a rival's too: a
   **Recover** of a captured [[Hero]] of a [[Fallen]] [[Faction]]
   brings that [[Faction]] back
3. Half the [[Price]]: a repair costs about half of a new [[Card]],
   a [[Damaged]] [[Card]] is worth fighting over

1. **Recover** is [[Culture]] and [[Production]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. Flavour names:
	1. **Heal** for [[Social]]
	2. **Fix** for [[Industrial]]
3. **Recover** flips a [[Damaged]] card to [[Enabled]]
4. **Direction**: from the card that provides **Recover** to the
   target card
5. **Cost**: current [[Level]] of the target card

#### Notes

1. **Recover** answers the check of [[Goal]]: a player whose last
   [[Hero]] got [[Damaged]] has one turn to recover it

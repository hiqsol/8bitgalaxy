1. **Plus Pile** is [[Production]] and [[Science]]
   [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Plus Pile** turns its own [[Card]] into an additional pile: the
   [[Card]] leaves the [[Field]] and goes to [[Home]] [[Back]] up as
   the bottom of a new [[Factory]] or [[Laboratory]] pile, the
   player's choice
3. The new pile works as the other piles of its row: [[Card]]s go on
   top of the **Plus Pile** [[Card]], which is never available itself
4. The **Plus Pile** [[Card]] stays there for the rest of the game

#### Notes

1. The [[Card]] is the marker of the pile: an empty extra pile still
   shows on the table
2. One [[Card]] makes one pile, so the number of extra piles is the
   number of [[Card]]s with **Plus Pile**

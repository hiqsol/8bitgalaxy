1. **Factory** is a row of 4 piles in [[Home]]
2. **Factory** piles hold [[Card]]s that are ready to [[Create]]
3. Only the top [[Card]] of each pile is available

#### Notes

1. **Factory** is the pool of [[Card]]s to (re)build, that's why
   [[Destroy]]ed [[Card]]s go there
2. Managing own piles is a subgame, as in the [[Laboratory]]: the
   order of [[Card]]s sets what can be built next

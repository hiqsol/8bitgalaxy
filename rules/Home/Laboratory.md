1. **Laboratory** is a row of 5 piles in [[Home]]
2. **Laboratory** piles hold [[Card]]s that must be [[Research]]ed
   before building
3. Only the top [[Card]] of each pile is available
4. Moving a [[Card]] between **Laboratory** piles costs 1 [[Power]]
   per [[Card]], whatever [[Action]] moves it

#### Notes

1. Managing own piles is a subgame: the order of [[Card]]s sets the
   pace of the tech tree, [[Research]] and [[Manage]] [[Shift]] change
   it

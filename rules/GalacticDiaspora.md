1. **Galactic Diaspora** is the [[Culture]] achievement of [[Goal]]
2. A faction has **Galactic Diaspora** while every [[Star]] on the
   [[Field]] contains a non-[[Damaged]] [[Actor]] or [[Structure]]
   card of that [[Faction]]
3. The faction need not [[Control]] the card or its Star
4. A [[Hidden]] card counts without being revealed

#### Notes

1. The faction lives throughout the universe without having to rule
   it

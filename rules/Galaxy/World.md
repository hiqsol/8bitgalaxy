1. A **World** is a [[Colony]] [[Card]] of the [[Galaxy]] [[Faction]]
2. **World** represents the planets of a [[Star]]
3. **World** prints one [[Ability]]
4. **World** lies in the [[World slot]] of its [[Star]], not in a
   [[Structure]] [[Slot]], and never leaves it
5. **World** gives no [[Control]]
6. **Worlds** form the **World deck**, face down beside the [[Field]];
   a conquest draws from it, see [[Wild]]
7. A [[Destroy]]ed **World** goes under the [[WorldDeck|World deck]]

#### Notes

1. In a [[Disputed]] [[Star]] the **World** stays the [[Galaxy]]'s: no
   player [[Faction]] uses it and it counts for nobody
2. A **World** does not count toward a player [[Faction]]'s
   [[GalacticDiaspora|Galactic Diaspora]]

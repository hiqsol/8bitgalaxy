1. During **Galaxy move** player takes the top [[Card]] of the
   [[GalaxyDeck]] and plays it by its kind:
	1. [[Event]]: to [[FutureEvents]]
	2. [[Ancient]]: [[Ancient]]s breed and spread, [[Hidden]] to a
	   [[Star]] in one of the ways:
		1. **Develop**: any [[Card]] to a [[Star]] that already has
		   active [[Ancient]]s
		2. **Expand**: a [[Unit]] [[Card]] to a [[Star]] adjacent to a
		   [[Star]] with active [[Ancient]]s
	3. [[Derelict]]: to a [[Star]]
	4. Other [[Card]]s: as the [[Card]] says
2. **Galaxy move** is mandatory every [[Turn]] when the [[Galaxy]] is
   present
3. When the [[GalaxyDeck]] runs out, [[Twilight]] is triggered; at its
   end, the player [[Faction]] with the most [[VictoryPoints]] wins

#### Notes

1. Not only enemies: the [[Galaxy]] generates content, a threat, an
   opportunity or a puzzle

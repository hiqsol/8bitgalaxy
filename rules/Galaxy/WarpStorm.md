1. **Warp storm** is a reddish marker tile of the [[Galaxy]]
2. **Warp storm** lies on two neighbouring [[Space]] [[Tile]]s
3. A route can't pass a [[Space]] under a **Warp storm**, see
   [[Distance]]
4. An [[Event]] moves a **Warp storm**

#### Notes

1. A nod to the warp storms of Forbidden Stars

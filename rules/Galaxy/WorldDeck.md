1. **World Deck** lists the [[World]] cards of the [[Duel]]: 20
   cards, counted by the [[Level]] of the strong End as in the
   [[Deck]]:
	1. Level 2: 8 cards, 2 of each [[Class]]
	2. Level 4: 8 cards, 2 of each Class
	3. Level 6: 4 cards, 1 of each Class
2. A World is a [[Colony]], made like the Deck's Structures:
	1. Both [[End]]s of one Class, the weak End one Level lower
	2. [[ExtraPrice]] 1 on both Ends, so the [[Price]] is the strong
	   Level + 1
	3. [[Prerequisites]]: none at Level 2, else the strong Level − 2
	   of its Class
3. A World prints one [[Ability]], the Primary of its strong End, on
   the weak End too where the table says both
4. A weak Ability comes in many copies, a strong one is rare:

#### Notes

1. A better World is rarer, costs more and so more [[Ancient]]s guard
   it: 3, 5 or 7
2. A conquest draws until the participants meet a World's
   Prerequisites, see [[Wild]]: early conquests find the weak Worlds,
   the strong ones wait for developed factions
3. Energy is above power: an Energy token Boosts an action of any
   Class anywhere and stays between Turns
4. +1 Draw is the Draw [[Plus]], see [[DeckBuilding]]
5. No World reaches Level 8, the Level of the crowns: a World never
   gives a faction [[Singularity]]

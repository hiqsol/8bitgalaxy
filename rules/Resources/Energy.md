1. **Energy** is stockable [[Resource]]
2. **Energy** is a [[Token]] in [[Hand]], the same [[Token]] in a
   [[Star]] is [[Goods]], see [[Component]]
3. Obtained from an **Energy** [[Plus]], e.g. `+1e`, at once when its
   [[Card]] joins an [[Action]]: it can [[Boost]] that [[Action]] or
   stay in [[Hand]]
4. Spent globally from [[Hand]]: any participant can [[Boost]] any
   [[Action]] with it
5. [[Token]]s are not [[Card]]s: they don't count to the [[Hand]] size
   and stay in [[Hand]] at **Draw** phase of [[Turn]]
6. [[Token]]s in [[Hand]] lie open beside the [[Card]]s: every player
   sees them
7. [[Hand]] holds at most as many [[Token]]s as the [[Hand]] size:
   [[Token]]s above it are lost

#### Notes

1. Warp gives huge amounts of energy, though not limitless, see
   [[Lore]]

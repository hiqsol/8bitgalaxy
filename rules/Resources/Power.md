1. **Resource** pays [[Action]] [[Requirements]], see
   [[PerformAction]], in two flavours:
	1. Non-stockable: **Power**
	2. Stockable: [[Goods]], [[Energy]]
2. **Power** satisfies [[Action]] [[Cost]] [[Requirements]]
3. Each [[Class]] has its **Power**: **Military power**, **Culture
   power**, **Production power** and **Science power**, see [[Class]]

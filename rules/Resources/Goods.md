1. **Goods** is stockable [[Resource]]
2. **Goods** is a [[Token]] in a [[Star]], the same [[Token]] in
   [[Hand]] is [[Energy]], see [[Component]]
3. Carried as [[Cargo]] of a [[Move]] by any [[Ship]], a [[Token]]
   weighs 1
4. Can be used to [[Boost]] [[Action]]
5. Spent only from its [[Star]]: added to an [[Action]] starting or
   finishing there
6. **Star Capacity** is the sum of the [[Capacity|Capacities]] of the
   [[Structure]]s in the [[Star]]:
	1. A [[Star]] can't hold more **goods** than its [[Capacity]]
	2. When the **Star Capacity** drops below its **goods**, the
	   excess is lost
7. At [[Takeoff]] an [[Actor]] can carry [[Energy]] [[Token]]s from
   [[Hand]] as [[Cargo]], within its [[Capacity]]: in the [[Star]]
   they become **goods**, up to the **Star Capacity**, and take no
   [[Slot]]

#### Notes

1. Carrying [[Energy]] to a [[Star]] at [[Takeoff]] stocks it beyond
   the [[Hand]] limit, see [[Energy]]

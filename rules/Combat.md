1. **Combat** is a fight in a [[Star]], an **Attacker** against a
   **Defender**
2. First **attacker**:
	1. selects **attacking participants** and [[Supporter]]s
	2. selects **defending participants**: [[Direct participant]]s
	   from the **Defender**'s [[Card]]s in the target [[Star]]
3. Then **Combat Rounds** follow, in each **Combat Round** every party
   adds any number of own [[Participants]] and [[Supporter]]s, or
   passes, in [[Turn]] order starting with the **defender**: with two
   players the **Defender**, then the **attacker**
4. More players can join the **combat** on any side:
	1. They must have active [[Military]] [[Actor]]s in the [[Star]]
	2. Their [[Card]]s follow the [[Range]] [[Requirements]], see
	   [[PerformAction]]
	3. They keep their [[Turn]] order in the **Combat Rounds**: e.g.
	   with the [[Turn]] order A, C, B, when A attacks B, C goes after
	   A and before B
5. [[Card]]s from [[Hand]] join as [[Supporter]]s, also at rival's
   [[Turn]]
6. Parties can [[Boost]] with [[Card]]s at the moment when they add
   the [[Card]], not later
7. **Combat** resolves when every party passes in one **Combat Round**
8. Then reveal [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s, count total [[Power]]s and
   determine winner:
	1. An attacking [[Card]] adds its [[MilitaryPower]]
	2. A defending [[Card]] adds its [[Defense]]
	3. A tie goes to the **Defender**
	4. **Advantage** = winner total − loser total
9. After [[Action]]:
	1. Winner can save from damage own [[Direct participant]]s with
	   total sum of [[Level]]s **not more** than the **Advantage**
	2. All other [[Direct participant]]s become [[Damaged]].
	3. If the **Attacker** wins, the **Attacker** may move surviving
	   attacking [[Unit]]s into the [[Destination]] [[Star]], the
	   others stay in the [[Source]]
	4. Then [[Deplete]], but not the [[Direct participant]]s

#### Notes

1. No dice: multiple **Combat Rounds**, gradual commitments and
   [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s bring decisions, risks, surprises and bluff,
   the result isn't predetermined
2. [[Direct participant]]s hold ground like chess pieces: survivors
   stay or move in
3. Both sides can add any [[Card]]s, but a non-[[Military]] [[Card]]
   has no [[MilitaryPower]]: it attacks with 0, though it can
   [[Boost]], and defends with 1, see [[Defense]]. It joins an
   [[Attack]] for its [[Technology|Technologies]], [[Range]] and
   [[Capacity]], or to move in

1. **Combat** is a fight in a [[Star]], an **Attacker** against a
   **Defender**
2. First **attacker**:
	1. selects **attacking participants** and [[Supporter]]s
	2. selects **defending participants**: [[Direct participant]]s
	   from the **Defender**'s [[Card]]s in the target [[Star]]
3. Then **Combat Rounds** follow, in each **Combat Round** every party
   adds any number of own [[Participants]], [[Source]] [[Card]]s too,
   and [[Supporter]]s, or passes, in [[Turn]] order starting with the
   **defender**: with two players the **Defender**, then the
   **attacker**
4. More players can join the **combat** on any side:
	1. They must have active [[Military]] [[Actor]]s in the [[Star]]
	2. Their [[Card]]s follow the [[Range]] [[Requirements]], see
	   [[PerformAction]]
	3. They keep their [[Turn]] order in the **Combat Rounds**: e.g.
	   with the [[Turn]] order A, C, B, when A attacks B, C goes after
	   A and before B
5. [[Card]]s from [[Hand]] join as [[Supporter]]s, also at rival's
   [[Turn]]
6. Parties can [[Boost]] with [[Card]]s at the moment when they add
   the [[Card]], not later:
	1. **Boost** adds to the **Combat power** of either side
	2. A [[Card]] boosts not more than its own **Combat power**,
	   instead of its [[Capacity]]
7. **Combat** resolves when every party passes in one **Combat Round**
8. Then reveal [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s, count each side's **Combat
   power** and determine winner:
	1. An attacking [[Card]] adds its [[Military power]]
	2. A defending [[Card]] adds its [[Defense]]
	3. A tie goes to the **Defender**
	4. **Advantage** = winner's **Combat power** − loser's **Combat
	   power**
9. After [[Action]]:
	1. The loser's [[Participants]], [[Supporter]]s too, become
	   [[Damaged]]
	2. The winner Damages own [[Participants]] of total **Combat
	   power** at least the loser's **Combat power** − **Advantage**,
	   of its choice
	3. If the **Attacker** wins, the **Attacker** may move surviving
	   attacking [[Unit]]s into the [[Destination]] [[Star]], the
	   others stay in the [[Source]]
	4. Then [[Deplete]] the not [[Damaged]] [[Participants]], but a
	   [[Unit]] [[Direct participant]] holds its [[Slot]]
10. **Rout**: when the winner's **Combat power** is at least twice the
    loser's, the winner chooses for each loser's
    [[Participants|Participant]]: [[Damaged]] or [[Destroy]]ed
11. A [[Damaged]] [[Card]] stays in its place:
	1. A [[Direct participant]] in its [[Star]]
	2. A [[Supporter]] from another [[Star]] in its own [[Star]]
	3. A [[Supporter]] from [[Hand]] goes [[Damaged]] into the target
	   [[Star]]
12. Without a free [[Slot]] in the target [[Star]] the winner
    [[Destroy]]s the loser's [[Card]]s [[Damaged]] there in this
    **Combat**, of its choice, to make room for its moving-in
    [[Unit]]s and the [[Supporter]]s from [[Hand]]; a [[Supporter]]
    from [[Hand]] left without a [[Slot]] is [[Destroy]]ed

#### Notes

1. No dice: multiple **Combat Rounds**, gradual commitments and
   [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s bring decisions, risks, surprises and bluff,
   the result isn't predetermined
2. [[Direct participant]]s hold ground like chess pieces: survivors
   stay or move in
3. Both sides can add any [[Card]]s, but a non-[[Military]] [[Card]]
   has no [[Military power]]: it attacks with 0 and can't [[Boost]],
   and defends with 1 in a [[Star]], with 0 from [[Hand]], see
   [[Defense]]. It joins an [[Attack]] for its
   [[Technology|Technologies]], [[Range]] and [[Capacity]], or to
   move in, and falls with its side
4. Every [[Card]] added is a bet: a [[Card]] with **Combat power** 0
   doesn't raise the winner's loss, but the loser loses it too
5. One scale from a tie to a **Rout**: at a tie the winning
   **Defender** Damages its whole **Combat power**, the loss falls as
   the **Advantage** grows and reaches 0 at twice the loser's
   **Combat power**, where the **Rout** starts. A loser with **Combat
   power** 0 is always routed
6. A tie wrecks both sides: a [[Star]] left with only [[Damaged]]
   [[Card]]s is nobody's, see [[Control]], a graveyard for whoever
   comes next
7. The defeated material is a bet too: a **Defender** losing at home
   keeps its wrecks to repair unless the **Attacker** takes the
   [[Star]] and captures them, see [[ControlledBy]]

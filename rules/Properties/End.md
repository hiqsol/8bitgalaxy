1. **End** is [[Physical]] [[Card]] [[Property]]: one of two short
   edges of a [[Card]]
2. The **End** that is **up** is the one pointing:
	1. On [[Field]] - to the top of the [[Star]] the [[Card]] is in
	2. Elsewhere - away from the player
3. Changing the **End** is [[Manage|Rotate]] (180°):
   movement keeps the **End**
4. [[Front]] [[Side]] of a [[Card]] is double ended, each **End** has
   the same layout, see [[Property]]:
	1. [[Level]] and [[Class]] in one corner
	2. [[PrimaryAbility]] and [[SecondaryAbility]] in the other corner
5. **Strong** **End** is the one with higher [[Level]], the other is
   **weak**
6. A [[Card]] gives [[Ability|Abilities]] from one **End** only, the
   **end in use**: in a [[Slot]] the visible **End**, elsewhere the
   selected **End**
7. Both **Ends** can provide [[Ability|Abilities]], the **Weak**
   **End**'s are just weaker

#### Notes

1. Not to be confused with orientation: [[Unit]]s are vertical,
   [[Structure]]s are horizontal

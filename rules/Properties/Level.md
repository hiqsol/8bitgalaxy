1. **Level** is [[Explicit]] [[Card]] [[Property]]
2. **Level** is number from 1 to 8
3. A [[Card]] has a **strong** and a **weak** [[End]], each with its
   own **Level**
4. A [[Card]] provides one **Level**, the **current** one: the
   **Level** of its [[End]] in use, with its [[Class]], e.g. for
   [[Prerequisites]] and [[Singularity]]

#### Notes

1. After [[Takeoff]] the other [[End]] is inside the [[Pocket]] of the
   [[Slot]]: a [[Card]] shows one **Level**, so it gives one **Level**
   everywhere

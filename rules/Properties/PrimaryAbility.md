1. **Primary Ability** is [[Explicit]] [[Card]] [[Property]]
2. **Primary Ability** is an [[Ability]]: [[Power]], other [[Plus]] or
   [[Technology]]
3. [[Power]] of an [[Actor]] is printed as `X+Y`:
	1. `X` always
	2. `+Y` is added on the [[Field]]: for a [[Direct participant]]
	   and a [[Supporter]] from the [[Field]]

#### Notes

1. No [[Deck]] [[Card]] prints `+Y` yet, the rules allow it: going to
   the [[Field]] pays without it, it thins the deck and keeps an
   unused [[Card]] from going to [[Discard]] at **Draw**, see [[Turn]]

1. **Range** is [[Implicit]] [[Card]] [[Property]] derived from
   [[Level]] and [[Type]]
2. **Range** is the [[Distance]] [[Card]] can cover
3. Default value: current [[Level]] for an [[Actor]], else 0
4. **Range** of a [[Group]] is the sum of the **Ranges** of its
   [[Card]]s, plus **Range** from [[Support]]: of the [[Supporter]]s
   from [[Hand]] that join it, see [[PerformAction]]

#### Notes

1. Big [[Group]]s go far: a [[Group]] reaches farther than any of its
   [[Card]]s

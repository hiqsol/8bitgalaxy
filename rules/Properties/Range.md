1. **Range** is implicit card [[Property]] derived from [[Level]] and
   [[Type]]
2. **Range** is the [[Distance]] card can cover
3. Default value: current [[Level]] for an [[Actor]], else 0
4. **Range** of a group is the sum of the Ranges of its cards, plus
   Range from [[Support]]: of the supporters from [[Hand]] that join
   it, see [[PerformAction]]

#### Notes

1. Big groups go far: a group reaches farther than any of its cards

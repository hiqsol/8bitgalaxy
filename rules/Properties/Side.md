1. **Side** is [[Physical]] [[Component]] [[Property]]: one of two
   faces of a [[Card]] or a [[Tile]]:
	1. **Front** - [[Property|Properties]] and [[Ability|Abilities]]
	2. **Back** - what is known of a [[Hidden]] [[Card]]: its
	   [[Faction]], and by its dashing its [[Role]], [[Unit]] or
	   [[Structure]], and the [[End]] up: [[Hidden]] or [[Damaged]]
2. Changing the **Side** is called **flip**
3. [[Tile]] **Front** up - the [[Tile]] is revealed

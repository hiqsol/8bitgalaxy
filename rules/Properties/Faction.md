1. **Faction** is [[Explicit]] [[Card]] [[Property]]
2. **Faction** is visible from both [[Side]]s of [[Card]]: its color
   and design
3. All [[Card]]s of **faction** form a **Faction** deck
4. There are two **faction** types:
	1. for players
	2. [[Galaxy]]
5. Player **factions** are:
	1. **Symmetric**: their **Faction** decks are the same by [[Card]]
	   values, only color and design differ
	2. **Asymmetric**: each has a **Faction** deck of its own

#### Notes

1. **Symmetric** **Factions** are for an even contest, like white and
   black in chess, see [[GameBalance]]

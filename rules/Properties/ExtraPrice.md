1. **Extra price** is [[Explicit]] [[Component]] [[Property]], of a
   [[Card]] [[End]] or a [[Space]]
2. Printed after the [[Level]] at each [[Card]] [[End]]: `2+1` is
   [[Level]] 2, **Extra price** 1
3. A [[Card]]'s **extra price** can be negative, printed as `2-1`: its
   [[Price]] and [[Size]] stay at least 1

#### Notes

1. Negative **Extra price** makes a strong [[Card]] cheap and light,
   e.g. a [[Ship]] whose [[Strong]] [[End]] keeps the same [[Size]] as
   its [[Weak]] [[End]]

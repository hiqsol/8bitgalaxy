1. **Defense** is [[Implicit]] [[Card]] [[Property]] derived from
   [[Class]] and [[Level]]
2. **Defense** is the [[Combat power]] of a defending [[Card]]
3. Default value:
	1. Current [[Level]] for [[Military]] [[Card]]s
	2. 1 for other [[Card]]s in a [[Star]]
	3. 0 for other [[Card]]s from [[Hand]]
4. [[Plus]] **Defense** increases it
5. A defending [[Supporter]] adds its whole **Defense**, the [[Plus]]
   **Defense** included, as its one [[Ability]], see [[Support]]

#### Notes

1. A [[Military]] [[Card]] defends with its [[Level]], not with its
   [[Power]]: a [[Depleted]] [[Base]] 2M on its [[Weak]] [[End]] has
   no [[Power]], but defends with [[Level]] 1
2. Only [[Military]] [[Card]]s defend from [[Hand]]: otherwise any
   [[Hand]] is a reserve of 5 [[Defense]] behind every [[Star]]

1. **Defense** is [[Implicit]] [[Card]] [[Property]] derived from
   [[Class]] and [[Level]]
2. **Defense** is the [[Combat]] [[Power]] of defending [[Card]] in
   [[Combat]]
3. Default value = current [[Level]] for [[Military]] [[Card]]s, 1 for
   other [[Card]]s
4. [[Plus]] **Defense** increases it
5. A defending [[Supporter]] adds its **Defense** as its one
   [[Ability]], see [[Support]]

#### Notes

1. A [[Military]] [[Card]] defends with its [[Level]], not with its
   [[Power]]: a [[Depleted]] [[Base]] 2M on its [[Weak]] [[End]] has
   no [[Power]], but defends with [[Level]] 1

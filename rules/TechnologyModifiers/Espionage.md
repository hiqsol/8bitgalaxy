1. **Espionage** is [[Science]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. With **Espionage** an [[Action]] can use a [[Technology]] of a
   rival [[Enabled]] [[Card]] in the [[Star]] of the [[Card]] with
   **Espionage**, as if a participant provided it
3. A [[TechnologyAction]] used so pays its [[Cost]] in
   [[SciencePower]]

#### Notes

1. The rival [[Card]] is not a participant: it doesn't [[Deplete]]

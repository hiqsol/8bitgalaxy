1. **Standby** is [[Science]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. **Standby** keeps one unused [[Card]] in [[Hand]] at **Draw** phase
   of [[Turn]]: it doesn't go to [[Discard]], then the player
   [[Draw]]s up to a full [[Hand]]
3. Works from a [[Ready]] [[Structure]]: [[Restore]] comes after
   **Draw**, so the [[Structure]] stays unused in the [[Turn]]
4. A [[Faction]] keeps at most one [[Card]], whatever number of
   **Standby** it has

#### Notes

1. Not a [[Card]] more: the [[Hand]] stays at its size. **Standby**
   saves a good [[Card]] from a deck full of [[Tool]]s
2. A kept [[Card]] can be lost to [[ForceDiscard]] or as a
   [[Defender]] in [[Combat]], it counts for [[Singularity]] like any
   [[Card]] in [[Hand]]

1. **Settle** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. A [[Ship]] with **Settle** converts itself into a [[Colony]]
3. [[Create]] puts the [[Colony]] in the [[Star]] of the [[Ship]],
   no [[Hub]] needed:
	1. The [[Ship]] is a [[Direct participant]] in the
	   [[Destination]], the [[Star]] it is in
	2. [[Range]] is checked as usual, see [[PerformAction]]
	3. The [[Ship]] [[Deplete]]s to [[Discard]], the [[Colony]] takes
	   a free [[Structure]] [[Slot]] there

#### Notes

1. A colony ship: a [[Colony]] far from the [[Academy]]
2. The [[Source]] is usually the same [[Star]], at [[Distance]] 0, the
   [[Ship]] there gives [[Control]] of it
3. A rival **Settle** in a controlled [[Star]] can be [[Intercept]]ed

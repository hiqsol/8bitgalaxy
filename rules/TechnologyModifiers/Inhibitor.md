1. **Inhibitor** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. The [[Star]] of an [[Enabled]] card with **Inhibitor** stops the
   routes of the rivals of its controller: they can't pass the Star,
   they can end there, see [[Distance]] and [[ControlledBy]]
3. Every [[Range]] check obeys it: [[Move]], [[Attack]], a remote
   [[Support]] group, [[Explore]] reach and [[Create]] placement

#### Notes

1. A fortress: [[Pinning]] and [[Jamming]] without [[Actor]]s
2. On a [[Hyperstructure]], which never moves, it holds a fixed front

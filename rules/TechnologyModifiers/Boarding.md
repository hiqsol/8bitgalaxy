1. **Boarding** is [[Production]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. When a [[Combat]] with a [[Card]] using **Boarding** among the
   winner's [[Participants]] makes rival [[Ship]]s [[Damaged]], the
   winner picks one of them instead:
	1. The rival [[Ship]] is [[Destroy]]ed: it goes to its
	   [[Faction]]'s [[Factory]]
	2. The winner's own copy of that [[Ship]], if it is anywhere in
	   the winner's [[Factory]] or [[Laboratory]] piles, goes to the
	   winner's [[Discard]], without [[Research]], [[Create]] or
	   [[Price]]; the other [[Card]]s of the pile keep their order

#### Notes

1. Captured and studied: every [[Card]] stays in its own
   [[Faction]]'s deck, like with [[Assimilate]], a [[Card]] of
   another color never enters a player's deck or [[Field]] side
2. The rival builds the [[Ship]] again with [[Create]] at full
   [[Price]] instead of [[Recover]]ing it, the winner gains a
   [[Ship]] it has not built yet
3. Against a [[Ship]] whose own copy the winner has in play already,
   **Boarding** only [[Destroy]]s: it pays off against late
   [[Ship]]s

1. **Envoy** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. A [[Card]] with **Envoy** comes in peace:
	1. It gives no [[Control]]
	2. It doesn't [[Pinning|Pin]]
	3. It can't be selected in an [[Intercept]]
3. The other [[Card]]s of its [[Action]], its moving [[Group]] or the
   [[Card]] it helps [[Create]], don't [[Pinning|Pin]] and can't be
   selected in an [[Intercept]] during that [[Action]]

#### Notes

1. An embassy in the rival's home: no front, so it can't make a
   [[Star]] [[Disputed]]
2. [[GalacticDiaspora|Galactic Diaspora]] needs no [[Control]]: an
   envoy counts for it, an [[Attack]] can still hit it
3. A delegation travels in peace, but once it lands it is fair game:
   a [[Colony]] raised in a rival [[Star]] with its help can be
   captured at once

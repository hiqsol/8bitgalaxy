1. **Unified Theory** is [[Science]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. A [[Card]] with **Unified Theory** also provides its current
   [[Level]] in one more [[Class]] of the player's choice
3. It counts for [[Prerequisites]] only, still one pair per
   participant, not for [[Singularity]]

#### Notes

1. One theory for all sciences opens the way to the other
   [[Crown]]s, it doesn't replace one: [[Singularity]] still needs
   all four, see [[Deck]]
2. Like Mech [[World]] of Star Realms, an ally of every faction

1. **Emulate** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. With **Emulate** an [[Action]] can use a [[Technology]] printed on
   either [[End]] of the top [[Card]] of a rival's [[Discard]], as if
   a participant provided it
3. A [[TechnologyAction]] used so pays its [[Cost]] in
   [[CulturePower]]

#### Notes

1. Like the Diplomat of Concordia: the face-up [[Discard]] shows what
   the rival just used
2. The rival's [[Card]] is not a participant, it stays in the
   [[Discard]]

1. **Xenoarchaeology** is [[Science]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. With **Xenoarchaeology** a conquest of a [[Wild]] [[Star]] looks at
   the top 3 [[Card]]s of the [[WorldDeck|World deck]] and takes one
   whose [[Prerequisites]] the [[Participants]] satisfy, the others go
   under the [[WorldDeck|World deck]]; if none does, the draw goes on
   as usual

#### Notes

1. Choice instead of luck: the ruins of the [[Ancient]]s are studied
   before the landing
2. On a [[World]] it serves the [[Faction]] that controls its [[Star]]

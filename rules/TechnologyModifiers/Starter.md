1. **Starter** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. **Starter** allows a card on [[Field]] to be the **starter** of an
   [[Action]], see [[PerformAction]]:
	1. Its **source** is its own [[IPS]]
	2. It is a direct participant there and can use all of its
	   abilities
	3. It always [[Deplete]]s after the action, even when it moved or
	   fought
	4. A [[Structure]] must be [[Ready]]

#### Notes

1. A starter from [[Hand]] is only a supporter, so a Field starter is
   stronger: it saves a card from Hand and uses all of its abilities
2. Depleting limits it: an [[Actor]] goes to [[Discard]], a
   [[Structure]] waits for [[Restore]]
3. Always depleting means nobody has to remember which cards already
   moved: an [[Actor]] that starts its own [[Move]] ends in
   [[Discard]]
4. A Structure alone acts only inside its own [[Star]], with its
   [[Range]] 0: it rebuilds, it doesn't fight
5. A Field starter can act with an empty [[Hand]]: the faction is not
   over, see [[Goal]]

1. **Starter** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. **Starter** allows a [[Card]] on [[Field]] to be the **starter** of
   an [[Action]], see [[PerformAction]]:
	1. Its **source** is its own [[IPS]]
	2. It is a [[Direct participant]] there and can use all of its
	   [[Ability|Abilities]]
	3. It always [[Deplete]]s after the [[Action]], even when it moved
	   or fought
	4. A [[Structure]] must be [[Ready]]

#### Notes

1. A **Starter** from [[Hand]] is only a [[Supporter]], so a [[Field]]
   **Starter** is stronger: it saves a [[Card]] from [[Hand]] and uses
   all of its [[Ability|Abilities]]
2. Depleting limits it: an [[Actor]] goes to [[Discard]], a
   [[Structure]] waits for [[Restore]]
3. Always depleting means nobody has to remember which [[Card]]s
   already moved: an [[Actor]] that starts its own [[Move]] ends in
   [[Discard]]
4. A [[Structure]] alone acts only inside its own [[Star]], with its
   [[Range]] 0: it rebuilds, it doesn't fight
5. A [[Field]] **Starter** can act with an empty [[Hand]]: the
   [[Faction]] is not over, see [[Goal]]

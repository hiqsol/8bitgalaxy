1. **Salvage** is [[Production]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. A player whose [[Card]] uses **Salvage** in a [[Combat]] may take 1
   [[Goods]] for each rival [[Card]] that becomes [[Damaged]] in it,
   whoever wins
3. **Requirements**: after the [[Combat]] the player has an
   [[Enabled]] [[Card]] in the target [[Star]]
4. The [[Goods]] go into the target [[Star]]

#### Notes

1. [[Combat]] feeds the economy of both sides, the loser's too if it
   stands in the [[Star]]
2. Taking is a choice: the [[Goods]] stay in the [[Star]], worth it
   only to a player who expects to use the [[Star]] later

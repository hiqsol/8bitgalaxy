1. **Jamming** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. The [[Star]] of a face-up [[Card]] with **Jamming** stops the
   routes of the rivals of its controller, see [[Distance]] and
   [[ControlledBy]]:
	1. They can't pass the [[Star]], they can end there
	2. They pass a [[Space]] bordering the [[Star]] only on the way
	   into or out of it
3. Every [[Range]] check obeys it: [[Move]], [[Attack]], a remote
   [[Support]] [[Group]], [[Explore]] reach and [[Create]] placement

#### Notes

1. Jammed warp: a front blocks fleets, scouting, remote [[Support]]
   and building beyond it, [[Pinning]] without [[Actor]]s
2. On a [[Ship]] the front moves; on a [[Hyperstructure]], which never
   moves, it holds a fixed front

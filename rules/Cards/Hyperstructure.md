1. **Hyperstructure** is a card [[Type]]
2. **Hyperstructure** is a wonder with a strong permanent effect
3. [[Role]] = [[Structure]]
4. [[Origin]] = both [[Social]] and [[Industrial]]
5. [[Range]] = 0 and cannot be cargo of a [[Move]]
6. [[Size]] = current [[Level]] + [[ExtraPrice]]
7. [[Capacity]] = current [[Level]]
8. [[Level]] is at most 8
9. **Hyperstructure** is built by two [[Create]]s completed in one
   [[Star]] during the same [[Turn]]; it stays in the [[Factory]]
   until both are complete:
	1. First as a [[Social]] card, for its [[Price]]
	2. Then as an [[Industrial]] card, for the [[Level]] +
	   [[ExtraPrice]] of its weak [[End]]
10. A faction **possesses** a **Hyperstructure** while it is on the
    [[Field]] and [[ControlledBy]] that faction
11. A **Hyperstructure** works for the faction that possesses it

#### Notes

1. Wonders like in Through the Ages and Civilization
2. Seven wonders nods to 7 Wonders, see [[ComparisonReview]]

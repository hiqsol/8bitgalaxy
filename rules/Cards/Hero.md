1. **Hero** is a [[Card]] [[Type]]
2. **Hero** represents a [[Faction]] **leader**
3. A [[Faction]]'s **active Heroes** are its **Heroes** in the deck:
   on the [[Field]] in any [[State]], in [[Hand]], [[Discard]] and
   [[Reserve]]
4. The **Hero limit** is 2, [[Triumvirate]] raises it to 3
5. A **Hero** is [[Create]]d, or joins the deck otherwise, only while
   the **active Heroes** are fewer than the **Hero limit**
6. A [[Faction]] over the **Hero limit** keeps its **Heroes**

#### Notes

1. **Hero** is a strong figure: its [[Capacity]] can help a [[Group]]
   much
2. **Hero** is a living being upgraded with Warp, see [[Lore]]: that's
   why it takes a [[Slot]] and is as potent as a huge [[Ship]]
3. The limit is checked only when a **Hero** joins: the other
   **Heroes** are in the [[Factory]], the [[Laboratory]] and
   [[History]], the player can show them
4. A [[Damaged]] **Hero** counts: a captive keeps its [[Faction]]
   from raising a new leader, see [[PaxGalactica|Pax Galactica]];
   [[Destroy]]ing it makes room
5. Stronger **Heroes** come by [[Upgrade]], one for one: [[Create]]
   makes only **Heroes** 2/1, see [[Create#3]]

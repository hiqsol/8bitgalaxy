1. **Strategy card** is a role drafted by player every **Politics
   Round**
2. **Politics Round**: every player has one [[Turn]]
3. Each **strategy card** has:
	1. **Primary** effect - strong, for the player who drafted it
	2. **Secondary** effect - weak, other players may use it
4. List of **strategy cards**: TBD

#### Notes

1. Makes the game political without extra rules: players watch and use
   each other's roles

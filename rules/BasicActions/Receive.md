1. **Receive** is [[BasicAction]] of any [[Class]]: the starter's
2. **Receive** has no outcome of its own: its participants' [[Energy]]
   and [[Goods]] [[Plus]]es pay out, as in any action of their Class
3. **Direction**: [[Star]] to [[Star]], the source Star too
4. **Requirements**: [[Range]] only, no [[Power]]

#### Notes

1. The income action: a card with an Energy or Goods Plus pays it in
   any action of its Class, **Receive** is the action for it alone

1. **Hub** is a kind of [[Technology]], the list is in [[Technology]]:
   [[Action]]s on [[Card]]s in [[Home]] piles are directed to a
   **hub**
2. **Social hubs** and **Industrial hubs** take the [[Card]]s of their
   [[Origin]]:
	1. They provide the [[Create]] [[TechnologyAction]] for those
	   [[Card]]s
	2. [[Manage]] of those [[Card]]s in the [[Factory]] is directed to
	   them
3. **Research hubs** take any [[Card]] in the [[Laboratory]]:
   [[Research]] and [[Manage]] of it are directed to them

#### Notes

1. [[Action]]s on [[Home]] [[Card]]s go through a **hub** on the
   [[Field]]: the [[Distance]] to it and its importance put pressure,
   a lost **Hub** stops them, a captured one serves the captor
2. A [[Faction]] without a fitting **hub** builds, [[Research]]es or
   [[Manage]]s no such [[Card]] until it captures one: no global
   **Hub** off the [[Field]]
3. The kinds follow the [[Class]]es that handle the [[Card]]s:
   [[Culture]] the [[Social]], [[Production]] the [[Industrial]],
   [[Science]] any

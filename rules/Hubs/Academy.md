1. **Academy** is [[Culture]] [[Hub]]: the **Social hub**, for
   [[Social]] [[Card]]s
2. When the target [[Star]] of an [[Attack]] has an **Academy**
   [[ControlledBy]] the [[Defender]], the [[Attacker]] can also select
   defending [[Participants]] from the [[Defender]]'s [[Discard]],
   [[Reserve]] or [[Hand]], see [[Combat]]:
	1. From the [[Reserve]] or the [[Hand]] the [[Attacker]] picks
	   blindly, any [[Card]] of the pile
	2. They add their [[Defense]] like every defending participant
	3. A saved one returns to its pile
	4. A [[Damaged]] one goes to the [[Field]], [[Damaged]], into a
	   free [[Slot]] of the target [[Star]]; with no free [[Slot]] it
	   is [[Destroy]]ed

#### Notes

1. Losing the only **Academy** stops [[Social]] building, intended:
   keep another **Academy** or capture a rival's one, a [[Structure]]
   is [[ActiveFor]] the [[Faction]] that controls it, see
   [[ControlledBy]]
2. Attacking the **Academy** reaches [[Hero]]es in the deck, so
   [[Leader]]s can be defeated, see [[PaxGalactica|Pax Galactica]]
3. A pile shows no [[Damaged]] [[State]]: the [[Discard]] is face up,
   a [[Damaged]] [[Card]] in it would need a mark

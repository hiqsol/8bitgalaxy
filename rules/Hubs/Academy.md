1. **Academy** is [[Culture]] [[Hub]]: the **Social hub**, for
   [[Social]] cards
2. When the target [[Star]] of an [[Attack]] has an **Academy**
   [[ControlledBy]] the defender, the attacker can also select
   defending participants from the defender's [[Discard]], [[Reserve]]
   or [[Hand]], see [[Combat]]:
	1. From the Reserve or the Hand the attacker picks blindly, any
	   card of the pile
	2. They add their [[Defense]] like every defending participant
	3. A saved one returns to its pile
	4. A [[Damaged]] one goes to the [[Field]], Damaged, into a free
	   [[Slot]] of the target Star; with no free Slot it is
	   [[Destroy]]ed

#### Notes

1. Losing the only **Academy** stops [[Social]] building, intended:
   keep another **Academy** or capture a rival's one, a [[Structure]]
   is [[ActiveFor]] the faction that controls it, see [[ControlledBy]]
2. Attacking the **Academy** reaches [[Hero]]es in the deck, so
   leaders can be defeated, see [[Goal]]
3. A pile shows no Damaged state: the [[Discard]] is face up, a
   Damaged card in it would need a mark

1. **Pax Galactica** is the [[Military]] achievement of [[Goal]]
2. A [[Faction]] has **Pax Galactica** when every rival is **Fallen**
   at once, and wins immediately
3. A [[Faction]] becomes **Fallen** when one of:
	1. **Can not make a move**: at the start of own [[Turn]], see
	   [[Turn]]
	2. **Has no leader**: at **Check** of own [[Turn]] every [[Hero]]
	   of its [[Faction]] deck is [[Damaged]], in its [[Factory]] or
	   in its [[Laboratory]]
4. A **Fallen** [[Faction]] stays in the game: its [[Card]]s stay
   where they are and it keeps its [[Turn]]s
5. At the start of own [[Turn]] a **Fallen** [[Faction]] stands again
   when it can make a move and has a leader, then plays the [[Turn]]
6. Otherwise it skips **Play** and **Check**, then does **Draw** and
   **Restore**
7. A **Fallen** [[Faction]] has no achievement and can't win at
   [[Twilight]]

#### Notes

1. Named after Pax Romana: the peace of the last power standing
2. Losing all [[Hero]]es is inspired by checkmate in Chess. Like
   check: a player whose last [[Hero]] got [[Damaged]] has one
   [[Turn]] to get a [[Hero]] back
3. The leader check counts the dead [[Hero]]es: the others are alive
   wherever they are, nobody reveals a [[Hand]], the [[Reserve]] or a
   [[Hidden]] [[Card]]
4. Losing the home [[Star]] is a heavy loss by intention, a goal for
   rivals, but it doesn't end the game, neither does losing the
   [[Hub]]s: the [[Faction]] still moves and can defeat the rival
   leaders. The captor gets the [[Hub]]s, like taking the queen in
   Chess
5. Losing the last [[Star]] [[ActiveFor|Active]] for a [[Faction]]
   makes it **Fallen**: it has no place for [[Takeoff]] and no
   [[Source]] for an [[Action]] from [[Hand]]. Losing [[Control]] is
   not enough: a [[Disputed]] [[Star]] is not lost, the [[Faction]]
   takes off and acts there
6. Not an empty [[Hand]] but no move makes a [[Faction]] **Fallen**:
   the [[Attacker]] draws a full [[Hand]] at the end of own [[Turn]],
   see [[Turn]], while a [[Defender]] who spends the whole [[Hand]] in
   [[Combat]] may start own [[Turn]] with no move, like an unexpected
   checkmate
7. In a [[Duel]] the first fall ends the game. With more rivals a
   **Fallen** [[Faction]] can come back: no move costs a
   [[Turn]], a lost [[Star]] comes back when its remaining [[Card]]s
   regain one, a lost leader when its [[Hero]] is face up again
8. A **Fallen** [[Faction]] can't bring back its own leaders: it skips
   **Play**. A rival does, to keep a third [[Faction]] from
   **Pax Galactica**, or an ally in a cooperative game
9. A [[Hero]] face up again returns to its [[Faction]]: a
   face-up [[Actor]] is controlled by its own [[Faction]], see
   [[ControlledBy]]

1. A [[Faction]]'s **victory points** are the sum of the current
   [[Level]]s of its non-[[Damaged]] [[Social]] [[Card]]s on the
   [[Field]] [[ControlledBy]] it
2. At [[Twilight]], current achievements are counted before
   [[Hidden]] [[Card]]s are revealed to count their [[Level]]s
3. A rule can add **Victory points**
4. When **Victory points** decide the winner, the [[Faction]] with the
   most wins; a tie is a draw

#### Notes

1. **Victory points** value the living empire on the current board
2. Only [[Social]] [[Card]]s score: their advantage over
   [[Industrial]] [[Card]]s

1. **Kardashev Ascension** is the [[Production]] achievement of
   [[Goal]]
2. A [[Faction]] has **Kardashev Ascension** while it possesses at
   least 7 [[Hyperstructure]]s

#### Notes

1. Named after the Kardashev scale, which ranks civilizations by the
   energy they harness: seven wonders across the galaxy make a
   galactic civilization
2. Seven wonders nods to 7 Wonders, see [[ComparisonReview]]

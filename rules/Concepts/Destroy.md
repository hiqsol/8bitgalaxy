1. **Destroy** moves a [[Card]] from anywhere back to its supply:
	1. A player [[Faction]]'s [[Card]] goes on top of a [[Factory]]
	   pile of the player's choice, so it can be rebuilt next
	2. Any other [[Card]] goes under its deck, e.g. a [[World]] under
	   the [[WorldDeck|World deck]]

#### Notes

1. **Destroy** is not a [[State]]: a destroyed [[Card]] is like any
   other [[Card]] in the [[Factory]]

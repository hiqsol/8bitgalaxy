1. **Destroy** moves a [[Card]] from anywhere to its [[Faction]]'s
   [[Factory]]
2. The player puts it on top of a [[Factory]] pile of their choice, so
   it can be rebuilt next

#### Notes

1. **Destroy** is not a [[State]]: a destroyed [[Card]] is like any
   other [[Card]] in the [[Factory]]

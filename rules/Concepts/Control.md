1. **Control** is a [[Faction]]'s level of presence in a [[Star]]
2. A faction has **Control** in a Star only with cards of its
   [[Faction]] there that are not [[Damaged]], the level in ascending
   order:
	1. **Structure** - a [[Structure]]
	2. **Actor** - an [[Actor]]

#### Notes

1. Only own cards count, so Control doesn't depend on itself: a
   captured Structure gives the captor no Control, see
   [[ControlledBy]]
2. A Star with no such cards is nobody's, not [[Disputed]]

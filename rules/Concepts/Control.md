1. **Control** is a [[Faction]]'s level of presence in a [[Star]]
2. A [[Faction]] has **Control** in a [[Star]] only with [[Card]]s of
   its [[Faction]] there that are not [[Damaged]], the level in
   ascending order:
	1. **Structure** - a [[Structure]]
	2. **Actor** - an [[Actor]]

#### Notes

1. Only own [[Card]]s count, so **Control** doesn't depend on itself:
   a captured [[Structure]] gives the captor no **Control**, see
   [[ControlledBy]]
2. A [[Star]] with no such [[Card]]s is nobody's, not [[Disputed]]

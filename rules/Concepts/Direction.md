1. **Direction** is where an [[Action]] goes: from the **source**,
   where it starts, to the **destination**, where it finishes
2. An [[Action]] directed to a [[Card]], e.g. to a [[Hub]], is
   directed to the [[Star]] the [[Card]] is in
3. **Direction** has length: [[Distance]] from **Source** to
   **Destination**

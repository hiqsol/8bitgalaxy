1. **Support** is taking part in [[PerformAction]] from remote:
	1. From [[Hand]], including the **starter** of the [[Action]], or
	2. From another [[IPS]], not the [[Source]] or the
	   [[Destination]], as a [[Group]], see [[PerformAction]]:
	   reaching doesn't use their one [[Ability]]
2. **Supporters** add to [[Action]] only one [[Ability]]:
	1. [[PrimaryAbility]]
	2. [[SecondaryAbility]]
	3. [[Range]]
	4. [[Capacity]]

#### Notes

1. **Support** is local: positions on the [[Field]] matter
2. A [[Structure]] has [[Range]] 0: from another [[IPS]] it supports
   only in a [[Group]] with an [[Actor]] from its [[Star]] or from
   [[Hand]], see [[PerformAction]]

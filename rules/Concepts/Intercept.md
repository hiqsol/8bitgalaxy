1. **Intercept** is a [[Combat]] the controller of a [[Star]] starts
   against rival [[Card]]s that come into it
2. When rival [[Card]]s come into [[Slot]]s of a [[Star]], not by
   winning a [[Combat]], the [[Faction]] that
   [[ControlledBy|Controlled]] it before can **intercept** them right
   after the [[Action]] that brought them:
	1. It starts a [[Combat]] in that [[Star]] as the [[Attacker]]
	2. It is not an [[Attack]]: no [[Starter]] and no [[Military]]
	   [[Actor]] needed, that [[Star]] is the [[Source]] and the
	   target
	3. The [[Attacker]] selects defending [[Participants]] from the
	   [[Card]]s that came in

#### Notes

1. Moving in is not a free win: the controller answers at once, at the
   rival's [[Turn]], with own [[Card]]s in the [[Star]] and from
   [[Hand]]
2. A rival [[Move]] or [[Create]] into a controlled [[Star]] can be
   intercepted, a rival [[Takeoff]] can't come there

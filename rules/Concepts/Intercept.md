1. **Intercept** is a [[Combat]] the controller of a [[Star]] starts
   against rival cards that come into it
2. When rival cards come into [[Slot]]s of a [[Star]], not by winning
   a [[Combat]], the faction that [[ControlledBy|controlled]] it
   before can **intercept** them right after the action that brought
   them:
	1. It starts a [[Combat]] in that Star as the attacker
	2. It is not an [[Attack]]: no starter and no [[Military]]
	   [[Actor]] needed, that Star is the source and the target
	3. The attacker selects defending participants from the cards
	   that came in

#### Notes

1. Moving in is not a free win: the controller answers at once, at the
   rival's [[Turn]], with own cards in the Star and from [[Hand]]
2. A rival [[Move]] or [[Create]] into a controlled Star can be
   intercepted, a rival [[Takeoff]] can't come there

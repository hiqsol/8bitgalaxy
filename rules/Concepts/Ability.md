1. **Ability** is what a card or a tile provides, see [[Component]]
2. **Ability** is a [[Plus]] or a [[Technology]]
3. **Abilities** come from:
	1. Card print: [[PrimaryAbility]], [[SecondaryAbility]]
	2. Card [[Level]]: [[Range]], [[Capacity]] and [[Level]] with
	   [[Class]] for [[Prerequisites]]
	3. [[Space]]: given by its Type
4. A combined print, several items in one [[PrimaryAbility]] or
   [[SecondaryAbility]], is one **Ability**, e.g. a [[Technology]]
   with a [[Plus]]

#### Notes

1. A [[Support]]er's one **Ability** gives the whole combined print

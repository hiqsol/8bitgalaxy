1. **Technology** is a kind of [[Ability]]: a named rule
2. **Technologies** come in three kinds: [[TechnologyAction]],
   [[TechnologyModifier]] and [[Hub]]
3. The list below is the only catalog of **Technologies**, an item per
   **Technology**:
   `Name: LevelClasses - Kind - Types - Factions - Scope`, then a
   short description:
	1. **LevelClasses**: the [[Level]] of each [[Deck]] [[Card]] that
	   carries the **Technology**, as in the [[Card]] name, with the
	   [[Class]]es of the carrying [[End]]s, e.g. `2cp 4cp`
	2. **Kind**: [[Action]], Modifier, or a [[Hub]] with its
	   [[Origin]] or [[Research]]
	3. **Types**: the [[Type]]s of the carrying [[Card]]s
	4. **Factions**: Player for every [[Symmetric]] player [[Faction]]
	   or [[Galaxy]]; a **Technology** of an [[Asymmetric]]
	   [[Faction]] only names it
	5. **Description** starts with a **label** when one expresses the
	   **Technology**: the term it adds or removes, e.g. `+Control`;
	   then "Immediate" for an [[ImmediateAction]], then a colon
	6. Values without a carrying [[Card]] yet are intended, e.g. all
	   values of a planned **Technology**
	7. **Technology** pages name their kind and **Class** in the first
	   rule, as in the list, and describe behavior, without level

1. **Plus** is a kind of [[Ability]]: a number, shown as `+N`
2. **Plus** of all [[Participants]] sums up
3. **Plus** can add:
	1. [[Power]] - plain `+N` is [[Power]] of the [[End]]'s [[Class]]
	2. [[Goods]]
	3. [[Energy]]
	4. [[Defense]]
	5. [[Range]] - to the [[Group]] in [[Move]]
	6. [[Capacity]] - to the [[Cargo]] total in [[Move]]
	7. [[DeckBuilding|Draw]], an [[ImmediateAction]]
	8. [[Slot]]s - free [[Slot]]s in a [[Star]]
	9. [[Hand]] size
	10. [[Restore]] - more [[Structure]]s that become [[Ready]] at the
	    **Restore** phase of own [[Turn]]
	11. [[VictoryPoints|Victory points]] for [[Objective]]s
	12. Votes for [[Politics]]
4. A [[Resource]] **Plus**, [[Energy]] or [[Goods]], works only in an
   [[Action]] of its [[Card]]'s [[Class]], see [[Action]]

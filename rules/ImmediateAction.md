1. **Immediate action** is an [[Action]] used by showing the card
   that provides it, from [[Hand]] or the [[Field]]
2. **Immediate action** is used at any time of the player's activity:
   own [[Turn]] or a [[Combat]] the player takes part in
3. **Immediate action** has no starter, no Direction, no Cost and no
   other participants
4. The shown card [[Deplete]]s immediately

#### Notes

1. Like Star Realms abilities: show a card, get the effect at once

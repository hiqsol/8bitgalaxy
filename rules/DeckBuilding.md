1. **Deck building**: [[Unit]] [[Card]]s cycle from [[Discard]] by
   **Shuffle** to [[Reserve]], by **Draw** to [[Hand]], then by
   [[Takeoff]] to the [[Field]] or back to [[Discard]], see [[Turn]],
   [[Deplete]]
2. **Draw** takes [[Card]]s from the top of [[Reserve]] to [[Hand]]
3. **Shuffle**, only when a [[Card]] must be drawn from an empty
   [[Reserve]]: all [[Card]]s of [[Discard]] are shuffled into a new
   [[Reserve]], then the **Draw** continues

#### Notes

1. [[Unit]]s form the cycling deck, [[Structure]]s form the persistent
   engine on the [[Field]]

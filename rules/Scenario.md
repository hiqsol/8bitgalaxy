1. **Scenario** is a predefined framework that describes setup for a
   game session.
2. It can include:
	1. Number of players
	2. Game mode: solo, co-op, competitive
	3. [[Field]] layout: its shape, home [[Star]]s, [[Star]]
	   [[Level]]s other than 1 and the [[Space]] [[Tile]]s placed at
	   the start, e.g. [[Void]]s
	4. The [[Space bag]] and how a [[Hidden]] [[Space]] draws from
	   it, see [[Space]]
	5. [[GalaxyDeck]] composition, see [[Galaxy]]
	6. **Ancient deck** and **World deck**, see [[Ancient]], [[World]]
	7. Starting [[Home]] piles

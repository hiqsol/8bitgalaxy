1. Player represents one of the galaxy [[Faction]]s.
2. A [[Faction]]'s **goal** has four paths, one per [[Class]]
3. [[Military]] victory is immediate: a [[Faction]] wins when every
   rival is over, see rule 7
4. The three equal non-[[Military]] achievements are:
	1. [[Culture]]: [[GalacticDiaspora|Galactic Diaspora]]
	2. [[Production]]: possesses at least 7 [[Hyperstructure]]s
	3. [[Science]]: [[Singularity]]
5. At **Check** of own [[Turn]], having a non-[[Military]] achievement
   triggers [[Twilight]]
6. At the end of a **Goal**-triggered [[Twilight]], compare the
   current state:
	1. The [[Faction]] with the most non-[[Military]] achievements
	   wins
	2. If tied, the [[Faction]] with the most [[VictoryPoints]] wins
	3. A further tie is a draw
7. A [[Faction]] is over when one of:
	1. **Can not make a move**, see [[Turn]]
	2. **Has no leader**: at **Check** phase of own [[Turn]] every
	   [[Hero]] of its [[Faction]] deck is [[Damaged]], in its
	   [[Factory]] or in its [[Laboratory]]

#### Notes

1. Four paths like the four victories of Civilization
2. The three non-[[Military]] achievements are equal, but reaching
   more than one gives an advantage at [[Twilight]]
3. Achievements are checked again at the end of [[Twilight]]: a rival
   can break one during the last [[Turn]]
4. Losing all [[Hero]]es is inspired by checkmate in Chess. Like
   check: a player whose last [[Hero]] got [[Damaged]] has one
   [[Turn]] to get a [[Hero]] back
5. The [[Leader]] check counts the dead [[Hero]]es: the others are
   alive wherever they are, nobody reveals a [[Hand]], the [[Reserve]]
   or a [[Hidden]] [[Card]]
6. Losing the home [[Star]] is a heavy loss by intention, a goal for
   rivals, but it doesn't end the game, neither does losing the
   [[Hub]]s: the [[Faction]] still moves and can defeat the rival
   [[Leader]]. The captor gets the [[Hub]]s, like taking the queen in
   Chess
7. Losing the last [[Star]] [[ActiveFor|Active]] for a [[Faction]]
   does end the game: it has no place for [[Takeoff]] and no
   [[Source]] for an [[Action]] from [[Hand]]. Losing [[Control]] is
   not enough: a [[Disputed]] [[Star]] is not lost, the [[Faction]]
   takes off and acts there
8. An empty [[Hand]] at the start of own [[Turn]] can end the game
   too, like an unexpected checkmate: spending the whole [[Hand]] on
   defense in [[Combat]] is a real risk

1. **Wild** is a [[Star]] [[State]]
2. [[Star]] is **wild** when its [[World slot]] is empty, see [[Star]]
3. An [[Attack]] on a **wild** [[Star]] conquers it:
	1. The [[Attacker]] draws [[Card]]s from the
	   [[WorldDeck|World deck]] until the [[Attack]]'s
	   [[Participants]] satisfy the [[Prerequisites]] of one, the
	   others go under the [[WorldDeck|World deck]]. If none does,
	   there is no conquest
	2. [[Ancient]]s defend the [[Star]]
	3. If the [[Attacker]] wins, the [[World]] goes into the [[Star]]:
	   it is not **wild** any more
	4. Else the [[World]] goes under the [[WorldDeck|World deck]], the
	   [[Star]] stays **wild**

#### Notes

1. [[Star]]s are open [[Tile]]s, so movement is fast: a [[Star]] is
   conquered only to use it
2. A **wild** [[Star]] can be [[ControlledBy]] a [[Faction]]:
   [[Control]] comes from [[Card]]s, conquest gives the [[World]]

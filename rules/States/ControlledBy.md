1. **Controlled by** is a [[Star]] and card [[State]] relative to a
   [[Faction]]
2. [[Star]] is **controlled by** the faction with strictly the highest
   [[Control]] in it, a tie makes it [[Disputed]]
3. A card is **controlled by** its [[Faction]], but a [[Structure]] or
   a [[Damaged]] card in a Star controlled by another faction is
   controlled by that faction

#### Notes

1. Taking a Star captures the rival Structures and wrecks in it, a
   [[Disputed]] Star captures nothing

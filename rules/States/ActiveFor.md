1. **Active for** is a [[Card]] and [[Star]] [[State]] relative to a
   [[Faction]]
2. [[Card]] is **active for** [[Faction]] when:
	1. It is not [[Damaged]]
	2. It is [[ControlledBy]] the [[Faction]]
	3. It is on the [[Field]], in [[Hand]] or in [[History]]
3. [[Star]] is **active for** [[Faction]] when it is [[ControlledBy]]
   the [[Faction]] or [[Disputed]] with the [[Faction]] among the tied
   ones

#### Notes

1. [[History]] [[Card]]s are out of play but still active: past
   greatness counts

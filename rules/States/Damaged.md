1. **Damaged** is a [[Card]] [[State]]
2. [[Card]] becomes **damaged** in [[Combat]], when the winner doesn't
   save it
3. A **Damaged** [[Card]] that becomes **Damaged** again is
   [[Destroy]]ed
4. A **Damaged** [[Card]] stays on the [[Field]] until it is
   [[Destroy]]ed or face up again
5. Anyone may look at a **Damaged** [[Card]]: a wreck is no secret
6. A **Damaged** [[Card]] is no [[Participants|Participant]] of any
   [[Action]] until face up again: it doesn't fight, support or
   act, and its [[Ability|Abilities]] and effects don't work
7. [[Attack]] [[Destroy]]s **Damaged** [[Card]]s in a separate
   [[Combat]]: the [[Attacker]] selects only **Damaged** [[Card]]s in
   the target [[Star]], nobody defends them, each 1 [[Military power]]
   [[Destroy]]s one of them

#### Notes

1. **Disabled**, **Exhausted** are the same [[State]],
   indistinguishable
2. Wrecks are cleared by force, in their own [[Combat]], not in a
   fight for the [[Star]]: three wrecks take 3 [[Military power]]

1. **State** is a condition of a [[Card]] or a [[Star]]
2. [[Card]] **States** shown by [[Side]] and [[End]] are in the table
   below, only a [[Card]] on the [[Field]] has them
3. A [[Card]] with its [[Front]] up is face up: a face-up [[Unit]] has
   no other **State**, a face-up [[Structure]] is [[Ready]] or
   [[Depleted]]
4. [[Card]] **States** relative to a [[Faction]] or player:
	1. [[ActiveFor]]
	2. [[ControlledBy]]
5. [[Star]] **States**:
	1. [[ActiveFor]]
	2. [[ControlledBy]]
	3. [[Disputed]]
	4. [[Wild]]

|---|---|---|---|
| [[Ready]] | [[Structure]] | [[Front]] | [[Strong]] |
| [[Depleted]] | [[Structure]] | [[Front]] | [[Weak]] |
| [[Damaged]] | any | [[Back]] | [[Damaged]] |
| [[Hidden]] | any | [[Back]] | [[Hidden]] |

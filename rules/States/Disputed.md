1. **Disputed** is a [[Star]] [[State]]
2. [[Star]] is **disputed** when two or more [[Faction]]s have equal
   highest [[Control]] in it

#### Notes

1. A **Disputed** [[Star]] is a front: nobody controls it, each tied
   [[Faction]] takes off and acts there with its own [[Card]]s, see
   [[ActiveFor]]

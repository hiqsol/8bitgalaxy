1. **Takeoff** plays **ONE** [[Unit]] [[Card]] from [[Hand]] into a
   [[Star]] [[ActiveFor]] the player, see [[Turn]]
2. Player chooses the [[End]] the [[Card]] is placed with
3. **Takeoff** has no [[Cost]] and is not an [[Action]]: the [[Card]]
   can act in the same [[Turn]]

#### Notes

1. Acting in the same [[Turn]] means nobody has to remember which
   [[Card]]s took off

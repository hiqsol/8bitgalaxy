# Combat playtest

Ten [[Combat]] situations, each played by a subagent twice from the
same start: under the current rules and under the proposed package of
2026-11-07. Both sides played as well as they could, each deciding
from its own knowledge only. One game per situation: these are
examples, not statistics. The scripted simulations are in
[[CombatVariants]]; the cost units are those of [[WarEconomy]].

## Summary

1. **The Attack power works and changes no number**: in all ten games
   it gave the same totals as [[MilitaryPower]]. It needs one sentence
   on [[Boost]], and a name: [[Attack]] is already the [[BasicAction]]
2. **Supporters at stake fix the current rules' free moves**: an
   attack with [[Supporter]]s only risked nothing (S3), a lost raid
   still paid (S9, +1.4), a spare card cut the winner's saves for 1
   card (S5, S6). All gone: every added card is a bet
3. **But they break with the winner's damage measured in the loser's
   [[Level]]s**: a beaten side throws in cards of 0 [[Defense]], pure
   [[Level]], so the winner must Damage everything, the [[Actor]] that
   would move in included. A winning raid worth +5.1 became a mutual
   wipe worth 0 (S1); the same lever decided S4 and S10. A tie or a
   close win wipes out the winning [[Defender]] (S6, S8): an
   [[Attacker]]'s [[Level]] is about twice its **Attack power**
4. **The rout at more than twice the loser's total fires only against
   a small garrison** (S4 opening, S7), never after both sides
   escalate (S5, S6, S8, S9). In S10 it turned into a seesaw: each
   side added 1-point cards to switch it on and off. It deters probes,
   and a careful [[Attacker]] computes the rout-proof total (S2)
5. **Damaged [[Hand]] Supporters landing as wrecks is the weakest
   part**, rated worse in 8 games:
	1. One-sided: the [[Attacker]]'s land in the rival [[Star]]
	   and are captured, the [[Defender]]'s land at home and repair at
	   half the [[Price]]: a cheap deployment, so a winner picks its
	   own [[Hand]] cards as damage fodder
	2. Graveyards: 8 to 10 wrecks around one [[Ship]] or around nobody
	   (S1, S4, S6), more than the 5 [[Unit]] [[Slot]]s (S4)
6. **Remote [[Structure]] Supporters Damaged at their [[Star]]s work**
   (S3, S10): the far [[Colony]] becomes a real stake, and its
   [[Star]] is left exposed. But a [[Colony]] 4M gives [[Defense]] 7
   for [[Level]] 4, the cheapest fodder for a winner's damage
7. **Net for the [[Attacker]]**: better in the two raids it won (S4,
   S7), much worse in the escalated losses (S6 −4.1 → −15.1, S8
   −2.7 → −10.6). The [[Defender]] gains most, from the tie, the last
   word in each [[Combat Round]] and the home wrecks
8. **A fix for both 3 and 4 in one rule**: measure the winner's damage
   in totals, not [[Level]]s: Damage own [[Participants]] of total
   [[Level]] at least the loser's total − **Advantage**, that is twice
   the loser's total minus the winner's. A 0-power card adds nothing,
   and the damage falls to 0 exactly where the rout begins. Not
   played yet

## Rules played

**Common**: [[Combat]] as written, [[Defense]] as amended, capture by
[[ControlledBy#3]], [[Recover]] at half the [[Price]].

**Current**: [[Combat#9]]: the winner saves own
[[Direct participant]]s of total [[Level]] up to the **Advantage**,
all other [[Direct participant]]s are [[Damaged]]; [[Supporter]]s
never are.

**Proposed**:

1. **Attack power**: the [[MilitaryPower]] of a [[Military]] [[Card]],
   0 for any other. [[Combat]] compares **Attack power** with
   [[Defense]]
2. [[Participants]] are [[Direct participant]]s and [[Supporter]]s
   alike. The loser's [[Participants]] become [[Damaged]]
3. The winner Damages own [[Participants]] of total [[Level]] at least
   the loser's total [[Level]] − **Advantage**, chosen by the winner
4. **Rout**: the winner's total is more than twice the loser's; the
   winner chooses for each loser [[Participants|Participant]]:
   [[Damaged]] or [[Destroy]]ed
5. A [[Damaged]] [[Supporter]] from [[Hand]] lands as a wreck in the
   [[Combat]] [[Star]]; a remote one is [[Damaged]] at its own
   [[Star]]

**Ledger**, in [[Hand]] cards: a spent card 1, a repair half the
[[Price]] plus 1, a [[Destroy]]ed card the [[Price]] plus 1 and 1 more
for an [[Actor]], a taken [[Star]] 3, see [[WarEconomy]].

### The games in brief

1. **S1 Border raid**: under the current rules two old [[Ship]]s and a
   [[Tool]] broke the guard 4 to 3, the [[Ship]] 1M moved in and took
   the [[Colony]]. Under the proposed rules the beaten colonists threw
   their useless cards into the line to raise their [[Level]], the
   raiders threw fodder back, and both fleets lay wrecked around a
   [[Colony]] nobody held
2. **S2 Fortress**: [[Defense]] 11 and five cards behind it deterred a
   fleet of 6 under both rules. The proposed rules raise the price of
   a mistake and add an upside the current ones lack: a narrow win now
   keeps the [[Hero]] whole to move in
3. **S3 Remote battery**: under the current rules the [[Attacker]]
   left its [[Ship]] out and shelled the [[Star]] with a remote
   [[Colony]] and [[Hand]] cards only: no risk, two cards spent on
   each side, nothing changed. Under the proposed rules the [[Colony]]
   at home is at stake, and the raid stays home
4. **S4 Lone Hero**: the same seven cards decided it 7 to 6 under
   both rules. Under the proposed ones the [[Defender]]'s first card
   was a lever on the [[Attacker]]'s damage bill, not a bid to win,
   and one [[Ship]] 2C held the captured [[Hero]] among eight wrecks,
   four its own
5. **S5 The prize**: a [[Hero]] 4M thrown at the [[Hyperstructure]]
   led 11 to 10, the [[Defender]]'s [[Hero]] answered. The winner
   chose its own [[Hyperstructure]] as damage fodder: [[Level]] 6 for
   one repair
6. **S6 Even clash**: under the current rules a win by less than 4
   saved nothing and took nothing, so the [[Attacker]] passed and both
   fought over saves. Under the proposed rules a win by 1 takes the
   [[Star]], so both went all in. A tie at 18 left the winning
   [[Defender]] holding a graveyard with no working [[Ship]]
7. **S7 Wreck clearing**: the [[Attacker]] ignored the wrecks, beat
   the lone picket and captured the wrecks with the [[Star]]. Clearing
   the wrecks by force would cost the winner heavily under the
   proposed rules: a wreck defends with 1 but counts its full
   [[Level]]
8. **S8 Hidden sentinel**: the [[Attacker]] bet on a two-card outpost,
   the [[Hidden]] [[Colony]] 4M flipped at the end. Under the proposed
   rules every added card was at stake, so the [[Attacker]] escalated
   step by step into a sunk-cost trap, and four of its cards lie
   captured in the rival orbit
9. **S9 Boost and colors**: under the current rules three cheap cards
   and the [[Energy]] lost 5 to 8 and still cracked the [[Base]].
   Under the proposed rules the raid would turn into captured wrecks,
   so the fleet stayed home
10. **S10 Two-sided attack**: a remote [[Colony]] 4M made a free wall
    of 7 under the current rules. Under the proposed ones the doomed
    [[Attacker]] fed [[Level]] to make the [[Defender]] pay, the rout
    switched on and off with each card, and the far [[Colony]] went
    down with the [[Defender]]'s [[Ship]] 4M

## Findings per change

### 1. Attack power

1. Neutral in all ten: the same numbers as [[MilitaryPower]], a
   cleaner pair with [[Defense]]
2. It needs one sentence on [[Boost]] (S9): either [[Energy]] adds to
   the total whatever the [[Card]], as [[Combat]] note 3 says now, or
   only a [[Military]] [[Card]] carries it. The totals barely changed
   (10 or 9 against 6), but the choice decides whether a
   non-[[Military]] [[Ship]] or the [[Defender]]'s [[Energy]] matters
   at all
3. The name clashes with the [[Attack]] [[BasicAction]]

### 2. Supporters at stake

1. Better: no more free attacks with [[Supporter]]s only (S3), no
   profitable lost raid (S9), no spite card for 1 (S5, S6), the far
   [[Colony]] is no free wall (S10). Commitments carry risk, a
   mistaken attack costs more (S2, S3, S9)
2. Worse with rule 3: every [[Card]] of 0 power is a [[Level]] dump
   that raises the winner's damage (S1, S4, S10), a 0-power
   starter is pure stake (S2)

### 3. The winner's damage in Levels

The main source of trouble, not in the list of changes but played
with them:

1. **Level dumping**: the loser adds [[Card]]s of 0 power to deny the
   winner its mover (S1, S4, S10). Each [[Defender]] card of 1M raises
   the bill by 2, each [[Attacker]] card of 1M lowers it by 1 (S4)
2. **Mixed units**: it subtracts power (the **Advantage**) from
   [[Level]]s. **Attack power** is about half the [[Level]],
   [[Defense]] about all of it, so the [[Attacker]]'s [[Level]] dwarfs
   any lead and a [[Defender]] that wins close damages everything (S6,
   S8)
3. **Bill over the winner's [[Level]]s**: the winner is wholly
   [[Damaged]], and its **Advantage** no longer matters, so it adds
   the minimum (S6, S8). The rule doesn't say this case
4. **Fodder**: the winner picks the most [[Level]] per repair: its own
   [[Hand]] cards as home wrecks, its [[Hyperstructure]] (S5), the
   remote [[Colony]] 4M (S10)
5. **[[Level]] against [[Price]]**: a [[Ship]] 4C on its [[Weak]]
   [[End]] counts [[Level]] 3 but repairs at [[Price]] 5 (S1, S2)

**Fix to play next**: the winner Damages own [[Participants]] of total
[[Level]] at least the loser's total − **Advantage**. [[Card]]s of 0
power add nothing, totals meet totals, and since loser − (winner −
loser) = 2 × loser − winner, the damage reaches 0 exactly where the
rout of more than 2× begins: one scale from a narrow win to a rout.

### 4. Rout at more than 2×

1. It fires against a lone small [[Card]]: 4 against 1 (S7), 5
   against 1 (S4, broken at once by one [[Defender]] card)
2. It never fires after both sides escalate: 22, 24 or 36 needed
   (S5, S8, S6)
3. A seesaw near the threshold: 1-point cards switch it on and off
   (S10). A careful [[Attacker]] buys a rout-proof total, exactly 2×
   is no rout (S2)
4. The winner's choice: equal in the ledger for a [[Unit]] in the
   winner's [[Star]]; Damage keeps a [[Hero]] as hostage (S4),
   [[Destroy]] stops a heal back. A [[Structure]] is better captured
5. Coarse at small totals: 4 against 2 is no rout (S1)

### 5. Wrecks and remote damage

1. **Asymmetric**: the [[Attacker]]'s [[Hand]] wrecks are captured,
   the [[Defender]]'s stay home (S6, S8, S9). With the tie and the
   last word in every [[Combat Round]] this pushes hard toward the
   [[Defender]]
2. **Cheap deployment**: a [[Damaged]] own [[Hand]] card on the
   [[Field]] repairs at half the [[Price]], cheaper than building it
   (S2, S3, S5, S8)
3. **Graveyards and [[Slot]]s**: 8 to 10 wrecks in one [[Star]] (S1,
   S4, S6); with 5 [[Unit]] [[Slot]]s the mover may not get in
   ([[Slot#6]], S4); wrecks in a [[Star]] nobody holds wait for the
   first [[Actor]] (S1)
4. **Captured rival [[Unit]] wrecks** give the captor nothing, since
   [[Recover]] returns them, and lie there for ever (S5, S6)
5. **Which [[End]]**: a [[Ship]] 4C played as 3M, as a wreck on which
   [[End]]? It sets its [[Level]] and [[Defense]] (S8)
6. **Remote damage** works (S3, S10): the far [[Structure]] is a
   stake, and its [[Star]] is weaker after

Options for a [[Damaged]] [[Hand]] [[Supporter]]:

1. It is [[Destroy]]ed: symmetric, no wreck piles, no cheap
   deployment, no [[Slot]] problem; the harshest, scripted V3 cut the
   attacks
2. It goes to [[Discard]] [[Damaged]]: a new rule for [[Recover]] off
   the [[Field]]
3. It goes to [[Discard]] as now and only counts for the totals: then
   [[Hand]] cards are at stake only in a rout

## Frictions of the current rules

1. A win by less than the smallest own [[Direct participant]]'s
   [[Level]] equals a loss: nothing saved, no mover, no [[Star]] (S2,
   S5, S6). Passing beats winning narrowly
2. [[Supporter]]s are free: an attack with [[Supporter]]s only (S3), a
   remote wall (S10), a lost raid that pays (S9), a spite card (S5,
   S6)
3. A tie wins, but the winning [[Defender]] Damages all it committed
   (S1, S5, S8)
4. The [[Attacker]] selects the defenders, yet the [[Defender]] adds
   the rest in the first [[Combat Round]] (S3, S10); an [[Enabled]]
   defender left out blocks the take (S5)
5. A wreck selected as a defender is Destroyed even if the
   [[Attacker]] loses (S7), and capturing wrecks with the [[Star]]
   beats clearing them

## Rule gaps found

1. The brief let a [[Tool]] start the [[Action]], but [[Actor#3]]:
   only an [[Actor]] starts one. S1 and S7 started with a [[Tool]] 1M;
   a [[Ship]] 2C as 1M gives the same power, the play holds
2. [[Capacity#3]]: [[Capacity]] is the [[Level]], the brief said 2 and
   1 (S9)
3. Does an empty [[Hand]] make the [[Attacker]] [[Fallen]] too?
   Assumed yes (S1, S2)
4. Can [[Source]] [[Card]]s join after the setup? Can a [[Defender]]
   add its own wrecks?
5. A [[Base]] 2M as remote [[Supporter]]: is its one
   [[Ability]] [[Defense]] 3, the [[Level]] and the +1 together? (S3)
6. A [[Hero]] in [[Hand]] keeps a [[Faction]] from falling
   ([[PaxGalactica#3]]), so a decapitation threatens less than the
   [[Attacker]] can see (S4)

## Recommendation

1. Keep the **Attack power**, with a [[Boost]] sentence and another
   name, e.g. **Strike**
2. Keep [[Supporter]]s at stake
3. Keep the rout at more than 2× the loser's total, with the winner's
   choice
4. Measure the winner's damage in totals: the loser's total −
   **Advantage**, which meets the rout at 2×
5. Decide the fate of a [[Damaged]] [[Hand]] [[Supporter]]: of the
   three options, [[Destroy]]ed is the simplest and symmetric
6. Replay S1, S4, S6, S8 and S10 with 4 and 5 before any rule changes

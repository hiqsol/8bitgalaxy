# Deferred diagnostic

[[Diagnostic]] questions on extensions and `advanced` rules that the
[[Duel]] doesn't need. They wait until the [[Duel]] is clear.

1. Answer and close them by the rules of [[Diagnostic]]
2. A question moves back to [[Diagnostic]] when its rule joins the
   duel

1. Contest is even, like in Chess:
	1. [[Asymmetric]] [[Faction]] means [[Asymmetric]] deck
	2. For an even contest players get even decks: [[Symmetric]]
	   [[Faction]]s
2. Playing a [[Card]] from [[Field]] is stronger than from [[Hand]]:
	1. A [[Field]] [[Card]] takes about twice the effort: [[Takeoff]]
	   and then a **starter** from [[Hand]]
	2. So a [[Field]] [[Card]] as a [[Direct participant]] uses all
	   [[Ability|Abilities]] and its `+Y` [[Power]] if printed, a
	   [[Hand]] [[Card]] only one, see [[PerformAction]] and
	   [[PrimaryAbility]]
	3. No other bonuses for playing from [[Hand]]
3. Taking [[Card]]s to [[Field]] thins the deck, that's intended: no
   scrap [[Technology]] needed
4. [[Field]] space is limited to [[Slot]]s, no unlimited parking:
	1. [[Space]] pressure is good: it pushes to expand
	2. It limits deck thinning: [[Field]] can't hold all weak
	   [[Card]]s
5. Limited free [[Restore]] per [[Turn]], no free return from
   [[Field]] to the deck
6. No limit on [[Action]]s from the same [[Star]] in a [[Turn]]

#### Notes

1. Pace: about 5 [[Action]]s per [[Turn]], [[Hand]] size is the main
   knob, 4 is an option

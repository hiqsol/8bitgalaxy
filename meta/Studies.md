# Studies

**Studies** made for the designer's decisions. A study page lives
until its decisions are applied, then it shrinks to an entry here: the
question, the method, the numbers that decided it, the decision. The
full text stays in git, the commit is given.

## Combat

[[Combat]] resolution in a [[Duel]], 2026-11-07, full text in
a837f3dc: a simulator of 18 variants on 400 mid-game setups, the war
economy of those setups against history and other games, and ten
situations played twice by subagents. [[Cost]]s are in HC, one
[[Hand]] [[Card]].

1. **The old rules were exploitable**: 28% of [[Attack]]s were lost
   and still paid, a narrow [[Defender]] win [[Damaged]] its own
   garrison (I81)
2. **Margin and rout won**: the loser is [[Damaged]], the winner pays
   the loser's total − **Advantage**, a crushing win [[Destroy]]s.
   Profitable losses fell from 28% to 8%, the [[Attacker]] won 61%
   instead of 40%, 94% of fights had a story event: a pyrrhic win, a
   rout, an upset
3. **Count in Combat power, not Levels**: with [[Supporter]]s at stake
   a loser dumped 0-power [[Card]]s to raise the winner's damage
   bill. In **Combat power** a 0-power [[Card]] adds nothing, and the
   winner's damage reaches 0 exactly where the **Rout** at twice the
   loser's begins
4. **The odds curve** has the shape of a wargame table: hopeless
   [[Attack]]s are routed (−4.9 HC), even ones bleed both sides, over
   3:2 they win cheaply (+6 to +7 HC), break-even at 1:1. A chosen
   [[Attack]] returns 68% over building, 2 setups in 3 offer one
5. **A battle costs both sides about a [[Turn]]**, 8.9 HC together: in
   a [[Duel]] a slow transfer to the stronger side, with 3+ players a
   gift to the bystander, not measured
6. **Defense lives on the [[Field]]**: a [[Ship]] attacks with half
   its [[Level]] and defends with all of it. A [[Hand]] of 5 attacks
   with 3.1 and defends with 3.5, its [[Military]] [[Card]]s only
7. **Repair**: [[Recover]] at half the [[Price]] costs 56-64% of a
   rebuild, the starter [[Card]] is most of it
8. **Technologies**: a retreating [[Defender]] left the [[Attacker]]
   0-3% wins; [[Ambush]] cut [[Attack]]s from 61% to 51%; [[Salvage]]
   for a side standing in the [[Star]] doesn't feed the exploit;
   [[ForceDiscard]] became a blockade, the rival discards a [[Card]]
   that defends with 0
9. **Applied**: [[Combat#9]]-12, the margin and **Rout** in **Combat
   power**, the winner of a **Rout** may keep a [[Hero]] [[Damaged]],
   a captive; a retreat loses as by a **Rout**, see [[Retreat]]; no
   move, not an empty [[Hand]], makes a [[Faction]] [[Fallen]]
10. **Rejected**: a reserve capped by the garrison, a withdraw for
    every side, a repair yard that [[Recover]]s a whole [[Star]]
11. **Watch in playtest**: [[Attack]]s per game, the model expects one
    in 2 opportunities of 3

## Deck

The TBD [[End]]s of the [[Card]]s 6/5 and 8/7 and the
[[Hyperstructure]] Primaries, 2026-11-07, full text in 56c470f9: four
variants in 8 games played by hand, full games and **Act II** games
from a mirrored mid-game. Applied: Crossroads with the snowball fix,
see [[Deck]]; what to watch is I19 in [[Backlog]].

1. **A wonder changing hands is the best story**: a
   [[Hyperstructure]] works for whoever possesses it, see
   [[Hyperstructure#11]]
2. **The wonder snowball** decides too many games: each
   [[Hyperstructure]]'s Secondary of +5 to +8 [[Power]] pays for the
   next, [[StellarForge]] pays both halves; [[KardashevAscension]] in
   Act II [[Turn]]s 9-11, in 3 games of 3 that tried
3. **Dyson Collector**, +1 [[Energy]] per [[Ready]] [[Structure]] in
   its [[Star]], breaks the economy; capped at +2 it is fine
4. **[[UnifiedTheory]]** in place of the [[Military]] [[Crown]] makes
   [[Singularity]] too easy
5. **Specialists never reach [[Level]] 6** when it needs [[Level]] 4
   of its [[Class]] and 3 of another; with [[Level]] 4 of its own
   [[Class]] only they do by [[Turn]] 8-11
6. **[[Military]] [[Level]] 6+ [[Card]]s make the best fights**:
   [[Boarding]], Sabotage, [[Siege]]; the [[Ship]] 8M is the late
   hammer
7. **[[Culture]] has no way in** without a wider [[Envoy]]: every
   [[GalacticDiaspora]] stalled at 5 of 7 [[Star]]s
8. **Cheap 2/1 [[Hero]]es** make "no leader" unreachable,
   [[Decapitation]] is never worth it
9. **Applied, Crossroads**: 4 [[Tool]]s, [[Hyperstructure]]s with
   [[ExtraPrice]] 0 (11 and 15), [[Prerequisites]] of a [[Level]] 6
   [[Card]] only [[Level]] 4 of its own [[Class]], a [[Technology]] on
   both [[End]]s of a [[Level]] 6+ [[Actor]], [[UnifiedTheory]] for
   [[Prerequisites]] only, [[Envoy]] widened to its [[Action]], the
   capped [[DysonCollector]], and the **snowball fix**: a
   [[Hyperstructure]]'s Secondary gives [[Energy]], +1 on a 6/5, +2 on
   an 8/7. New: [[Siege]], [[Boarding]], [[Defection]],
   [[DysonCollector]]; the cost of [[Defection]] is not from the
   study

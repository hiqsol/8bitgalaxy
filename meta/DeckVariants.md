# Deck variants

Four variants to finish the [[Duel]] [[Deck]], the TBD [[End]]s and
the [[Hyperstructure]] Primaries, tested in 8 games played by hand,
2026-11-07, under the [[Combat]] rules of a837f3dc. Ranked by the
stories the games told and the decisions they asked for. Nothing is
applied to the rules.

How the games were played: one person plays both sides, by hand, under
the rules of the day. Python only shuffles and draws: [[Space bag]],
[[World]] deck, [[Ancient]] deck, and from game 2 on the [[Hand]]s,
drawn at random from the [[Unit]]s not on the [[Field]]. Full games
start by [[StartDraw]]. **Act II** games start from a mirrored
mid-game: both players hold [[Level]] 4 engines in two [[Class]]es, so
the [[Level]] 6 and 8 [[Card]]s come within a few [[Turn]]s. The full
games show the pace, the Act II games the late [[Card]]s. Quiet
stretches are abridged to one checked line a [[Turn]]. Four games were
stopped and adjudicated; the table says which. The logs are in the
session scratchpad, `deck-loop/games/`.

## Summary

1. **Every game's best moment was a wonder changing hands**: the Great
   Library of game 1 had three owners in four [[Turn]]s, and the
   [[WarEconomy|War Economy]] of game 4 was taken as a wreck and Fixed
   by the captor. A [[Hyperstructure]] that works for whoever
   possesses it ([[Hyperstructure#11]]) is the strongest story engine
   in the [[Deck]]. Keep it
2. **The wonder snowball decides too many games**: each
   [[Hyperstructure]]'s Secondary (+5 to +8 [[Power]]) pays for the
   next, and [[StellarForge]] lets [[Production]] pay both halves. A
   builder reached [[KardashevAscension]] in Act II [[Turn]]s 9-11 in
   three of three games where one tried, and nobody could stop it
3. **The Dyson Collector (+1 [[Energy]] per Ready [[Structure]] in its
   [[Star]]) breaks the economy**: +4-5 a [[Turn]], at the [[Token]]
   cap by [[Turn]] 10, and whoever builds it first leads (games 3, 5,
   8). Capped at +2 (V4) it is fine
4. **[[UnifiedTheory]] removes the [[Military]] [[Crown]] from
   [[Singularity]]** (game 1): the [[Science]] player never needs the
   [[Ship]] 8M. In V4 it counts for [[Prerequisites]] only
5. **Specialists never reached Level 6** under today's
   [[Prerequisites]] (game 2): a [[Level]] 6 card asks for [[Level]] 4
   of its [[Class]] and [[Level]] 3 of another, so the Warlord had no
   [[Science]] and the Diplomat no [[Military]]. With "[[Level]] 4 of
   its own [[Class]]" (V4) both reach their [[Level]] 6 cards by
   [[Turn]] 8-11 (game 6)
6. **The cheap [[Hero]] line is fast** in every variant: a [[Grow]]
   lands on the [[Weak]] [[End]] of the bigger [[Card]], a 1-[[Power]]
   [[Rotate]] brings the [[Strong]] one. A [[Level]] 6 [[Hero]] by
   [[Turn]] 10 of a full game, a [[Crown]] by [[Turn]] 22
7. **The [[Military]] Level 6+ cards make the best fights**: Boarding
   (game 4) took two rival [[Ship]]s into the winner's deck, Sabotage
   delayed a rival economy by a [[Turn]], Siege broke a home [[Star]].
   [[Ship]] 8M is the Act II hammer: [[Range]] 8, attacks 4, defends 8
8. **The [[Culture]] path has no way in** without [[Envoy]]: every
   [[GalacticDiaspora]] attempt stalled at 5 of 7 [[Star]]s against
   [[Intercept]]s paid with open [[Energy]] (games 2, 3, 7).
   [[Heritage]] gave the best last [[Turn]] of all games (game 5)
9. **Recommendation: V4 Crossroads plus the snowball fix** (below):
   the V4 [[Prerequisites]], the [[Military]] and rivalry cards on the
   [[Level]] 6 [[Actor]]s, the capped Dyson, and a [[Hyperstructure]]
   Secondary paid in [[Energy]] instead of [[Power]]. The snowball fix
   is untested

### V3 Lean

The V1 [[Technology|Technologies]], restructured for pace and choices.

1. 4 [[Tool]]s, one per [[Class]]: [[Reserve]] 14, [[Deck]] 52

   11 for a 6/5, 8 + 7 = 15 for an 8/7, instead of 13 and 17

## Stories

1. **The Great Library of 01** (game 1, V1): the Architect has no free
   [[Slot]] at home, so the wonder goes to the front [[Star]]. The
   Warlord storms it and captures the wreck. The Architect doesn't
   retake it at once: the Warlord's S4M at home reaches 01 as a remote
   [[Support]] [[Group]]. The Warlord [[Heal]]s the wreck and owns the
   rival's Library; the Architect storms back the same round, and the
   healed wonder, [[Damaged]] again, is [[Destroy]]ed. Built,
   captured, healed, razed
2. **The captive [[Hero]]** (game 1): the Warlord [[Heal]]s its own
   captured [[Hero]] inside the rival's [[Star]], and the [[Star]]
   turns [[Disputed]] under the captor
3. **The Battle of 20** (game 4, V2): the Warlord's whole army against
   the Scholar's front [[Star]] and its [[WarEconomy|War Economy]].
   The boosts go on the [[Ship]] the Warlord means to lose, so the
   [[Hero]] and the [[Crown]] live. Boarding turns a rival [[Ship]]
   into its own. The next [[Turn]] the Warlord [[Fix]]es the captured
   wonder and possesses it
4. **The tokens on the table** (game 4): the Scholar's 4 open
   [[Energy]] hold the home [[Star]] for two [[Turn]]s. The Scholar
   spends them on a wonder, and the home falls the next [[Turn]]:
   [[PaxGalactica]] by the last [[Star]]
5. **The Heritage gambit** (game 5, V1): after the rival's
   [[KardashevAscension]], the Builder's last [[Turn]] sends every
   [[Ship]] into the rival's [[Star]]s, knowing [[Heritage]] counts
   the wrecks. A tie holds a [[Ship]] in the rival's home, and a Rout
   at the armed front [[Star]] ends the [[GalacticDiaspora]]
6. **Wonders eat their cities** (game 3, V3): with both [[Star]]s full
   by [[Turn]] 11, every new wonder replaces a [[Base]] or a
   [[Colony]] by [[Recycle]]: tear down the trade post to raise a
   Forge
7. **The land grab and the Void wall** (game 3): the Expansionist
   claims [[Star]]s with lone [[Ship]]s, so a rival [[Structure]]
   built there would be captured at once, and lays a [[Void]] on
   purpose to wall the rival in
8. **The ambassador seized** (game 6, V4): the Sage's [[Crown]]
   [[Hero]] walks into the rival's front [[Star]] as an [[Envoy]],
   safe from [[Intercept]], and is captured there by a plain
   [[Attack]]
9. **Colonies under a foreign flag** (game 7, V4): a [[Colony]] raised
   into the rival's [[Star]] with the [[Envoy]]'s help is captured the
   moment it lands, and still counts for its [[Faction]]'s
   [[GalacticDiaspora]]

## Decisions the games asked for

1. Where to stake the boosts: on the [[Card]]s the player plans to
   lose, because the winner's loss counts boosted [[Combat power]]
   (games 1, 4, 8)
2. [[Attack]] or not, reading the rival's [[Field]], its remote
   [[Support]] [[Group]]s and its open [[Energy]] (games 1, 2, 3, 4):
   visible [[Token]]s work as a deterrent
3. Spend the [[Energy]] on a wonder or keep it to defend (game 4,
   decisive)
4. Where to build a wonder: a full home, a safe side [[Star]] or an
   armed front [[Star]] (games 1, 5, 7)
5. Which [[Card]]s join a conquest: a [[Level]] 2 [[Card]] of a
   [[Class]] meets a [[World]]'s [[Prerequisites]] and brings 5
   [[Ancient]]s instead of 3 (games 2, 6)
6. A [[Field]] [[Unit]] used for [[Prerequisites]] goes to the
   [[Discard]]: research the [[Crown]] now and lose its [[Level]] 6
   [[Card]] for a while, or wait (games 1, 8)
7. [[Destroy]] captured [[Hero]] wrecks (dead for Pax, and only a
   [[Grow]] brings them back) or keep them as hostages (games 1, 4)
8. Which lesser [[Structure]] to [[Recycle]] for a wonder [[Slot]]
   (games 3, 7)
9. Lay the drawn [[Void]] as a wall, or fail the [[Explore]] on
   purpose to keep the [[Space]] hidden (games 2, 3)

## Recommendation

V4 Crossroads with one more fix, the **snowball fix**: a
[[Hyperstructure]]'s Secondary gives [[Energy]], not [[Power]]: +1
[[Energy]] on a 6/5, +2 on an 8/7, [[Strong]] [[End]] only. The
wonders still pay, but through the capped [[Token]]s, which every
player sees.
Untested; the next playtest should be V4 with this fix, Act II,
Industrialist against Warlord.

## Open questions

- **Q1. The snowball**: how should the wonders stop paying for the
  next one?
	- a) The Secondary in [[Energy]] (recommended above)
	- b) A [[Hyperstructure]]'s [[Power]] can't pay the [[Create]] of
	  a [[Hyperstructure]]
	- c) [[StellarForge]] needs an own [[Academy]] in its [[Star]]
	- d) [[KardashevAscension]] counts only [[Ready]]
	  [[Hyperstructure]]s at **Check**
- **Q2. Prerequisites**: is "[[Level]] 4 of its own [[Class]]" for a
  [[Level]] 6 [[Card]] (V4) acceptable, or should the specialists stay
  locked out?
- **Q3. Envoy**: one entry a [[Turn]] isn't enough for
  [[GalacticDiaspora]]. Options:
	- a) Its whole moving [[Group]] (V4)
	- b) The [[Envoy]] [[Card]] can't be attacked while it gives no
	  [[Control]]
	- c) [[GalacticDiaspora]] needs 6 of 7 [[Star]]s
- **Q4. Tools**: 4 [[Tool]]s (V3, V4) shrink a builder's cycling pool
  to 5-7 [[Card]]s by [[Turn]] 16: its [[Hand]]s repeat. Keep 8, or 6?
- **Q5. Siege**: is the tuned Siege ([[Depleted]] [[Structure]]s
  defend 0) too much or too little? Untested after the tuning
- **Q6. Pax**: the cheap 2/1 [[Hero]]es make "no leader" unreachable,
  [[Decapitation]] is never worth it. Should a [[Faction]] fall by
  losing its home [[Star]] instead, or should raised [[Hero]]es cost
  more?

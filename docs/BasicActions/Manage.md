1. **Manage** is [[BasicAction]] of any [[Class]]
2. **Manage** changes cards:
	1. **Rotate**: to the other [[End]]
	2. **Shift**:
		1. A [[Structure]] to another [[Slot]] of its [[IPS]]
		2. A card to another pile of its [[Laboratory]] or [[Factory]]
		   row, directed to a [[Hub]] that takes it
3. **Cost**: 1 [[Power]] of the starter's [[Class]] per card
4. **Manage** works only on cards the starter's Class handles, see
   [[Class]]

1. **Research** is [[Science]] [[BasicAction]]
2. **Research** allows to move a card:
	1. From [[Laboratory]] to [[Factory]]
	2. Between piles of own [[Laboratory]], like [[Manage]] Shift: the
	   player has options, the price is set by [[Laboratory]]
3. Similar to [[Manage]] action
4. **Direction**: any [[Star]] to corresponding [[Hub]]
5. **Requirements**: to [[Factory]], all [[Prerequisites]] of the card
   are provided by the action's participants
6. **Cost**: the card's [[Price]] of [[SciencePower]] to [[Factory]]

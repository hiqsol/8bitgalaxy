1. **Move** is [[BasicAction]]
2. **Move** allows cards that move on their own, [[Range]] above 0, to change
   place:
	1. Within one [[Star]] system: from one [[IPS]] slot to other
	2. From one [[Star]] to another
3. Can't finish in [[Space]]
4. **Requirements**: [[Range]] only, no [[Power]]: the [[Distance]] to the
   destination must be within the moving group's Range
	1. Range can be increased with [[Support]]
5. Destination [[IPS]] must have a free [[Slot]] for every moving card
6. Several cards can move together in group, its [[Range]] is the sum, see
   [[Range]]
7. Moving cards are not depleted after **Move**, except the starter, see
   [[Deplete]]
8. The route obeys [[Pinning]]

#### Notes

1. [[Actor]]s move on their own, a **Move** costs only its starter
2. [[Structure]]s have Range 0: they change Slots with [[Manage]] Shift and go
   to other Stars by [[Transport]]

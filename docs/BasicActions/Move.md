1. **Move** is [[BasicAction]]
2. **Move** allows cards that move on their own, [[Range]] above 0, to change
   place:
	1. Within one [[Star]] system: from one [[IPS]] slot to other
	2. From one [[Star]] to another
3. Can't finish in [[Space]]
4. **Requirements**: [[Range]] only, no [[Power]]: the [[Distance]] to the
   destination must be within the moving group's Range
	1. Range can be increased with [[Support]]
5. Several cards can move together in group, its [[Range]] is the sum, see
   [[Range]]
6. [[Ship]]s of the group carry **cargo**: cards and [[Materials]]
	1. The total [[Size]] of cargo is within the group's total [[Capacity]]
	2. A [[Hero]] adds Capacity, but cargo needs a Ship in the group
	3. Every cargo card needs a Ship in the group whose [[Class]] carries it:
	   [[Military]] carries [[Actor]]s, [[Culture]] [[Social]] cards,
	   [[Production]] [[Industrial]] cards, [[Science]] anything
	4. Any Ship carries [[Hero]]es and [[Materials]]
	5. Cargo is a target, not a participant
7. Destination [[IPS]] must have a free [[Slot]] for every moving and carried
   card
8. After **Move**: see [[Deplete]]
9. The route obeys [[Pinning]]

#### Notes

1. [[Actor]]s move on their own, a **Move** costs only its starter
2. [[Structure]]s have Range 0: they change Slots with [[Manage]] Shift or go as
   cargo, both cost a starter: the player has options
3. E.g. a [[Colony]] is carried only by Culture and Science Ships: colony ships

1. **Energy** is stockable [[Resource]]
2. **Energy** is a token in [[Hand]], the same token in a [[Star]] is
   [[Materials]], see [[Component]]
3. Obtained from [[Star]] ability, by [[Export]]
4. Spent globally from [[Hand]] or [[Field]] to [[Boost]] any action
5. Tokens are not cards: they don't count to the [[Hand]] size and
   stay in Hand at **Draw** phase of [[Turn]]
6. Tokens in Hand lie open beside the cards: every player sees them
7. [[Hand]] holds at most as many tokens as the total [[Capacity]] of
   the player's cards with a [[Hub]] on the [[Field]]: tokens above
   it are lost

#### Notes

1. Warp gives huge amounts of energy, though not limitless, see
   [[Lore]]

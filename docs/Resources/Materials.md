1. **Materials** is stockable [[Resource]]
2. **Materials** is a token in a [[Star]], the same token in [[Hand]]
   is [[Energy]], see [[Component]]
3. Stored at [[Star]] or card
4. Carried as cargo of a [[Move]] by any [[Ship]]
5. Can be used to [[Boost]] action
6. Spent locally - can be added to action starting or finishing where
   it is stored
7. Card can not add more **materials** than its [[Capacity]]
8. **Star Capacity** is the sum of the [[Capacity|Capacities]] of the
   [[Structure]]s in the Star:
	1. A Star can't hold more **materials** than its Capacity
	2. When the Star Capacity drops below its **materials**, the
	   excess is lost
9. [[Takeoff]] can put [[Energy]] tokens from [[Hand]] into a Star
   [[ControlledBy]] the player instead of a card, up to the Star
   Capacity: they become **materials** and take no [[Slot]]

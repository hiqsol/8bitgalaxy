1. Each **turn** player plays cards from [[Hand]] one by one, in any order. Each played card either:
	1. Goes to [[Field]] with [[Takeoff]] or
	2. Starts an [[Action]] as its **starter**, see [[PerformAction]]
2. More cards from [[Hand]] can join an action as [[Support]]ers
3. One [[AlienSpawn]] at any moment of the **turn**
4. **End of turn**:
	1. Unused cards from [[Hand]] go to [[Discard]]
	2. [[Draw]] a full [[Hand]]
5. Player can pass: play no cards

#### Notes

1. The [[Hand]] is the budget of the **turn**: no action counting and no checks
2. Cards drawn at the end of the turn stay in [[Hand]] during the rivals' turns, so they can be used for defense in [[Combat]]. Cards used for defense are missing at your own turn.

1. Player represents one of the galaxy [[Faction]]s.
2. A [[Faction]]'s **goal** has four paths, one per [[Class]]
3. [[Military]] victory is immediate: a faction wins when every rival
   is over, see rule 7
4. The three equal non-Military achievements are:
	1. [[Culture]]: [[GalacticDiaspora|Galactic Diaspora]]
	2. [[Production]]: possesses at least 7 [[Hyperstructure]]s
	3. [[Science]]: [[Singularity]]
5. At **Check** of own [[Turn]], having a non-Military achievement
   triggers [[Twilight]]
6. At the end of a Goal-triggered Twilight, compare the current state:
	1. The faction with the most non-Military achievements wins
	2. If tied, the faction with the most [[VictoryPoints]] wins
	3. A further tie is a draw
7. A [[Faction]] is over when one of:
	1. **Can not make a move**, see [[Turn]]
	2. **Has no leader**: at **Check** phase of own [[Turn]] every
	   [[Hero]] of its faction deck is [[Damaged]], in its [[Factory]]
	   or in its [[Laboratory]]

#### Notes

1. Four paths like the four victories of Civilization
2. The three non-Military achievements are equal, but reaching more
   than one gives an advantage at Twilight
3. Achievements are checked again at the end of Twilight: a rival can
   break one during the last Turn
4. Losing all [[Hero]]es is inspired by checkmate in Chess. Like
   check: a player whose last Hero got [[Damaged]] has one turn to
   get a Hero back
5. The leader check counts the dead Heroes: the others are alive
   wherever they are, nobody reveals a Hand, the Reserve or a
   [[Hidden]] card
6. Losing the home [[Star]] is a heavy loss by intention, a goal for
   rivals, but it doesn't end the game, neither does losing the
   [[Hub|hubs]]: the faction still moves and can defeat the rival
   leader. The captor gets the hubs, like taking the queen in Chess
7. Losing the last Star does end the game: a faction with no [[Star]]
   [[ActiveFor|active]] for it can not make a move, it has no place
   for [[Takeoff]] and no source for an action from [[Hand]]
8. An empty [[Hand]] at the start of own [[Turn]] can end the game
   too, like an unexpected checkmate: spending the whole Hand on
   defense in [[Combat]] is a real risk

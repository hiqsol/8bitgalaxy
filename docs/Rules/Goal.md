1. Player represents one of the galaxy [[Faction]]s.
2. [[Faction]]'s **goal** is to become the greatest in the galaxy, which means:
	1. Survive and save leaders
	2. Populate and expand
	3. Prosper and develop
3. A [[Faction]] wins at the end of own [[Turn]] by one of four paths, one per [[Class]]:
	1. [[Military]]: every rival **has no leader**
	2. [[Culture]]: total [[Level]] of own [[Colony]] cards on the [[Field]] reaches the threshold of the [[Scenario]]
	3. [[Production]]: TBD
	4. [[Science]]: for every [[Class]], has [[Research]]ed a card with an [[End]] of that Class and the highest [[Level]], 8
4. A [[Faction]] is over when one of:
	1. **Can not make a move**: at the start of own [[Turn]] can make neither a [[Takeoff]] nor an [[Action]]
	2. **Has no leader**: at the end of own [[Turn]] has no [[Enabled]] [[Hero]] on [[Field]], [[Hand]], [[Reserve]] or [[Discard]]
5. The last [[Faction]] that is not over wins
6. [[Faction]]s greatness can be compared by:
	1. Population
	2. Expansion
	3. Wealth
	4. Development
7. With [[Objective]]s extension the first to get enough victory points wins

#### Notes

1. Four paths like the four victories of Civilization
2. Losing all [[Hero]]es is inspired by checkmate in Chess:
	1. Like check: a player whose last Hero got [[Damaged]] has one turn to [[Recover]] it
	2. Heroes in the deck are reached by attacking the [[Academy]], see [[Combat]]
3. Losing the home [[Star]] or the [[BuildingFacility|building facilities]] doesn't end the game: the faction still moves and can defeat the rival leader
4. Losing all [[Actor]]s doesn't end the game either: a [[Field]] card with [[BeActor]] starts actions without them
5. A faction that controls no [[Star]] can not make a move, see [[Takeoff]]

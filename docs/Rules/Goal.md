1. Player represents one of the galaxy [[Faction]]s.
2. Game **goal** is optional. In a duel the goal is to defeat the rival.
3. [[Faction]]'s **goal** is to become the greatest in the galaxy, which means:
	1. Survive and save leaders
	2. Populate and expand
	3. Prosper and develop
4. A [[Faction]] is over when one of:
	1. **Can not make a move**: at the start of own [[Turn]] can make neither a [[Takeoff]] nor an [[Action]]
	2. **Has no leader**: at the end of own [[Turn]] has no [[Enabled]] [[Hero]] on [[Field]], [[Hand]], [[Reserve]] or [[Discard]]
5. [[Faction]]s greatness can be compared by:
	1. Population
	2. Expansion
	3. Wealth
	4. Development
6. With [[Objective]]s extension the first to get enough victory points wins

#### Notes

1. Losing all [[Hero]]es is inspired by checkmate in Chess:
	1. Like check: a player whose last Hero got [[Damaged]] has one turn to [[Recover]] it
2. Losing the home [[Star]] or the [[BuildingFacility|building facilities]] doesn't end the game: the faction still moves and can defeat the rival leader
3. A faction that controls no [[Star]] can not make a move, see [[Takeoff]]

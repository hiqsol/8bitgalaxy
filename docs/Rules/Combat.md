1. **Combat** starts when player performs [[Attack]] action
2. First **attacker**:
	1. selects **attacking participants** and supporters
	2. selects **defending participants**: direct participants from
	   the defender's cards in the target [[Star]]
	3. When the target [[Star]] has an [[Academy]] [[ControlledBy]]
	   the defender, defending participants can also be selected from
	   the defender's [[Discard]], [[Reserve]] or [[Hand]]
3. Then **defender**:
	1. Chooses to pass or add more participants and supporters
4. More players can join the **combat**:
	1. On any side
	2. They must have active [[Military]] [[Actor]]s in the Star
5. Parties can bluff by adding [[Hidden]] cards from [[Hand]], also at
   rival's [[Turn]]
	1. Hidden card can be revealed by its player at any time
	2. Player can reveal rival's hidden cards with [[Explore]]
6. Parties can [[Boost]] with cards at the moment when they add the
   card, not later
7. At following rounds parties continue to pass or add more
   participants and supporters
8. After all parties have passed reveal hidden cards, count total
   powers and determine winner:
	1. An attacking card adds its [[AttackPower]]
	2. A defending card adds its [[Defense]]
	3. A tie goes to the defender
	4. **Advantage** = winner total − loser total
9. After action:
	1. Winner can save from damage own direct participants with total
	   sum of levels **not more** than the advantage
	2. All other direct participants become [[Damaged]].
	3. If the attacker wins, their surviving attacking [[Unit]]s move
	   to free [[Slot]]s of the destination [[Star]], keeping their
	   [[End]]. Units that don't fit stay in the source.
	4. Then [[Deplete]]

#### Notes

1. Combat has no random resolution, but hidden commitments keep the
   result from being predetermined.
2. Multiple rounds make [[Hidden]] cards and [[Explore]] relevant.
3. Players make decisions, take risks, prepare surprises and bluff.
4. Attacking the [[Academy]] reaches [[Hero]]es in the deck, so
   leaders can be defeated, see [[Goal]]
5. Direct participants hold ground like chess pieces: survivors stay
   or move in
6. Both sides can add any cards, but a non-[[Military]] card has no
   [[AttackPower]]: it attacks with 0, though it can [[Boost]], and
   defends with 1, see [[Defense]]. It joins an attack for its
   technologies, Range and Capacity, or to move in

1. **Takeoff** is one of two ways to play a card from [[Hand]] in a [[Turn]],
   the other is to start an [[Action]]
2. **Takeoff** puts **ONE** [[Unit]] card from [[Hand]] to a free [[Unit]]
   [[Slot]] in the [[IPS]] of a [[Star]] [[ControlledBy]] the player
3. Player chooses the [[End]] the card is placed with, for free, including the
   [[Hidden]] end
4. **Takeoff** has no cost and is not an [[Action]]: the card does nothing else
   in this turn

#### Notes

1. Free slots are limited, that creates pressure to expand
2. On [[Field]] the card can be a direct participant of actions and use all of
   its abilities, from [[Hand]] it uses only one, see [[PerformAction]]
3. Player who controls no [[Star]] can neither **Takeoff** nor start an action
   from [[Hand]], and is over, see [[Goal]]

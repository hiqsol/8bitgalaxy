1. **Takeoff** is one of two ways to play a card from [[Hand]] in a [[Turn]], the other is to start an [[Action]]
2. **Takeoff** puts **ONE** [[Unit]] card from [[Hand]] to a free [[Unit]] [[Slot]] in the [[IPS]] of a [[Star]] [[ControlledBy]] the player
3. Owner chooses the [[End]] the card is placed with, for free
4. **Takeoff** has no cost and is not an [[Action]]: the card does nothing else in this turn

#### Notes

1. Free slots are limited, that creates pressure to expand
2. On [[Field]] the card is a direct participant of actions and uses all of its abilities, from [[Hand]] it uses only one, see [[PerformAction]]
3. Player who controls no [[Star]] can neither **Takeoff** nor start an action

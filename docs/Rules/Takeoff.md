1. **Takeoff** plays **ONE** [[Unit]] card from [[Hand]] into a
   [[Star]] [[ActiveFor]] the player, see [[Turn]]
2. Player chooses the [[End]] the card is placed with
3. **Takeoff** has no cost and is not an [[Action]]: the card can act
   in the same turn

#### Notes

1. Acting in the same turn means nobody has to remember which cards
   took off

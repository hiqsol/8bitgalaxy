1. **Deck building**: [[Unit]] cards cycle between [[Discard]],
   [[Reserve]] and [[Hand]], through the [[Field]]:
	1. Appear in [[Discard]]
	2. **Shuffle** to [[Reserve]]
	3. **Draw** to [[Hand]]
	4. Play from [[Hand]]:
		1. [[Takeoff]] to [[Field]]
		2. Or start or support an action and [[Deplete]] to
		   [[Discard]]
	5. Unused cards go from [[Hand]] to [[Discard]] at **Draw** phase
	   of [[Turn]]
	6. From [[Field]] to [[Discard]] after an action, see [[Deplete]]
2. **Draw** takes cards from the top of [[Reserve]] to [[Hand]]
3. **Shuffle**, only when a card must be drawn from an empty
   [[Reserve]]: all cards of [[Discard]] are shuffled into a new
   Reserve, then the **Draw** continues

#### Notes

1. Units form the cycling deck, [[Structure]]s form the persistent
   engine on the Field

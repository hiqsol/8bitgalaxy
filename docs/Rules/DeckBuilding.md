1. **Deck building**: [[Unit]] cards cycle between [[Discard]],
   [[Reserve]] and [[Hand]], through the [[Field]]:
	1. Appear in [[Discard]]
	2. [[Shuffle]] to [[Reserve]]
	3. [[Draw]] to [[Hand]] at the end of [[Turn]]
	4. Play from [[Hand]]:
		1. [[Takeoff]] to [[Field]]
		2. Or start or support an action and [[Deplete]] to
		   [[Discard]]
	5. Unused cards go from [[Hand]] to [[Discard]] at the end of
	   [[Turn]]
	6. From [[Field]] [[Deplete]] to [[Discard]] after an action,
	   exceptions are in [[Deplete]]

#### Notes

1. Units form the cycling deck, [[Structure]]s form the persistent
   engine on the Field

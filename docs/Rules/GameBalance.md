1. Contest is even, like in Chess:
	1. Asymmetric [[Faction]] means asymmetric deck
	2. For an even contest players get even decks
2. Playing a card from [[Field]] is stronger than from [[Hand]]:
	1. A Field card takes about twice the effort: [[Takeoff]] and
	   then a **starter** from [[Hand]]
	2. So a Field card as a direct participant uses all abilities
	   and its `+Y` power if printed, a Hand card only one, see
	   [[PerformAction]] and [[PrimaryAbility]]
	3. No other bonuses for playing from [[Hand]]
3. Taking cards to [[Field]] thins the deck, that's intended: no scrap
   technology needed
4. Field space is limited to [[Slot]]s, no unlimited parking:
	1. Space pressure is good: it pushes to expand
	2. It limits deck thinning: [[Field]] can't hold all weak cards
5. Limited free [[Restore]] per [[Turn]], no free return from
   [[Field]] to the deck
6. No limit on actions from the same [[Star]] in a [[Turn]]

#### Notes

1. Pace: about 5 actions per turn, [[Hand]] size is the main knob, 4
   is an option

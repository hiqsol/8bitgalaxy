1. **Action** is a part of [[Turn]]
2. **Action** is started by a **starter**: a card from [[Hand]] or a [[Field]]
   card with [[Starter]], see [[PerformAction]]
3. **Actions** come in two flavours:
	1. [[BasicAction]]s are available at any time
	2. [[TechnologyAction]]s need a participant that provides the [[Technology]]

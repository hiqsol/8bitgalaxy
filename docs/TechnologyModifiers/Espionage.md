1. **Espionage** is [[Science]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. With **Espionage** an action can use a [[Technology]] of a rival
   [[Enabled]] card in the [[Star]] of the card with **Espionage**, as
   if a participant provided it
3. A [[TechnologyAction]] used so pays its Cost in [[SciencePower]]

#### Notes

1. The rival card is not a participant: it doesn't [[Deplete]]

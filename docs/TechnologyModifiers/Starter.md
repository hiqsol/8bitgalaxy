1. **Starter** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. **Starter** allows a card on [[Field]], not [[Depleted]], to be the **starter** of an [[Action]], see [[PerformAction]]:
	1. Its **source** is its own [[IPS]]
	2. It is a direct participant there and can use all of its abilities
	3. It [[Deplete]]s after the action, as any participant

#### Notes

1. A starter from [[Hand]] is only a supporter, so a Field starter is stronger: it saves a card from Hand and uses all of its abilities
2. Depleting limits it: an [[Actor]] goes to [[Discard]], a [[Structure]] waits for [[Restore]]

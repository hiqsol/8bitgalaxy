1. **Settle** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. A [[Ship]] with **Settle** converts itself into a [[Colony]]:
	1. [[Create]] puts a Colony in the [[Star]] of the Ship, no [[Academy]]
	   needed
	2. The Ship is a direct participant in the destination, the Star it is in
	3. [[Range]] is checked as usual, see [[PerformAction]]
	4. The Ship [[Deplete]]s to [[Discard]], the Colony takes a free
	   [[Structure]] [[Slot]] there

#### Notes

1. A colony ship: a Colony far from the Academy
2. The source is usually the same Star, at [[Distance]] 0, the Ship there gives
   control of it

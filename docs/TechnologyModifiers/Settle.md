1. **Settle** is [[Culture]] and [[Production]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. A [[Ship]] with **Settle** converts itself into a [[Structure]]:
	1. With a [[Culture]] [[End]]: into a [[Colony]]
	2. With a [[Production]] [[End]]: into a [[Base]]
3. [[Create]] puts the Structure in the [[Star]] of the Ship, no
   [[Hub]] needed:
	1. The Ship is a direct participant in the destination, the Star
	   it is in
	2. [[Range]] is checked as usual, see [[PerformAction]]
	3. The Ship [[Deplete]]s to [[Discard]], the Structure takes a
	   free [[Structure]] [[Slot]] there

#### Notes

1. A colony ship or a station kit: a Colony or a Base far from the
   [[Academy]] or the [[Shipyard]]
2. The source is usually the same Star, at [[Distance]] 0, the Ship
   there gives control of it

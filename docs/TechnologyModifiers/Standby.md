1. **Standby** is [[Science]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. **Standby** keeps one unused card in [[Hand]] at **Draw** phase of
   [[Turn]]: it doesn't go to [[Discard]], then the player draws up
   to a full Hand
3. Works from a [[Ready]] [[Structure]]: [[Restore]] comes after
   **Draw**, so the Structure stays unused in the Turn
4. A [[Faction]] keeps at most one card, whatever number of
   **Standby** it has

#### Notes

1. Not a card more: the Hand stays at its size. **Standby** saves a
   good card from a deck full of [[Tool]]s
2. A kept card can be lost to [[ForceDiscard]] or as a defender in
   [[Combat]], it counts for [[Singularity]] like any card in Hand

1. **Prefab** is [[Production]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. A [[Ship]] with **Prefab** converts itself into a [[Base]]:
	1. [[Create]] puts a Base in the [[Star]] of the Ship, no [[Shipyard]]
	   needed
	2. The Ship is a direct participant in the destination, the Star it is in
	3. [[Range]] is checked as usual, see [[PerformAction]]
	4. The Ship [[Deplete]]s to [[Discard]], the Base takes a free
	   [[Structure]] [[Slot]] there

#### Notes

1. A station kit: a Base far from the Shipyard, like [[Settle]] for Colonies

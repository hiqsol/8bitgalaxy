1. **Emulate** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. With **Emulate** an action can use a [[Technology]] printed on
   either [[End]] of the top card of a rival's [[Discard]], as if a
   participant provided it
3. A [[TechnologyAction]] used so pays its Cost in [[CulturePower]]

#### Notes

1. Like the Diplomat of Concordia: the face-up Discard shows what the
   rival just used
2. The rival's card is not a participant, it stays in the Discard

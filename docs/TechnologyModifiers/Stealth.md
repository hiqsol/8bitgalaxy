1. **Stealth** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. **Stealth** is provided by one card and puts another card into
   **stealth mode**: the other card becomes [[Hidden]]
3. A card in stealth mode moves as any [[Hidden]] card: [[Range]] 1,
   [[Capacity]] 1

#### Notes

1. Stealth makes hidden moves on the [[Field]] possible, [[Explore]] counters it

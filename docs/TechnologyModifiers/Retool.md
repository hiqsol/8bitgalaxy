1. **Retool** is [[Production]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. With **Retool** an **Enhance** [[Upgrade]] can replace a [[Tool]]
   on the [[Field]] with a [[Ship]]
3. The Tool leaves the game, as a destroyed Tool, instead of going to
   the [[Factory]]

#### Notes

1. Parked junk becomes a fleet: [[Takeoff]] parks a Tool, **Retool**
   turns it into a Ship at the usual Upgrade Cost

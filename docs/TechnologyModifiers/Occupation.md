1. **Occupation** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyModifier]]
2. An own [[Enabled]] [[Military]] [[Actor]] with **Occupation** gives
   its [[Faction]] a [[Control]] level above **Actor** in its
   [[Star]]: **Occupation**

#### Notes

1. A warship holds a Star against a lone [[Hero]] or a civil
   [[Ship]]: the Star is the occupier's, not [[Disputed]]
2. Two occupiers tie as usual: the Star is [[Disputed]]

1. **Standing** is a card [[State]] by position relative to a [[Faction]]
2. **Standing** card is an [[Enabled]] card [[ControlledBy]] the faction:
	1. On the [[Field]]
	2. In [[Hand]]
	3. In [[History]]
3. [[Damaged]] cards and cards in [[Reserve]], [[Discard]], [[Factory]] and [[Laboratory]] are not **standing**
4. Used by the [[Goal]] paths: [[CulturePoints]] and Singularity count **standing** cards

#### Notes

1. [[History]] cards are out of play but still standing: past greatness counts

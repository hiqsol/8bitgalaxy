1. **State** is a condition of a card or a [[Star]]
2. Card states shown by [[Side]] and [[End]]:
	1. [[Enabled]] - front Side up, for a [[Structure]] either
	   [[Ready]] or [[Depleted]]
	2. [[Damaged]] - back Side up, Damaged End up
	3. [[Hidden]] - back Side up, Hidden End up
3. Card states relative to a faction or player:
	1. [[ActiveFor]]
	2. [[ControlledBy]]
4. [[Star]] states:
	1. [[ControlledBy]]
	2. [[Disputed]]

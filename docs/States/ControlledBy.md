1. **Controlled by** is a [[Star]] and card [[State]] relative to a [[Faction]]
2. [[Star]] is **controlled by** the faction with strictly the highest
   [[Control]] in it
3. Two or more factions with equal highest [[Control]]: the Star is
   [[Disputed]], nobody controls it
4. [[Actor]] is **controlled by** its [[Faction]] unless [[Damaged]]
5. [[Structure]] is **controlled by**:
	1. The faction that controls its Star
	2. Its own faction in a [[Disputed]] Star if the faction is in the tie,
	   else by nobody: it is out of control, nobody can use it

#### Notes

1. Consequences from the rules on examples:
	1. single faction is present in a star - the faction controls all the
	   structures
	2. single faction has [[Actor]]s in a star - the faction controls the star
	   and all the structures, rival ones captured
	3. no [[Actor]]s, structures of several factions - the star is disputed,
	   every faction controls its own structures
	4. Actors of several factions - the star is disputed, every faction with
	   Actors controls its own cards, structures of a faction with only
	   structures there are out of control
2. Agreements on control are [[Politics]], not core

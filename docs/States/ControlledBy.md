1. **Controlled by** is a [[Star]] and card [[State]] relative to a
   [[Faction]]
2. [[Star]] is **controlled by** the faction with strictly the highest
   [[Control]] in it, a tie makes it [[Disputed]]
3. [[Actor]] is **controlled by** its [[Faction]] unless [[Damaged]]
4. [[Structure]] and [[Damaged]] card are **controlled by** the
   faction that controls its Star

#### Notes

1. Consequences from the rules on examples:
	1. Single faction is present in a Star - the faction controls all
	   the Structures
	2. Single faction has [[Actor]]s in a Star - the faction controls
	   the Star and all the Structures, rival ones captured
	3. No [[Actor]]s, Structures of several factions - the Star is
	   [[Disputed]], every faction controls its own Structures
	4. Actors of several factions - the Star is Disputed, every
	   faction with Actors controls its own cards, Structures of a
	   faction with only Structures there are out of control
	5. A [[Damaged]] rival [[Actor]] in a Star the faction controls is
	   captured like a Structure

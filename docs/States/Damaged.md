1. **Damaged** card is:
	1. Back [[Side]] up
	2. Damaged [[End]] up
2. Card becomes **damaged**:
	1. After defeat in [[Combat]]
	2. After applying [[Exhaust]] modifier
3. **Damaged** is opposite to [[Enabled]]
4. [[Recover]] makes card [[Enabled]]
5. A Damaged card that becomes Damaged again is [[Destroyed]]

#### Notes

1. Disabled, Exhausted, Wrecked are the same state, indistinguishable

1. **Damaged** is a card [[State]]
2. **Damaged** card is:
	1. Back [[Side]] up
	2. Damaged [[End]] up
3. Card becomes **damaged**:
	1. After defeat in [[Combat]]
	2. After applying [[Exhaust]] modifier
4. **Damaged** is opposite to [[Enabled]]
5. [[Recover]] makes card [[Enabled]]
6. A Damaged card that becomes Damaged again is [[Destroy]]ed
7. A Damaged card in a [[Star]] where its [[Faction]] has no
   [[Enabled]] card is [[Destroy]]ed
8. Anyone may look at a **Damaged** card: a wreck is no secret

#### Notes

1. Disabled, Exhausted are the same state, indistinguishable

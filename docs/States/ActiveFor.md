1. **Active for** is a card [[State]] relative to a [[Faction]]
2. Card is **active for** faction when:
	1. It is not [[Damaged]]
	2. It is [[ControlledBy]] player

#### Notes

1. When card is **active for** faction and in [[IPS]] it can be used to
   participate in [[PerformAction]]

1. **Destroyed** is a card [[State]]
2. **Destroyed** card is removed from [[Field]] or [[Hand]] (or anywhere) to its
   [[Faction]]'s [[Factory]]
3. The player puts it on top of a [[Factory]] pile of their choice, so it can be
   rebuilt next

#### Notes

1. [[Factory]] is the pool of cards to (re)build

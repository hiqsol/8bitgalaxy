1. **Depleted** [[Structure]] is:
	1. Front [[Side]] up
	2. Weak [[End]] up
2. [[Structure]] cards become **depleted** after [[Deplete]]
3. **Depleted** structure still participates with its weak [[End]]: its [[Level]] and [[PrimaryAbility]], no [[SecondaryAbility]]
4. There is no free [[Restore]]

#### Notes

1. In the [[Deck]] a structure's [[PrimaryAbility]] is a [[Technology]] at both ends, so a depleted structure keeps its technology but gives no [[Power]]

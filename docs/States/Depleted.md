1. **Depleted** is a [[Structure]] [[State]]
2. **Depleted** [[Structure]] is:
	1. Front [[Side]] up
	2. Weak [[End]] up
3. [[Structure]] cards become **depleted** after [[Deplete]]
4. **Depleted** structure still participates with its weak [[End]]: its
   [[Level]] and abilities, weaker than at the strong End
5. [[Restore]] makes it [[Ready]]: with [[Manage]] or 1 free at the end of own
   [[Turn]]

#### Notes

1. In the [[Deck]] a structure's [[PrimaryAbility]] is a [[Technology]] or a
   non-power [[Plus]] at both ends, and only the strong end has
   [[SecondaryAbility]] power: a depleted structure keeps the technology but
   gives no [[Power]]

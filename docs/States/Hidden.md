1. **Hidden** is a card [[State]]
2. **Hidden** card is:
	1. Back [[Side]] up
	2. Hidden [[End]] up
3. Only the player of its [[Faction]] knows a **hidden** card, others
   see only its [[Faction]] and [[Role]]: being in the same [[Star]]
   is not enough to see it
4. **Hidden** card on [[Field]]:
	1. Gives [[Control]] by its [[Role]]
	2. Moves as a minimal [[Ship]]: [[Range]] 1, [[Capacity]] 1
	3. Can be attacked
5. **Hidden** card on [[Field]] is revealed:
	1. When it takes part in any action other than [[Move]], in
	   [[Combat]] at the reveal, not at the start
	2. By its player at any time of own [[Turn]], for free
	3. By rival's [[Explore]], if the rival chooses so
	4. By its player when a rival group passes its [[Star]], to pin
	   the group, see [[Pinning]]
6. In [[Combat]] parties bluff with **hidden** cards from [[Hand]]

#### Notes

1. A hidden card moves with minimal values because the rival can't
   know or check what card it is
2. Cards get hidden on the [[Field]] only by [[Stealth]]: at
   [[Takeoff]] or later
3. Hidden cards neither pin nor get pinned, see [[Pinning]]
4. Range 1 doesn't reach the next [[Star]]: a hidden card leaves with
   [[Support]] or inside a group, whose [[Range]] is the sum

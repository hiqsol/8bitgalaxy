1. **IPS**, Inter Planetary Space, is the inner part of a [[Star]]
   system
2. **IPS** has two vertical rows of [[Slot]]s:
	1. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Unit]]s
	2. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Structure]]s
3. Each [[Star]] has its own split between the rows, e.g. a Star of
   8 **Slots** is 3+5, 4+4 or 5+3 Unit+Structure Slots

#### Notes

1. Slots are pockets of the Star tile, in the physical and the virtual
   game alike

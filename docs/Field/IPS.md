1. **IPS** stands for Inter Planetary Space
2. **IPS** is the inner part of a [[Star]] system
3. **IPS** has two vertical rows of [[Slot]]s:
	1. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Unit]]s
	2. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Structure]]s
4. Each [[Star]] has its own split between the rows, e.g. a Star of
   Size 8 is 3+5, 4+4 or 5+3 Unit+Structure Slots
5. The total number of Slots is the [[Star]]'s **Size**

#### Notes

1. Slots are pockets of the Star tile, in the physical and the virtual
   game alike
2. The back of a Star shows only the Size, the split is seen after

1. **IPS** stands for Inter Planetary Space
2. **IPS** is the inner part of a [[Star]] system
3. **IPS** includes [[Slot]]s, their total number is the [[Star]]'s **Size**:
	1. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Unit]]s
	2. Up to 5 [[Slot]]s for [[Structure]]s

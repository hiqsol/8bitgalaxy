1. **Slot** aka **pocket** is a place for one card in [[IPS]]
2. Card in a **slot** shows only the [[End]] that is up, the other end
   is inside the pocket
3. **Slots** are shared by all players
4. [[Unit]] and [[Structure]] slots are separate rows, [[Unit]] slots
   are thinner - for vertically oriented cards
5. Slots in a row are numbered 1-5 by height, so cards overlap:
	1. A card is **overlapped** when the next lower **slot** of its
	   row holds a card
	2. Overlapped card's [[SecondaryAbility]] is not available
6. A card that comes into a [[Star]] takes a free **slot** of its
   row, its player picks which: without one it can't come

#### Notes

1. Overlap is a new mechanic, not seen in other board games: cards
   naturally get one under another

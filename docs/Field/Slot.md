1. **Slot** aka **pocket** is a place for one card in [[IPS]]
2. Card in a **slot** shows only the [[End]] that is up, the other end is inside
   the pocket
3. **Slots** are shared by all players
4. [[Unit]] and [[Structure]] slots are separate rows:
	1. [[Unit]] slots are thinner - for vertically oriented cards
	2. Cards overlap only within their row
	3. Each [[Star]] has its own split between the rows, see [[IPS]]
5. Slots in a row are numbered 1-5 by height, so cards overlap:
	1. A card is **overlapped** when the next lower **slot** of its row holds a
	   card
	2. Overlapped card's [[SecondaryAbility]] is not available
6. Cards can be shifted between slots with [[Manage]] action

#### Notes

1. Overlap is a new mechanic, not seen in other games

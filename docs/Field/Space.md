1. **Space** is a [[Field]] tile, see [[Component]]
2. **Space** represents Interstellar Space and separates [[Star]] from
   each other
3. Each [[Star]] side has 2 **Space** tiles, so a Star has 12, named
   by o'clock bearing:
	1. 1-2 upper right side, 3-4 right, 5-6 lower right, 7-8 lower
	   left, 9-10 left, 11-12 upper left
	2. A tile on a side shared by two Stars belongs to both: 1↔8, 2↔7,
	   3↔10, 4↔9, 5↔12, 6↔11
	3. Each tile is separate: explored and passed on its own, so two
	   Stars are linked by 2 routes
	4. A tile connects to the Stars it borders and to the next tiles
	   along the edges
4. **Space** tiles are hidden (turned face down) at the beginning of
   the game
5. They can be revealed with [[Explore]] action
6. Cards can't stop in a **Space**, they only pass a revealed one
7. **Space** tiles have multiple properties:
	1. **Price**: the Cost to [[Explore]]
	2. **Type**
	3. **Length** - independent of Type, shown on the back side
	4. [[Ability]] - given by its Type
8. Types of space:
	1. Normal
	2. [[Asteroids]]
	3. Nebula
	4. Black hole
	5. [[Void]]
	6. [[Supernova]]

#### Notes

1. The back shows the **Length**, as the back of a [[Star]] shows its
   Slots: a hint at the Price, while Type and Price stay hidden until
   [[Explore]]

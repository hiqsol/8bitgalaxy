1. **Black hole** is a [[Space]] type
2. A group passing a revealed **Black hole** [[Deplete]]s one of its
   cards, its player chooses which
3. A [[Science]] action started in a [[Star]] bordering a revealed
   **Black hole** gets +1 [[SciencePower]]

#### Notes

1. A gravity well: crossing it costs a card, working next to it pays
   the scientists
2. Passable both ways, like any revealed Space

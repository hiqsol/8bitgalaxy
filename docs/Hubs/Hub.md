1. **Hub** is a kind of [[Technology]] that provides the [[Create]]
   [[TechnologyAction]] for cards of one [[Origin]]
2. **Hubs** are:
	1. **Industrial hub**: [[Shipyard]], [[MegaYard]]
	2. **Social hub**: [[Academy]], [[MegAcad]]
3. [[Create]] and [[Upgrade]] require the **hub** for the card and are
   paid in its [[Class]]

#### Notes

1. Actions on [[Home]] cards go through a **hub** on the [[Field]]:
   the [[Distance]] to it and its importance put pressure, a lost hub
   stops them, a captured one serves the captor
2. Planned: a global **hub** for both [[Origin]]s as a [[Faction]]
   feature

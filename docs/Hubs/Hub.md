1. **Hub** is a kind of [[Technology]], the list is in
   [[Technology]]: actions on cards in [[Home]] piles are directed to
   a **hub**
2. **Social hubs** and **Industrial hubs** take the cards of their
   [[Origin]]:
	1. They provide the [[Create]] [[TechnologyAction]] for those
	   cards
	2. [[Manage]] of those cards in the [[Factory]] is directed to
	   them
3. **Research hubs** take any card in the [[Laboratory]]: [[Research]]
   and [[Manage]] of it are directed to them

#### Notes

1. Actions on [[Home]] cards go through a **hub** on the [[Field]]:
   the [[Distance]] to it and its importance put pressure, a lost hub
   stops them, a captured one serves the captor
2. A faction without a fitting **hub** builds, researches or manages
   no such card until it captures one: no global hub off the
   [[Field]]
3. The kinds follow the [[Class]]es that handle the cards: Culture
   the Social, Production the Industrial, Science any

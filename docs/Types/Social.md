1. **Social** is an [[Origin]], aka **Alive**
2. **Social** cards are:
	1. [[Hero]]
	2. [[Colony]]
3. **Social** cards are created at a Social [[Hub]]
4. Not to be confused with [[Culture]] [[Class]]

#### Notes

1. **Social** cards climb one [[Level]] at a time, see [[Upgrade]],
   and pay it back with 1 more [[Power]] than the [[Industrial]]
   cards of their tier, see [[Deck]]

1. **Extra cost** is explicit [[Component]] [[Property]] of a card and
   a [[Star]]
2. Printed after the [[Level]] at each card [[End]]: `2+1` is level 2,
   extra cost 1
3. **Extra cost** adds to the level for [[Cost]] and [[Size]]
4. A [[Star]]'s **extra cost** adds to its Size for its Cost
5. A card's **extra cost** can be negative, printed as `2-1`: its
   [[Cost]] and [[Size]] stay at least 1
6. A [[Star]]'s **extra cost** is not negative: its Size is its
   minimal Cost, see [[Explore]]

#### Notes

1. Negative extra cost makes a strong card cheap and light, e.g. a
   [[Ship]] whose strong end keeps the same Size as its weak end

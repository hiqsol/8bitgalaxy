1. **End** is physical card [[Property]]: one of two short edges of a card
2. The end that is **up** is the one pointing:
	1. On [[Field]] - to the top of the [[Star]] the card is in
	2. Elsewhere - away from the player
3. Changing the end is called **rotate** (180°)
4. In a [[Slot]] only the end that is up is visible, the other end is inside the
   pocket
5. Front [[Side]] of a card is double ended, each end has the same layout, see
   [[Property]]:
	1. [[Level]] and [[Class]] in one corner
	2. [[PrimaryAbility]] and [[SecondaryAbility]] in the other corner
6. **Strong** end is the one with higher [[Level]], the other is **weak**
7. Ends mean different things for different cards:
	1. [[Actor]]s ([[Ship]], [[Hero]]) - each end may (and usually does) have
	   its own [[Class]], [[Level]] and abilities:
		1. The card is used with the end that is up
		2. Player picks the end for free at [[Takeoff]], after that only
		   [[Manage]] rotates it: movement keeps the end
		3. Supporter from [[Hand]] can be used with either end, see [[Support]]
	2. [[Structure]]s:
		1. Strong end up - the card is [[Ready]]
		2. Weak end up - the card is [[Depleted]]
	3. Back [[Side]] of any card:
		1. Damaged end up - the card is [[Damaged]]
		2. Hidden end up - the card is [[Hidden]]
8. Both ends can provide abilities, the weak end's are just weaker

#### Notes

1. Not to be confused with orientation: [[Unit]]s are vertical, [[Structure]]s
   are horizontal
2. [[Hero]] ends may have equal Levels: which one is strong doesn't matter,
   an [[Actor]] depletes to [[Discard]] anyway

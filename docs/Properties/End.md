1. **End** is physical card [[Property]]: one of two short edges of a
   card
2. The end that is **up** is the one pointing:
	1. On [[Field]] - to the top of the [[Star]] the card is in
	2. Elsewhere - away from the player
3. Changing the end is called **rotate** (180°)
4. Front [[Side]] of a card is double ended, each end has the same
   layout, see [[Property]]:
	1. [[Level]] and [[Class]] in one corner
	2. [[PrimaryAbility]] and [[SecondaryAbility]] in the other corner
5. **Strong** end is the one with higher [[Level]], the other is
   **weak**
6. Ends mean different things for different cards:
	1. [[Actor]]s ([[Ship]], [[Hero]]): each end may (and usually
	   does) have its own [[Class]], [[Level]] and abilities. Player
	   picks the end for free at [[Takeoff]], after that only
	   [[Manage]] rotates it: movement keeps the end
	2. [[Structure]]s: strong end up - [[Ready]], weak end up -
	   [[Depleted]]
	3. Back [[Side]]: Damaged end up - [[Damaged]], Hidden end up -
	   [[Hidden]]
7. A card gives abilities from one end only, the **end in use**: in a
   [[Slot]] the visible end, elsewhere the selected end
8. Both ends can provide abilities, the weak end's are just weaker

#### Notes

1. Not to be confused with orientation: [[Unit]]s are vertical,
   [[Structure]]s are horizontal

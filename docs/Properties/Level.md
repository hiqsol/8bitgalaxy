1. **Level** is explicit card [[Property]]
2. **Level** is number from 1 to 6 (8)
3. Card can have two levels: **higher** and **lower**, one at each [[End]]
4. **Total level** = sum of lower and higher levels
5. **Current level**:
	1. the visible one when other is not visible, e.g. in a [[Slot]]
	2. else the lower one

1. **Level** is explicit card [[Property]]
2. **Level** is number from 1 to 8
3. Levels form four **tiers** of two Levels: I 1-2, II 3-4, III 5-6,
   IV 7-8; a card's tier is the tier of its strong [[End]]
4. Card can have two levels: **higher** and **lower**, one at each
   [[End]]. Higher level is on the **strong** end.
5. **Current level** is the level of the [[End]] in use
6. A card provides one **Level**, the current one, with its
   [[Class]], e.g. for [[Prerequisites]] and [[Singularity]]

#### Notes

1. After [[Takeoff]] the other End is inside the pocket of the
   [[Slot]]: a card shows one Level, so it gives one Level everywhere

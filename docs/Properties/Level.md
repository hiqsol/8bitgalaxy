1. **Level** is explicit card [[Property]]
2. **Level** is number from 1 to 6 (8)
3. Card can have two levels: **higher** and **lower**, one at each [[End]]. Higher level is on the **strong** end.
4. **Current level** is the level of the End in use:
	1. the visible one when other is not visible, e.g. in a [[Slot]]
	2. the chosen one for a supporter from [[Hand]], see [[Support]]
	3. else the lower one
5. A card provides one **Level** with its [[Class]], e.g. for [[Prerequisites]] and the Singularity path of [[Goal]]:
	1. On the [[Field]]: the [[End]] that is up
	2. Elsewhere: the End chosen by its player

#### Notes

1. After [[Takeoff]] the other End is inside the pocket of the [[Slot]]: a card shows one Level, so it gives one Level everywhere

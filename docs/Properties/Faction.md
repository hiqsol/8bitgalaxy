1. **Faction** is explicit card [[Property]]
2. **Faction** is visible from both [[Side]]s of card: its color and
   design
3. All cards of **faction** form a faction deck
4. There are two **faction** types:
	1. for players
	2. [[Galaxy]]
5. Player **factions** are:
	1. **Symmetric**: their faction decks are the same by card values,
	   only color and design differ
	2. **Asymmetric**: each has a faction deck of its own

#### Notes

1. Symmetric factions are for an even contest, like white and black
   in chess, see [[GameBalance]]

1. **Faction** is explicit card [[Property]]
2. **Faction** is visible from both [[Side]]s of card
3. All cards of **faction** form a faction deck
4. There are two **faction** types:
	1. for players
	2. [[Alien]]

#### Notes

1. Asymmetric **faction** is asymmetric deck. For an even contest players get even decks, see [[GameBalance]].

1. **Side** is physical [[Component]] [[Property]]: one of two faces of a card
   or a tile:
	1. **Front** - properties and abilities
	2. **Back** - what is known while hidden: [[Faction]] and states
	   [[Damaged]] / [[Hidden]] of a card, Size of a [[Star]], Length of a
	   [[Space]]
2. Changing the side is called **flip**
3. Card front up - the card is [[Enabled]]
4. Card back up - the card is [[Damaged]] or [[Hidden]], depending on its
   [[End]]
5. Tile front up - the tile is revealed, see [[Explore]]

1. **Side** is physical [[Component]] [[Property]]: one of two faces
   of a card or a tile:
	1. **Front** - properties and abilities
	2. **Back** - what is known while hidden: [[Faction]] and
	   [[State]] of a card, Size of a [[Star]], Length of a
	   [[Space]]
2. Changing the side is called **flip**
3. Card front up - the card is [[Enabled]], back up - [[Damaged]] or
   [[Hidden]] by its [[End]]
4. Tile front up - the tile is revealed, see [[Explore]]

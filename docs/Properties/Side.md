1. **Side** is one of two faces of a card:
	1. **Front** - card's properties and abilities
	2. **Back** - [[Faction]] and states: [[Damaged]] / [[Hidden]]
2. Changing the side is called **flip**
3. Front up - the card is [[Enabled]]
4. Back up - the card is [[Damaged]] or [[Hidden]], depending on its [[End]]

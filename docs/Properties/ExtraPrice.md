1. **Extra price** is explicit [[Component]] [[Property]] of a card and
   a [[Star]]
2. Printed after the [[Level]] at each card [[End]]: `2+1` is level 2,
   extra price 1
3. **Extra price** adds to the level for [[Price]] and [[Size]]
4. A [[Star]]'s **extra price** adds to its Size for its Price
5. A card's **extra price** can be negative, printed as `2-1`: its
   [[Price]] and [[Size]] stay at least 1
6. A [[Star]]'s **extra price** is not negative: its Size is its
   minimal Price, see [[Explore]]

#### Notes

1. Negative extra price makes a strong card cheap and light, e.g. a
   [[Ship]] whose strong end keeps the same Size as its weak end

1. **Primary Ability** is explicit card [[Property]]
2. **Primary Ability** is an [[Ability]]: [[Power]], other [[Plus]] or
   [[Technology]]
3. [[Power]] of an [[Actor]] is printed as `X+Y`:
	1. `X` always
	2. `+Y` is added on the [[Field]]: for a direct participant and a
	   supporter from the Field

#### Notes

1. No [[Deck]] card prints `+Y` yet, the rules allow it: going to the
   [[Field]] pays without it, it thins the deck and keeps an unused
   card from going to [[Discard]] at **Draw**, see [[Turn]]

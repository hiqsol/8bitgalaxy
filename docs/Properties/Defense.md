1. **Defense** is implicit card [[Property]] derived from [[Class]]
   and [[Level]]
2. **Defense** is the combat power of defending card in [[Combat]]
3. Default value = current [[Level]] for [[Military]] cards, 1 for
   other cards
4. [[Plus]] Defense increases it
5. A defending supporter adds its **Defense** as its one [[Ability]],
   see [[Support]]

#### Notes

1. A [[Military]] card defends with its Level, not with its power: a
   [[Depleted]] Base 2M on its weak end has no power, but defends with
   Level 1

1. **Defense** is implicit card [[Property]] derived from [[Class]]
   and [[AttackPower]]
2. **Defense** is the combat power of defending card in [[Combat]]
3. Default value = [[AttackPower]] or 1 for non-[[Military]] cards
4. [[Plus]] Defense increases it

#### Notes

1. Defense by [[AttackPower]] presses to keep a [[Military]] card in a
   position with visible [[SecondaryAbility]], e.g. [[Ready]], not
   [[Depleted]]

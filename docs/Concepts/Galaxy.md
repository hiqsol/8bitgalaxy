1. **Galaxy** is the non-player [[Faction]]: the living galaxy itself
2. **Galaxy** plays its [[GalaxyDeck]] with a [[GalaxyMove]] every [[Turn]]
3. **Galaxy** cards are:
	1. [[Event]]s
	2. [[Alien]]s
	3. [[Derelict]]s
	4. [[Tool]]s
	5. [[Objective]]s
	6. More [[Technology]]s
4. The [[Scenario]] composes the [[GalaxyDeck]], for example:
	1. **Dangerous**: hostile [[Alien]]s and disasters, for a cooperative or solo game against the Galaxy
	2. **Resourceful**: helpful [[Derelict]]s, [[Tool]]s and positive [[Event]]s, for quick duels
	3. **Campaign**: a series of Galaxy decks, one per game

#### Notes

1. The Galaxy is neutral but active: it helps, tests and threatens, so one rule set serves co-op, solo and duels

1. **Alien** is a hostile [[Galaxy]] card on the [[Field]]: aliens breed, spread and attack
2. A [[GalaxyMove]] places an **Alien** card to an [[IPS]] in one of the following ways:
	1. **Develop**: put any card to a [[Star]] that already has active **Alien** cards
	2. **Expand**: put a [[Unit]] card to a [[Star]] adjacent to a star with active **Alien** cards
3. A placed **Alien** card stays unknown for players

#### Notes

1. The old Alien civilization, see [[Lore]]

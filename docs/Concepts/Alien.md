1. **Alien** is a hostile card of the [[Galaxy]] [[Faction]] on the
   [[Field]]
2. **Alien** cards form the **Alien deck**, face down beside the
   [[Field]], the [[Scenario]] composes it
3. When [[Explore]] reveals a [[Star]], **Aliens** appear in it:
	1. As many as the Star's [[ExtraPrice]], from the top of the
	   **Alien deck**
	2. Face down: nobody knows them until they are revealed, see
	   [[Hidden]]
	3. They take no [[Slot]]
4. **Aliens** give [[Control]] as [[Actor]]s: a player controls their
   Star only after defeating them
5. **Aliens** are fought by an [[Attack]] on their Star:
	1. All **Aliens** of the Star defend together, each with its
	   printed [[Defense]], nobody adds more
	2. If the attacker wins, the **Aliens** are defeated: they go back
	   to the **Alien deck** instead of becoming [[Damaged]]
	3. Else the **Aliens** change: they go back to the **Alien deck**
	   and as many new hidden **Aliens** appear
6. The **Alien deck** is shuffled whenever **Aliens** go back to it

#### Notes

1. The old Alien civilization, see [[Lore]]
2. Like Eclipse Ancients: a better Star has a higher [[ExtraPrice]],
   so more **Aliens** guard it
3. Neutral resistance without the [[GalaxyDeck]]: [[Military]] has a
   job before the rivals meet
4. A lost [[Combat]] reveals the **Aliens**, but the next attack meets
   new ones: they stay unknown until defeated

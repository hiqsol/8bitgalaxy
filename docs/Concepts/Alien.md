1. **Alien** is a hostile card of the [[Galaxy]] [[Faction]]: it
   defends a [[Wild]] Star
2. **Alien** cards form the **Alien deck**, face down beside the
   [[Field]], the [[Scenario]] composes it
3. When an [[Attack]] conquers a [[Wild]] Star, **Aliens** come from
   the top of the **Alien deck**:
	1. As many as the [[Price]] of the drawn [[World]]
	2. Face down: nobody knows them until they are revealed, see
	   [[Hidden]]
	3. They take no [[Slot]]
4. All **Aliens** of the Star defend together, each with its printed
   [[Defense]], nobody adds more
5. After the [[Combat]] the **Aliens** go to the **Alien discard**,
   won or lost, they never become [[Damaged]]
6. When the **Alien deck** runs out, the **Alien discard** is shuffled
   into a new **Alien deck**

#### Notes

1. The old Alien civilization, see [[Lore]]
2. Like Eclipse Ancients: a better World has a higher [[Price]],
   so more **Aliens** guard it
3. Neutral resistance without the [[GalaxyDeck]]: [[Military]] has a
   job before the rivals meet
4. Every attack meets new **Aliens**: they stay unknown until the
   [[Combat]] reveal
5. As many as the Price is a first guess, playtests set the number

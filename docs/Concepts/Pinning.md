1. **Pinning** stops a moving group in a [[Star]] with rival [[Enabled]] [[Military]] [[Actor]]s:
	1. The group can't pass through such a Star, it can finish there
	2. The rival can allow the group to pass
2. **Pinning** applies to every route through Stars: [[Move]], [[Transport]], [[Attack]]
3. [[Hidden]] cards neither pin nor get pinned

#### Notes

1. Like in Twilight Imperium and Eclipse: fronts form and the home Star can be defended
2. Allowing to pass makes deals in games of 3+ players

1. **Pinning** stops a moving group in a [[Star]] with rival
   [[Enabled]] [[Military]] [[Actor]]s:
	1. The group can't pass through such a Star, it can finish there
	2. The rival can allow the group to pass
2. **Pinning** stops moving cards only, on every route through Stars:
   [[Move]] and [[Attack]]
	1. Other [[Range]] checks ignore it: [[Explore]] reach, a remote
	   [[Support]] group, [[Create]] placement
3. [[Hidden]] cards neither pin nor get pinned
4. A player can reveal own [[Hidden]] cards in a Star a rival group is
   passing: revealed [[Military]] [[Actor]]s pin it

#### Notes

1. Like in Twilight Imperium and Eclipse: fronts form and the home
   Star can be defended, a pinned group that stays meets an
   [[Intercept]]
2. Allowing to pass makes deals in games of 3+ players

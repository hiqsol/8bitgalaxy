1. **Support** is taking part in [[PerformAction]] from remote:
	1. From [[Hand]], including the **starter** of the action, or
	2. From another [[IPS]], not the source or the destination: its
	   supporters form a group that must reach the destination, see
	   [[PerformAction]], this doesn't use their one [[Ability]]
2. **Supporters** add to action only one [[Ability]]:
	1. [[PrimaryAbility]]
	2. [[SecondaryAbility]]
	3. [[Range]]
	4. [[Capacity]]
3. Supporter from [[Hand]] can be used with either [[End]]
4. After action [[Deplete]] all the **supporters**

#### Notes

1. [[Range]] applies to every action and in [[Combat]]: support is
   local, so positions on the [[Field]] matter
2. A [[Structure]] has Range 0: from another IPS it supports only in a
   group with [[Actor]]s from its [[Star]], and they become supporters
   too

1. **Support** is taking part in [[PerformAction]] from remote:
	1. From [[Hand]], including the **starter** of the action, or
	2. From remote (not source or destination) [[IPS]]
		1. Supporters from one remote IPS form a group: the destination must be within the group's [[Range]], this doesn't use their one [[Ability]]
		2. A [[Structure]] supports only together with [[Actor]]s from its [[Star]], they become supporters too
2. **Supporters** add to action only one [[Ability]]:
	1. [[PrimaryAbility]]
	2. [[SecondaryAbility]]
	3. [[Range]]
	4. [[Capacity]]
3. Supporter from [[Hand]] can be used with either [[End]]
4. After action [[Deplete]] all the **supporters**

#### Notes

1. [[Range]] applies to every action and in [[Combat]]: support is local, so positions on the [[Field]] matter

1. **Support** is taking part in [[PerformAction]] from remote:
	1. From [[Hand]], including the **starter** of the action, or
	2. From another [[IPS]], not the source or the destination, as a
	   group, see [[PerformAction]]: reaching doesn't use their one
	   [[Ability]]
2. **Supporters** add to action only one [[Ability]]:
	1. [[PrimaryAbility]]
	2. [[SecondaryAbility]]
	3. [[Range]]
	4. [[Capacity]]

#### Notes

1. Support is local: positions on the [[Field]] matter
2. A [[Structure]] has Range 0: from another IPS it supports only in a
   group with [[Actor]]s from its [[Star]], and they become supporters
   too

1. **Ability** is what a card or a [[Star]] provides to an [[Action]]
2. **Ability** can be:
	1. [[Power]]
	2. [[Materials]]
	3. [[Money]]
	4. [[Defense]]
	5. [[Technology]]
	6. [[Range]] or [[Capacity]]
	7. [[Level]] and [[Class]] for [[Prerequisites]]
3. Card abilities: [[PrimaryAbility]], [[SecondaryAbility]], [[AdditionalAbility]]
4. [[Star]] has one **ability**, usable once per own [[Turn]]

#### Notes

1. More kinds of **ability** can be added later

1. **Ability** is what a card or a [[Star]] provides
2. **Ability** comes in two kinds:
	1. [[Plus]] - a number, shown as `+N`
	2. [[Technology]] - a named rule, present or not
3. **Abilities** come from:
	1. Card print: [[PrimaryAbility]], [[SecondaryAbility]]
	2. Card [[Level]]: [[Range]], [[Capacity]] and [[Level]] with [[Class]] for [[Prerequisites]]
	3. [[Star]]: one **ability**, usable once per own [[Turn]]

#### Notes

1. An ability that only adds a number is a [[Plus]], not a [[Technology]]

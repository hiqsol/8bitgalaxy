1. **Homeworld** is a [[Hyperstructure]] card
2. **Homeworld** lies in the World slot of its [[Star]], not in a
   Structure [[Slot]], see [[Star]]
3. The [[Scenario]] sets the **Homeworld's** [[Ability]]
4. A [[Destroy]]ed **Homeworld** goes to its faction's [[Factory]]
5. **Homeworld** is rebuilt by the Hyperstructure rule into the World
   slot of any [[Wild]] Star

#### Notes

1. A possessed **Homeworld** counts toward the 7 Hyperstructures that
   trigger [[Twilight]]

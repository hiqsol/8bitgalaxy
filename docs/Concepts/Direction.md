1. **Direction** is one of definitive characteristics of action
2. **Direction** indicates:
	1. **Source** where action starts
	2. **Destination** where action finishes
3. Direct participants are in the **source** or the **destination**,
   see [[PerformAction]]
4. An action directed to a card, e.g. to a [[BuildingFacility]], is
   directed to the [[Star]] the card is in
5. **Direction** has length: [[Distance]] from source to destination

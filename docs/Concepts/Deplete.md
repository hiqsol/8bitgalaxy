1. After [[PerformAction]] is done participants are depleted:
	1. The starter and the [[Support]]ers always
	2. Direct participants unless they moved or fought: the moving
	   cards of [[Move]], the direct participants of [[Combat]]
2. Cards get depleted differently:
	1. [[Unit]] cards, from the [[Field]] or [[Hand]], go to
	   [[Discard]] pile
	2. [[Structure]] cards become (or stay) [[Depleted]]
3. Targets are not participants, they don't deplete, e.g. cargo of
   [[Move]]

#### Notes

1. Cards that move or fight hold the ground, the starter always pays:
   nobody has to remember which cards already moved

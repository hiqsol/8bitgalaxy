1. During **Galaxy move** player takes the top card of the [[GalaxyDeck]] and plays it by its kind:
	1. [[Event]]: to [[FutureEvents]]
	2. [[Alien]]: to an [[IPS]], see [[Alien]]
	3. [[Derelict]]: to a [[Star]]
	4. Other cards: as the card says
2. **Galaxy move** is mandatory every [[Turn]] when the [[Galaxy]] is present
3. When the [[GalaxyDeck]] runs out, [[Twilight]] is triggered

#### Notes

1. Not only enemies: the Galaxy generates content, a threat, an opportunity or a puzzle

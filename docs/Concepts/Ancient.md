1. **Ancient** is a hostile card of the [[Galaxy]] [[Faction]]: it
   defends a [[Wild]] Star
2. **Ancient** cards form the **Ancient deck**, face down beside the
   [[Field]], the [[Scenario]] composes it
3. When an [[Attack]] conquers a [[Wild]] Star, **Ancients** come from
   the top of the **Ancient deck**:
	1. As many as the [[Price]] of the drawn [[World]]
	2. Face up, before the first round of the [[Combat]]
	3. They take no [[Slot]]
4. All **Ancients** of the Star defend together, each with its printed
   [[Defense]], nobody adds more
5. After the [[Combat]] the **Ancients** go to the **Ancient
   discard**, won or lost, they never become [[Damaged]]
6. When the **Ancient deck** runs out, the **Ancient discard** is
   shuffled into a new **Ancient deck**

#### Notes

1. An old civilization, far ahead of the newcomers, see [[Lore]]
2. Like Eclipse Ancients: a better World has a higher [[Price]],
   so more **Ancients** guard it
3. Neutral resistance without the [[GalaxyDeck]]: [[Military]] has a
   job before the rivals meet
4. As many as the Price is a first guess, playtests set the number
5. The attacker sees the defense before committing more and can
   [[Retreat]] in time

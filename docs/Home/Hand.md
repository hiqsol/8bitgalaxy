1. **Hand** is a pile in [[Home]]: the cards a player holds
2. Cards in **Hand** are visible only to its player
3. **Hand** size is 5 cards: at **Draw** phase of [[Turn]] player
   [[DeckBuilding|Draw]]s up to it.
4. Cards from **Hand** are played at **Play** phase of own [[Turn]]
5. **Hand** also holds [[Energy]] tokens

1. **Hand** is a pile in [[Home]]: the cards a player holds
2. Cards in **Hand** are visible only to its player
3. **Hand** size is 5 cards: at the end of [[Turn]] player [[Draw]]s up to it.
4. Cards from **Hand** are played in [[Turn]]: [[Takeoff]] or start an
   [[Action]].
5. **Hand** also holds [[Energy]] tokens, open to all, they are not cards, see
   [[Energy]]

#### Notes

1. Hand size 4 is an option, see [[GameBalance]]

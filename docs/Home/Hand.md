1. **Hand** is a pile in [[Home]]: the cards a player holds
2. Cards in **Hand** are visible only to its player
3. **Hand** size is 5 cards: at **Draw** phase of [[Turn]] player
   [[Draw]]s up to it.
4. Cards from **Hand** are played in own [[Turn]]: [[Takeoff]], start
   an [[Action]] or join one as a [[Support]]er
5. **Hand** also holds [[Energy]] tokens, see [[Energy]]

#### Notes

1. Hand size 4 is an option, see [[GameBalance]]

1. **Factory** is a row of piles in [[Home]]
2. **Factory** piles hold cards that are ready to [[Create]]
3. Only the top card of each pile is available
4. Additional factory can be created with [[PlusFactory]] action

#### Notes

1. **Factory** is the pool of cards to (re)build, that's why [[Destroyed]] cards go there

1. **Technology action** is an [[Action]] that requires a
   [[Technology]] provided by a participant
2. **Technology action** has its own **Direction** and **Cost**, see
   [[PerformAction]]
3. **Cost** is paid in the [[Power]] of the [[Class]] of the
   Technology, for a two-class Technology of the [[End]] that
   provides it
4. List of technology actions, see [[Technology]]

1. **Explore** is [[Military]] and [[Science]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. Flavour names:
	1. **Recon** for [[Military]]
	2. **Survey** for [[Science]]
3. **Explore** reveals one target:
	1. Hidden [[Space]]
	2. Hidden [[Star]]
	3. [[Hidden]] card
4. **Direction**: [[Star]] to the target
5. **Requirements**:
	1. Hidden tile borders the source [[Star]] or a revealed tile reachable
	   within [[Range]]
	2. Hidden card is in the source [[Star]] or in a Star reachable within
	   [[Range]]
6. **Cost**: the target's Cost, for a card its [[Cost]], of [[AttackPower]] for
   **Recon** or [[SciencePower]] for **Survey**
7. The Cost is not shown: the player starts the action, then learns the Cost
	1. A hidden [[Star]] shows its Size, its minimal Cost: the action starts
	   with at least Size of power
8. If the power is not enough the player either:
	1. Adds more power by spending more cards
	2. Cancels the action, losing what's already spent
9. For a [[Hidden]] card the acting player chooses to reveal it to all or keep
   it hidden
10. **Recon** directed to an [[Academy]] [[ControlledBy]] a rival looks at that
    rival's [[Hand]]:
	1. The [[Academy]] card is the target: in reach as a Hidden card, the Cost
	   is its [[Cost]]

#### Notes

1. Both military and scientific factions can reveal, like [[Create]] serves
   both Culture and Production
2. Better tiles cost more, so running out of power hints at a good one
3. To reach a hidden Star explore the Space before it, then the Star itself
4. Learning the Cost and cancelling is a cheap look, that's fine: the back of a
   [[Space]] already hints at it, and a [[Star]] look costs at least its Size
5. The [[Academy]] is where a faction's leaders come from, so **Recon** of it
   opens the way to the faction's deck, see also [[Combat]]

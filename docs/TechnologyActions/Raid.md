1. **Raid** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Raid** is an [[Attack]] whose winning attackers don't move to the destination
3. **Outcome** of a won **Raid**, one of:
	1. [[Materials]] of the target [[Star]] move to the source Star
	2. Rival [[Structure]]s in the target Star become [[Depleted]]

#### Notes

1. Hit and run, like raids in Through the Ages

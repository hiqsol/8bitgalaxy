1. **Upgrade** is [[Culture]] and [[Production]] [[TechnologyAction]] of [[BuildingFacility]]
2. Flavour names:
	1. **Grow** for [[Social]]
	2. **Enhance** for [[Industrial]]
3. **Upgrade** allows to substitute a card for another card of the same type
4. **Direction**:
	1. For [[Actor]] - any [[Star]] to [[BuildingFacility]]
	2. For [[Structure]] - [[BuildingFacility]] to old card's [[Star]]
5. **Requirements**:
	1. Old [[Actor]] must be in [[Discard]]
	2. New card must be available at [[Factory]]
	3. New card's [[Cost]] must be greater than old card's Cost
6. **Cost**: [[Cost]] of the new card **minus** cost of the old card
7. **Outcome**:
	1. New card substitutes the old card in place
	2. Old card goes to selected [[Factory]]

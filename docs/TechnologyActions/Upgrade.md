1. **Upgrade** is [[Culture]] and [[Production]] [[TechnologyAction]]
   of [[Hub]]
2. Flavour names:
	1. **Grow** for [[Social]]
	2. **Enhance** for [[Industrial]]
3. **Upgrade** replaces a card with another card of the same type
4. **Direction**:
	1. For [[Actor]] - any [[Star]] to [[Hub]]
	2. For [[Structure]] - [[Hub]] to old card's [[Star]]
5. **Requirements**:
	1. Old [[Actor]] must be in [[Discard]]
	2. New card must be available at [[Factory]]
	3. New card's [[Price]] can be greater, equal or lower than old
	   card's Price: a downgrade is allowed
6. **Cost**: [[Price]] of the new card **minus** Price of the old
   card, at least 1
7. **Outcome**:
	1. New card substitutes the old card in place
	2. Old card goes to a selected [[Factory]] pile

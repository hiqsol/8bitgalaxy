1. **Retreat** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Retreat** is an [[ImmediateAction]] in a [[Combat]] the player
   takes part in
3. **Outcome**: escape from the [[Combat]]:
	1. The [[Combat]] finishes
	2. Own participants don't get [[Damaged]], they only [[Deplete]]

#### Notes

1. Useful to save your forces when you are losing the combat

1. **Sell** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Sell** is a [[Trade]] over a card: it converts a selected card
   [[ControlledBy]] the player in the source [[Star]] to
   [[Materials]], the card is a target, not a participant
3. **Direction**: from a [[Star]] with a [[Colony]] of one party to a
   Star with a Colony of the other
4. **Requirements**: consent of the other party's player
5. **Outcome**:
	1. The [[Price]] of the card converts to [[Materials]] one to one
	2. The players split the [[Materials]] between their Stars of the
	   Direction as they agree
	3. The card is [[Destroy]]ed

1. **Assimilate** is [[Culture]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Assimilate** replaces a rival [[Colony]] with an own Colony from
   the player's [[Factory]]
3. **Direction**: source [[Star]] to the Star of the rival Colony
4. **Requirements**:
	1. The target Star is [[ControlledBy]] the player
	2. The rival Colony is [[Enabled]], it is the target, not a
	   participant
	3. An own Colony with an [[End]] of the rival Colony's current
	   [[Level]] is available at the [[Factory]]
5. **Cost**: [[Price]] of that End of the own Colony
6. **Outcome**:
	1. The own Colony takes the [[Slot]], that End up
	2. The rival Colony goes to its [[Faction]]'s Factory

#### Notes

1. The rival's world changes sides: the Colony is of the player's
   Faction now, it counts for the player's
   [[GalacticDiaspora|Galactic Diaspora]] and goes to the player's
   Factory when [[Destroy]]ed
2. No [[Hub]] needed, unlike [[Create]]

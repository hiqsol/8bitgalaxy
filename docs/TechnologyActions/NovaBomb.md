1. **Nova Bomb** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Nova Bomb** [[Destroy]]s every card in the target [[Star]]: own,
   rival and [[Galaxy]] cards, the [[World]] included
3. **Direction**: source [[Star]] to another Star
4. **Cost**: the total current [[Level]] of the cards in the target
   Star, [[Hidden]] ones included: the player learns it after the
   start, as for [[Explore]]
5. **Outcome**: the cards are Destroyed, the card that provides
   **Nova Bomb** leaves the game, like a destroyed [[Tool]]

#### Notes

1. A doomsday weapon of the [[Ancient]]s, recruited from a
   [[Derelict]]: like the Supernova [[Event]], a Star burns for one
   shot
2. Not a [[Combat]]: cards from [[Hand]] can't defend, only the Cost
   stops it

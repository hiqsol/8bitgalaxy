1. **Stealth** is [[Military]] [[TechnologyAction]]
2. **Stealth** is an [[ImmediateAction]]
3. **Outcome**: the shown card puts another own card on the [[Field]]
   into **stealth mode**: the other card becomes [[Hidden]]
4. A card in stealth mode moves as any [[Hidden]] card: [[Range]] 1,
   [[Capacity]] 1

#### Notes

1. Stealth makes hidden moves on the [[Field]] possible, [[Explore]]
   counters it

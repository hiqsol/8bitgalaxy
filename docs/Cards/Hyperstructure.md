1. **Hyperstructure** is a card [[Type]]
2. **Hyperstructure** is a wonder with a strong permanent effect
3. [[Role]] = [[Structure]]
4. [[Origin]] = both [[Social]] and [[Industrial]]
5. [[Range]] = 0 and cannot be cargo of a [[Move]]
6. [[Size]] = current [[Level]] + [[ExtraPrice]]
7. [[Capacity]] = current [[Level]]
8. **Hyperstructure** is built by two [[Create]]s in one [[Star]]:
	1. First as a [[Social]] card, for its [[Price]]
	2. Then as an [[Industrial]] card, for the [[Level]] +
	   [[ExtraPrice]] of its weak [[End]]
9. List:
	1. **Homeworld**:
		1. Lies in the World slot of its [[Star]], not in a Structure
		   [[Slot]]: it gives the Star its [[Class]] and
		   [[Ability|abilities]], the Star is not [[Wild]]
		2. The [[Scenario]] sets its [[Ability]]
	2. `advanced`, one per [[Class]]:
		1. **Citadel** - [[Military]]: +3 [[Defense]] to the
		   controller's cards in its [[Star]]
		2. **Senate** - [[Culture]]: [[Hand]] size +1
		3. **Dyson Sphere** - [[Production]]: +2 [[Energy]] at the end
		   of each [[Turn]] of the controller
		4. **Warp Gate** - [[Science]]: [[Distance]] between its Star
		   and every Star with a [[Colony]] of the controller is 2, as
		   between adjacent Stars
10. A **Hyperstructure** works for the faction it is [[ControlledBy]]

#### Notes

1. Wonders like in Through the Ages and Civilization
2. Not a victory path, see [[Goal]]
3. A [[Destroy]]ed **Homeworld** goes to the [[Factory]] and is
   rebuilt like any **Hyperstructure**, into the World slot of any
   [[Wild]] Star

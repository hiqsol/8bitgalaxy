1. **Hyperstructure** is a card [[Type]]
2. **Hyperstructure** is a wonder with a strong permanent effect
3. [[Role]] = [[Structure]]
4. [[Origin]] = both [[Social]] and [[Industrial]], build two times
5. [[Range]] = 0 and cannot be cargo of a [[Move]]
6. [[Size]] = current [[Level]] + [[ExtraCost]]
7. [[Capacity]] = current [[Level]]
8. List, one per [[Class]]:
	1. **Citadel** - [[Military]]: +3 [[Defense]] to the controller's cards in
	   its [[Star]]
	2. **Senate** - [[Culture]]: [[Hand]] size +1
	3. **Dyson Sphere** - [[Production]]: +2 [[Energy]] at the end of each
	   [[Turn]] of the controller
	4. **Warp Gate** - [[Science]]: [[Distance]] between its Star and every Star
	   with a [[Colony]] of the controller is 2, as between adjacent Stars
9. A **Hyperstructure** works for the faction it is [[ControlledBy]]

#### Notes

1. Wonders like in Through the Ages and Civilization
2. Not a victory path, see [[Goal]]

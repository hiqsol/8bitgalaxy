1. **Galaxy** is the non-player [[Faction]]: the living galaxy itself
2. **Galaxy** plays its [[GalaxyDeck]] with a [[GalaxyMove]] every
   [[Turn]]
3. **Galaxy** cards are:
	1. [[Event]]s
	2. [[Ancient]]s
	3. [[Derelict]]s
	4. [[Tool]]s
	5. [[Objective]]s
	6. More [[Technology]]s
	7. [[Hyperstructure]]s
4. The [[Scenario]] composes the [[GalaxyDeck]], for example:
	1. **Dangerous**: hostile [[Ancient]]s and disasters, for a
	   cooperative or solo game against the Galaxy
	2. **Resourceful**: helpful [[Derelict]]s, [[Tool]]s and positive
	   [[Event]]s, for quick duels
	3. **Campaign**: a series of Galaxy decks, one per game
5. [[Hyperstructure]] examples, one per [[Class]]:
	1. **Citadel** - [[Military]]: +3 [[Defense]] to the controller's
	   cards in its [[Star]]
	2. **Senate** - [[Culture]]: [[Hand]] size +1
	3. **Dyson Sphere** - [[Production]]: +2 [[Energy]] at the end of
	   each [[Turn]] of the controller
	4. **Warp Gate** - [[Science]]: [[Distance]] between its Star and
	   every Star with a [[Colony]] of the controller is 2, as between
	   adjacent Stars

#### Notes

1. The Galaxy is neutral but active: it helps, tests and threatens, so
   one rule set serves co-op, solo and duels

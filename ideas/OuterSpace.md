1. **Outer space** is intermediate part of [[Star]] which is:
	1. Inside of the star system
	2. Outside of [[IPS]]
	3. Can hold unlimited number of cards
2. **Outer space** is represented as a pile inside of every Star
3. Cards in **outer space** can only [[Move]] not [[PerformAction]]
4. Starting or finishing a route in **outer space** doesn't add [[Distance]]

#### Notes

1. Out of the rules: unlimited parking removes space pressure and allows
   unlimited deck thinning, see [[GameBalance]]
2. Open holes if revived:
	1. Parked [[Hero]]es can't be attacked: an unkillable leader, see [[Goal]]
	2. Parked [[Actor]]s may count for [[Control]] and block the rival's star

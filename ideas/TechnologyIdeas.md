# Technology ideas

Proposals for new technologies, a description only. A liked one gets
its Level, [[Class]], kind and cards in [[Technology]].

1. **Assembly Line**: one Create makes two cards from the same Factory
   pile, paying both
2. **Orbital Drop**: Create puts a Structure into any Star with an own
   Unit, without Range from the hub
3. **Mass Driver**: Goods go from its Star to another own Star without
   a Ship
4. **Habitat**: +1 Structure Slot in its Star
5. **Overclock**: a Structure gives its Secondary twice, then it is
   Damaged
6. **Economy of Scale**: Create costs 1 less for each own Structure in
   the target Star
7. **Megaproject**: a Hyperstructure is built over several Turns, the
   paid power stays on it as Goods
8. **Terraforming**: replaces the World of an own Star with a higher
   one from the World deck
9. **Planet Cracker**: Destroys a World, its controller gets Goods
   equal to its Level twice
10. **Refinery**: 2 Goods of its Star become 1 Energy in Hand
11. **Dyson Collector**: +1 Energy at Restore for each Ready Structure
    in its Star
12. **War Economy**: Production power pays Military actions started in
    its Star
13. **Outpost**: Create a Structure into a Wild Star without a
    conquest: it gives Control, the Star stays Wild
14. **Fortification**: own Structures in its Star defend with their
    Level, not 1
15. **Salvage Yard**: a card Destroyed in its Star becomes Goods of
    its controller
16. **Standardization**: Upgrade raises a card by two Levels
17. **Prefab**: a created Structure comes Ready and acts this Turn
18. **Merge**: two Depleted Structures of one Class in a Star become
    one Ready Structure of their summed Level, up to the Deck's
    highest
19. **Infrastructure**: +1 Range to every own action started in its
    Star
20. **Supply Lines**: own Units in a Star linked to the home by own
    Stars get +1 Defense
21. **Blockade**: a Star with an own Military Actor pays out no rival
    Energy or Goods
22. **Bombardment**: an Attack Damages one defending Structure before
    the first round
23. **Boarding**: the winner of a Combat takes one defeated Ship into
    its Discard instead of Damaging it
24. **Escort**: a moving group with a Military Actor can't be
    Intercepted
25. **Siege**: defenders in the target Star lose their Plus Defense
26. **Mercenaries**: Energy buys Ancients from the Ancient deck to
    fight on own side
27. **Scorched Earth**: losing defenders Destroy the Goods and Deplete
    the Structures of their Star
28. **Conscription**: a Tool fights as a Military card of Level 2
29. **Defection**: a rival Damaged Hero in a Star the player controls
    joins the player's Discard
30. **Refugees**: when a rival loses a Star, its Damaged Colonies
    there may move into own free Slots
31. **Tolls**: a rival action passing its Star pays its controller 1
    Energy
32. **Golden Age**: a Turn with no Combat ends with +1 Draw
33. **Pilgrimage**: a rival Hero in its Star gives its controller 1
    Energy each Turn
34. **Treaty**: both players can't Attack each other until the end of
    the next Turn, both Draw 1
35. **Monument**: a Structure that can't be captured, only Destroyed
36. **Martyr**: an own Hero Destroyed in Combat gives Energy equal to
    its Level
37. **Prototype**: Research puts the card into Hand instead of a
    Factory pile
38. **Reverse Engineering**: Research ignores Prerequisites for a card
    the player defeated in Combat
39. **Probe**: look at a rival's Hand
40. **Warp Lens**: Explore reaches a tile at any Distance
41. **Entanglement**: two own cards in different Stars act as one
    group, no Range between them
42. **Tech Exchange**: swap a Laboratory card with a rival's, both
    agree
43. **Eureka**: a failed action gives 1 Energy
44. **Warp Overcharge**: doubles the Range of a group, its cards are
    Damaged after the action
45. **Precognition**: look at the top cards of the World or Ancient
    deck, put them back in any order

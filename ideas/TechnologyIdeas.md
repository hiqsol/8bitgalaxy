# Technology ideas

Proposals for new [[Technology|Technologies]], a description only. A
liked one gets its [[Level]], [[Class]], kind and [[Card]]s in
[[Technology]].

1. **Assembly Line**: one [[Create]] makes two [[Card]]s from the same
   [[Factory]] pile, paying both
2. **Orbital Drop**: [[Create]] puts a [[Structure]] into any [[Star]]
   with an own [[Unit]], without [[Range]] from the [[Hub]]
3. **Mass Driver**: [[Goods]] go from its [[Star]] to another own
   [[Star]] without a [[Ship]]
4. **Habitat**: +1 [[Structure]] [[Slot]] in its [[Star]]
5. **Overclock**: a [[Structure]] gives its Secondary twice, then it
   is [[Damaged]]
6. **Economy of Scale**: [[Create]] costs 1 less for each own
   [[Structure]] in the target [[Star]]
7. **Megaproject**: a [[Hyperstructure]] is built over several
   [[Turn]]s, the paid [[Power]] stays on it as [[Goods]]
8. **Terraforming**: replaces the [[World]] of an own [[Star]] with a
   higher one from the [[WorldDeck|World deck]]
9. **Planet Cracker**: [[Destroy]]s a [[World]], its controller gets
   [[Goods]] equal to its [[Level]] twice
10. **Refinery**: 2 [[Goods]] of its [[Star]] become 1 [[Energy]] in
    [[Hand]]
11. **Dyson Collector**: +1 [[Energy]] at [[Restore]] for each
    [[Ready]] [[Structure]] in its [[Star]]
12. **War Economy**: [[Production power]] pays
    [[Military]] [[Action]]s started in its [[Star]]
13. **Outpost**: [[Create]] a [[Structure]] into a [[Wild]] [[Star]]
    without a conquest: it gives [[Control]], the [[Star]] stays
    [[Wild]]
14. **Fortification**: own [[Structure]]s in its [[Star]] defend with
    their [[Level]], not 1
15. **Salvage Yard**: a [[Card]] Destroyed in its [[Star]] becomes
    [[Goods]] of its controller
16. **Standardization**: [[Upgrade]] raises a [[Card]] by two
    [[Level]]s
17. **Prefab**: a created [[Structure]] comes [[Ready]] and acts this
    [[Turn]]
18. **Merge**: two [[Depleted]] [[Structure]]s of one [[Class]] in a
    [[Star]] become one [[Ready]] [[Structure]] of their summed
    [[Level]], up to the [[Deck]]'s highest
19. **Infrastructure**: +1 [[Range]] to every own [[Action]] started
    in its [[Star]]
20. **Supply Lines**: own [[Unit]]s in a [[Star]] linked to the home
    by own [[Star]]s get +1 [[Defense]]
21. **Blockade**: a [[Star]] with an own [[Military]] [[Actor]] pays
    out no rival [[Energy]] or [[Goods]]
22. **Bombardment**: an [[Attack]] Damages one defending [[Structure]]
    before the first round
23. **Boarding**: the winner of a [[Combat]] [[Destroy]]s one defeated
    [[Ship]] and takes its own copy of it into its [[Discard]]
24. **Escort**: a moving [[Group]] with a [[Military]] [[Actor]] can't
    be Intercepted
25. **Siege**: [[Defender]]s in the target [[Star]] lose their
    [[Plus]] [[Defense]]
26. **Mercenaries**: [[Energy]] buys [[Ancient]]s from the
    [[Ancient deck]] to fight on own side
27. **Scorched Earth**: losing [[Defender]]s [[Destroy]] the [[Goods]]
    and [[Deplete]] the [[Structure]]s of their [[Star]]
28. **Conscription**: a [[Tool]] fights as a [[Military]] [[Card]] of
    [[Level]] 2
29. **Defection**: a rival [[Damaged]] [[Hero]] in a [[Star]] the
    player controls is Destroyed, the player's own copy of it joins
    the player's [[Discard]]
30. **Refugees**: when a rival loses a [[Star]], its [[Damaged]]
    [[Colony|Colonies]] there may move into own free [[Slot]]s
31. **Tolls**: a rival [[Action]] passing its [[Star]] pays its
    controller 1 [[Energy]]
32. **Golden Age**: a [[Turn]] with no [[Combat]] ends with +1
    [[Draw]]
33. **Pilgrimage**: a rival [[Hero]] in its [[Star]] gives its
    controller 1 [[Energy]] each [[Turn]]
34. **Treaty**: both players can't [[Attack]] each other until the end
    of the next [[Turn]], both [[Draw]] 1
35. **Monument**: a [[Structure]] that can't be captured, only
    Destroyed
36. **Martyr**: an own [[Hero]] Destroyed in [[Combat]] gives
    [[Energy]] equal to its [[Level]]
37. **Prototype**: [[Research]] puts the [[Card]] into [[Hand]]
    instead of a [[Factory]] pile
38. **Reverse Engineering**: [[Research]] ignores [[Prerequisites]]
    for a [[Card]] the player defeated in [[Combat]]
39. **Probe**: look at a rival's [[Hand]]
40. **Warp Lens**: [[Explore]] reaches a [[Tile]] at any [[Distance]]
41. **Entanglement**: two own [[Card]]s in different [[Star]]s act as
    one [[Group]], no [[Range]] between them
42. **Tech Exchange**: swap a [[Laboratory]] [[Card]] with a rival's,
    both agree
43. **Eureka**: a failed [[Action]] gives 1 [[Energy]]
44. **Warp Overcharge**: doubles the [[Range]] of a [[Group]], its
    [[Card]]s are [[Damaged]] after the [[Action]]
45. **Precognition**: look at the top [[Card]]s of the [[World]] or
    [[Ancient]] deck, put them back in any order

# Review ideas

[[Ideas]] from the rule reviews, borrowed from other games, kept for
later.

1. **Policies** (TtA governments, Civilization policies): one Policy
   [[Card]] in [[Home]] giving a [[Plus]]: [[Hand]] size, [[Range]],
   [[Energy]]. Changed by an [[Action]]. `advanced`
2. **Energy as an extra action** (Voidfall trade tokens): spend 2
   [[Energy]] to let a [[Field]] [[Card]] act without a [[Starter]]
   from [[Hand]]. Alternative to [[Boost]]
3. **Center prize** (TI Mecatol Rex): [[Star]] 11 of the first
   [[Duel]] has a fixed [[World]] with the best [[Ability]], e.g.
   [[DeckBuilding|Draw]] 1
4. **Derelict row** (TtA [[Card]] row): [[Derelict]]s get cheaper the
   longer they wait
5. **Upkeep at Shuffle** (Voidfall, Eclipse), only if the leader
   snowballs in playtests: 1 [[Energy]] per [[Star]] beyond 3
6. **One Space per side**: halves first-[[Duel]] [[Tile]]s (24 → 12)
   and [[Explore]] [[Action]]s, at the price of a single route between
   two [[Star]]s
7. **Drones** (TI fighters): [[Range]] 0 [[Military]] [[Unit]]s
   carried by [[Ship]]s, if [[Capacity]] needs more use
8. **Asymmetry** for later [[Faction]]s: one [[Faction]] [[Card]] with
   a single rule change on top of a mirrored deck (TI [[Faction]]
   abilities), lighter than a deck of its own

# Early ideas

Ideas of the first design notes that neither the rules nor the
[[Backlog]] hold. Kept for later, nothing here is a rule.

## For open items

1. **Objectives** for the list of [[Objective]]s, Backlog I43 a, all
   checked on the current table:
	1. Every [[Slot]] of a [[Star]] filled, or of the biggest Star
	2. A Star whose cards all share one [[Class]]
	3. Every [[Colony]] of the [[Deck]] on the [[Field]]
	4. A [[Hero]] at the top [[Level]], or every Hero of the faction
	5. A Hero in a Star a rival controls
	6. An empty [[Laboratory]]: the whole deck researched
2. **Technology seeds**, one line each:
	1. Gain a token when building a card of the Class: an engine
	   trigger, like Terraforming Mars
	2. Pay power of one Class with another
	3. Count a Class one Level higher for [[Prerequisites]] and
	   [[Singularity]]
	4. Turn Culture power into [[VictoryPoints]]
	5. Peek at the next [[Event]]
	6. Build a Structure outside the allowed place
	7. Move the [[Homeworld]] to another own Star
3. **Combat brainstorm**, [[Combat]] is on hold:
	1. **Siege**: at the end of own Turn, if own Military power in a
	   Star exceeds the total [[Defense]] of all rival cards there,
	   they are captured without a Combat
	2. **Prisoner**: a Hero left in a Star without own [[Ship]] or
	   [[Base]] is stranded, a rival captures it as a trophy, its
	   faction can rescue it
	3. **Convoy**: a Ship carries captured rival cards home, to
	   [[History]] as trophies or to use their technologies:
	   "robbing caravans"
	4. **Assassination**: a strike at one Hero that ignores the Ships
	   and Bases around it
	5. **Pursuit**: [[Intercept]] a group that [[Retreat]]s or passes
	   by
	6. **Attack outgrows defense**: power grows with Level faster than
	   Defense, so late defenses break and the game ends
	7. **Humanism option**: a Colony is never Destroyed, a lost one
	   stays [[Damaged]] until its faction [[Recover]]s it

## Cards and factions

1. **Ally abilities**, like Star Realms: a card prints a bonus that
   works only with another own card of the named Class in the same
   Star. Every early card had one, [[Tactics]] is a single case
2. **Scrap abilities**, like Star Realms: a card prints its own
   one-time effect for when it is Destroyed, stronger than its
   [[Ability]], beside [[Exhaust]] and [[Recycle]]
3. **Hero experience**: Heroes grow by deeds, not only by power: a
   Hero that fought in a won Combat [[Upgrade]]s cheaper
4. **Card names** by Class pairs, beside "Base 4C":
	1. Bases: Training camp and Military academy for Military and
	   Culture, Military research base, Military shipyard, Civil
	   shipyard, Scientific shipyard
	2. Ship: Military hospital
	3. Colonies: Scientific, Production and Military metropolis
5. **Life forms**: four asymmetric [[Faction]]s, each strong in one
   Class and weak in the next:
	1. Plasmoids: Military up, Science down
	2. Artificial intelligence: Science up, no Culture, no Colonies
	3. Silicon giants: Culture up, Production down
	4. Carbon humans: Production up, Military down
	5. Strength: 4 more Ships of the strong Class. Weakness: no
	   Colonies of the weak Class, 4 Ships of it instead
	6. Incomplete: a Biotech faction without Production grows
	   everything, like the Blobs of Star Realms

## Field

1. **Space draw**: Space tiles aren't laid out at setup, [[Explore]]
   draws two and places one: a choice instead of luck, and a faster
   setup on paper
2. **Growing Field**: a new Star joins the [[Field]] where a faction
   brings 6 power of every Class into an empty place
3. **Field draft**: each player builds own side of the Field from
   Star and Space tiles
4. **Layouts** for more players, from the first Field sketch:
	1. 3 players: a triangle of 10 = 4+3+2+1 or 15 = 5+4+3+2+1 Stars,
	   a home corner each
	2. 4-6 players: home zones of 2-3 Stars around a shared middle

## Setup and modes

1. **Draft**: a player offers 3 starting cards, the rival discards
   one
2. **Even quick start**: deal equal cards, each places them, equalize
   the sum of Levels, then each passes the rival 1-3 cards
3. **Solitaire**: no rival, [[Ancient]]s fill every Star but two home
   Stars; conquer them all in the fewest Turns or the shortest time
4. **Puzzles**: a position in [[Notation]], find the win or the best
   move, like chess problems
5. **Deterministic option**: the deck is shuffled once at the start,
   [[Discard]] goes under the [[Reserve]] in order: every move can be
   calculated, like in Chess
6. **Open option**: all cards face up, [[Hand]] included
7. **Learning game**: the [[Duel]] without technologies, only
   [[BasicAction]]s
8. **Coexistence**: find rules where a Military victory is
   impossible and factions can only coexist, a [[Scenario]] for
   [[Politics]]
9. **Epic Heroes**: a campaign to raise Heroes to the top Level and
   complete a mission
10. **Handicap**: a computed handicap for players of different
    strength, e.g. Hand size +1 for the weaker one, at the start or
    for the whole game, with a rating like in Chess

## Galaxy

1. **Ages**: the [[GalaxyDeck]] comes in Ages, like Through the Ages.
   First Age events are windfalls for every faction, threats come
   later:
	1. 2 [[Goods]] or a free Ship, Base or Colony
	2. A free [[Research]] of one card
	3. A Warp jump: a Ship moves to a near Star
	4. Reveal or move one Ancient
2. **Cataclysm** event: every Ship on the Field goes to Discard

## Digital

1. **Own setting**: each player sees own art set, the rules stay
2. **Replay**: an animated replay and the full log, a story of the
   game written from the log, undo against the AI
3. **Statistics**: total and average Military power, population,
   Stars, Energy, research depth
4. **Arena**: online play with a chess-like rating, team games
5. **Custom cards**: players send a card, its Level is computed by
   the balance formula, then it is released
6. **Photo**: cards are recognized by their top strip, a photo of the
   table gives the position, statistics and art

## Settings

1. **Reskin**: a setting names the 4 Classes and the 5 concepts:
   Hero, Ship, Base, Star, Colony; the rules stay:
	1. Fantasy: attack, building, magic and trade; troops,
	   fortresses, kingdoms and cities
	2. Dune: spice is Science. Star Wars: politics instead of
	   colonization
	3. Family, no war: fun, work, study and money; an attack is a
	   chat, a joke or a lullaby
	4. A cheerful farmer against the weeds
2. **Name**: a title that fits any setting

## Table

1. **Pictograms**: every effect printed as text and as a pictogram,
   ideally shown in the art too
2. **Class shapes** that read without color: Military triangle,
   Culture circle, Science star, Production square; or crosshair,
   globe, atom and gear
3. **Top strip**: Level and Class on the top strip of a card, so
   fanned piles stay readable
4. **Hero standees**: Heroes stand up among the flat cards

## Principles

1. For [[GameBalance]]:
	1. Minimize frustration, the first game matters
	2. Momentum from the first Turn, short-term goals
	3. Inertia: players feel the end approaching
	4. Risk triangle: a small risk gives a small reward, a big risk a
	   big one
	5. No simple winning strategy, rich tactics: every component is
	   worth using in some position
	6. A card's Level is its total value: a program can check the
	   balance
2. **Only cards**: no tokens, nothing stored between Turns, all that
   plays is visible; [[Energy]] and Goods break it, see Backlog
   S64
3. **Questions the game answers**: what matters more, military or
   science, expansion or economy, people or machines, growth or
   aggression; which faction is stronger and why

# Overtone

Variant 3 of 3, which reinvents the premise. The universe is
resonant. Travel, building and leadership are all kinds of sound, and
the rules are the physics of harmony. See [[LoreVariants]] for the
comparison.

Story: [[TheHeldChord]], a great battle at the end of the game.

## Name

**OVERTONE**. The [[Deck]] already sounds like the overtone series.
Levels 1 to 8 are its first eight harmonics. The two Ends of every
card are neighboring harmonics: 2/1, 4/3, 6/5 and 8/7 are the
octave, the fourth, the minor third and the septimal tone. The 1/0
starting cards are single notes. The name also keeps a trace of the
8-bit heritage, since chiptune is music built from a few pure tones.

## Pitch

Everything that is made sings: ships, colonies, leaders. Every song
is heard by everyone. Two young peoples, the Reed and the Bell, have
learned the same scale under different suns, because 2:1 is 2:1
everywhere. Between them and the wild stars hangs the Drone, a chord
older than anyone, which drowns out new voices.

## Tone

Mythic and lyrical. Later singers tell the stories to children. The
canon is a Hymnal of short verses that state the laws, and the
characters sing them the way sailors quote proverbs. Under the myth
the physics stays exact. Resonance is the hard SF here.

## Premise

1. **Resonance**: matter and travel answer to pitch. Only the first
   few harmonics are stable; above them, sound breaks into noise.
2. **Intervals**: every made thing sounds an interval, its high note
   (the strong End) and its low note (the weak End). A thing turned
   over sounds its other note. The least things sound a single note.
3. **Four voices**: Low (Military, red), Hearth (Culture, green),
   Forge (Production, yellow), High (Science, blue).
4. **Voices and instruments**: the living are voices (Social) and the
   made are instruments (Industrial). A Great Organ is both, a choir
   and a pipe.
5. **Song carries between stars**: every song is heard everywhere.
   The great hulls are huge, and their songs bend space-time, so a
   star can hold only a few of them before space tears into noise.
6. **The Breath**: a people holds a few voices in its breath, ready
   to sing out in any star that hears its song.
7. **The Drone** is the Ancients: an old sustained chord in the wild
   stars. Its voices are unheard until they rise, and when silenced
   they sink back into the Drone and rise elsewhere.

## Laws

Each law answers a required item of [[LoreRequirements]] by its
number. Facts that follow from it get a phrase in the same law. The
rule page stays the source of truth.
The Hymnal numbers its verses in its own way.

| # | Law |
|---|---|
| 1 | Great hulls are huge, and their songs bend space-time: a star rings with only a few songs before space tears into noise. A thing that has sung gives way, so a voice goes to rest and a hearth or instrument falls to its low note. Going to rest is a song too, and no song is free. A lower sound masks the overtone of the one above it. |
| 2 | The dark between the stars carries song but gives no rest. A song that leaves a star must end in a star, and every star it passes through takes one beat. |
| 3 | Every song is heard by everyone. Only the Hush can silence a song that stands in a star, and a hushed song is thin: it carries one step and one load. |
| 4 | Every lane echoes. From home you hear its length; its nature you learn by singing into it. |
| 5 | Voices in unison carry farther than any one voice. A voice carries as far as its harmonic, and a standing sound doesn't carry at all. War voices catch a passing song, but an echo sent to help is not caught. |
| 6 | A star sings the song of whoever sings in it, not of whoever built there. Where two songs are equal, the star is in discord: both songs begin there, and neither takes the other's instruments. |
| 7 | In a star that sings another people's song, the standing instruments and the silenced voices sound for that people, until a silenced voice is healed and sings its own song again. |
| 8 | The living are voices and the made are instruments. Voices are raised in Choir Schools and instruments are made in Workshops; a Great Organ is both. No voice is born above the first note. What is broken returns to the Scores. When songs are equal, the voices are counted, not the instruments. |
| 9 | A people with no living cantor falls silent. A cantor is a living voice tuned by the warp, as loud as a great horn. The silenced, the unborn and the unread don't count as alive. |
| 10 | A people is not its homeworld. It falls silent only when no star hears its song. |
| 11 | The Drone is an old chord that hums in the wild stars. Its voices rise against a claimant, more of them for a better world. A silenced drone voice sinks back into the Drone and rises elsewhere. A people that takes a wild star finds the world in tune with what it brought. |
| 12 | A song can end in four ways: Silencing, the Diaspora, the Seven Organs and the Full Chord. The Full Chord counts only held notes, because a struck note falls. Each of the last three begins the Coda, one last verse for each other people. |

## Human and Alien traits

Optional facts of today's Deck. In Overtone they are the scale the
Reed and the Bell happen to share:

1. **The same scale**: 2:1 is 2:1 under every sun, so both peoples
   sing the same intervals.
2. **The Deck's End pairs as intervals**: 2/1, 4/3, 6/5 and 8/7 are
   the octave, the fourth, the minor third and the septimal tone.
3. **Harmonics 1 to 8**.
4. **Five voices in breath**, and three tunings a verse.
5. **Rattles**: the Tools.

## Canon

1. **The Hymnal**: the laws as short verses, sung to children.
2. **The Tuner's Book**: hearths and instruments, the low note, the
   Tuning, growth and rebuilding.
3. **The Cantor's Rule**: how a song is begun, who joins it, how far
   it carries.
4. **The Ledger of Scores**: every interval a people can sing, the
   [[Deck]].
5. **The Lament of the Drone**: what the old peoples remember of the
   chord in the wild stars.

## Story seeds

1. **The First Note**: the first Reed child raised at the
   fundamental, and why no voice is born higher.
2. **The Captive Cantor**: a silenced cantor sounds for the enemy
   star around her, until a healer's song reaches her from the next
   star.
3. **The Unsung Breath**: a people who held back a voice for defense
   watches it sink into the Rest unused.
4. **Seven Organs**: a Forge people builds its seventh Great Organ in
   a single verse.
5. **The Drone Answers**: the first time anyone silences the Drone in
   a wild star and hears it rise again two stars away.

## Strains

1. **Built on a deck pattern**: the intervals come from today's Deck,
   which is optional. The resonance itself is general, since any
   Level is a harmonic and any two Ends are two harmonics. An
   asymmetric faction with other Levels keeps the physics and loses
   only the neat pairs. The same scale for both peoples also reads as
   a fixed set of designs, while a people builds what it wants, see
   [[LoreRequirements]].
2. **Fantasy drift**: song as physics can read as magic. It has to
   stay resonance, with numbers, to remain science fiction.
3. **Soft battles**: a contest of songs can sound gentle, so the
   stories have to make silencing as heavy as death.
